composability structure (lattice-to-continuum limits). Per AGENTS.md Law 1,
report claims cite a local path plus a line locator into an extraction or
source file. Topic scope: strict operator-algebraic scaling maps, soft
inductive systems, the Jones/Thompson-group forest-category composability

endpoints (`https://arxiv.org/e-print/<id>`, `https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>`)

  - `:245`--`:277` -- **the category of binary forests** `\cF`: objects are
    the natural numbers, morphisms `\cF(n,m)` are binary forests with `n` roots
    and `m` leaves, composition is vertical stacking (glue leaves of `f` to
    roots of `g`).
  - `:316`--`:326` -- tensor (monoidal) structure: `n\otimes m := n+m`, tensor
    of forests is horizontal concatenation; `\cF` is **singly generated by the
    caret `Y`** (tree with 2 leaves), any morphism is a composition of
    `f_{j,n} = I^{\otimes j-1}\otimes Y\otimes I^{n-j}`.
  - `:380`--`:393` -- trees `\fT` as a directed set `(\fT,\leq)` (`s\leq t` iff
    `t=fs`); the **group of fractions** `G_\cF = \Frac(\cF,1)` is Thompson's
    group `F`.
  - `:410`--`:480` -- symmetric forests `\cSF`, `\cSF(n,m)=\cF(n,m)\times S_m`;
    Thompson's group `V` is the fraction group of `\cSF`; affine subcategory
    `\cAF` with `\Z/m\Z` giving `T`.
  - `:495`--`:511` -- **Jones' action from a functor**: for `\Psi:\cC\to\cD`,
    an action of the fraction group `G_\cC`; the covariant functor
    `\Phi:\cF\to\cD` gives `X_\Phi = \varinjlim_{t\in\fT} X_t`, the inductive
    limit over `(\fT,\leq)` with connecting maps
    `\iota_t^{ft}:(t,\xi)\mapsto(ft,\Phi(f)\xi)`; the group action
    `\alpha_\Phi` is the "Jones' action".
  - `:522`--`:529` -- **tensor functor is one datum**: a tensor functor
    `\Phi:\cF\to\cD` is completely characterized by the single morphism
    `R:=\Phi(Y)\in\Hom_\cD(\Phi(1),\Phi(1)\otimes\Phi(1))`; conversely any
    object `A` with `R:A\to A\otimes A` defines a tensor functor. (This is the
    exact abstraction of the repo's 1->2 vacuum-insertion seed `V_L`.)
  - `:532`--`:541` -- extension of the `F`-action to `V` via the symmetric-group
    action `\theta` on tensor factors; target category `\Hilb` (Hilbert spaces
    with **isometries**) yields a unitary representation, `\Calg`
    (C*-algebras with **injective *-morphisms**, `:498`) an algebra action.
- **Why acquired:** the load-bearing composability formalism. Casts refinement
  as a functor `\Phi` out of the category of forests, so the whole refinement
  tower is one seed `R=\Phi(Y)`, composition is automatic from functoriality,
  and the Thompson group appears as the fraction-group symmetry. Directly
  models the repo's `\theta_L(T)=V_L T V_L^\dagger` refinement and its
  composability question; also the source cited by OARWavelets (ref [12]) and
  the MERA/Bratteli-standard-form remark (`:178`).

- **Verified anchor ranges (into `source/FC-arxiv2.tex`):**
  - `:188`--`:209` -- **calculus of fractions / Ore program**: a category
    `\cC` admits a cancellative calculus of fractions iff it is
    left-cancellative and satisfies **Ore's property** (`t,s` with same target
    => exist `f,g` with `tf=sg`); this yields the fraction groupoid
    `\Frac(\cC)` and fraction groups `\Frac(\cC,e)`. Any isometry `H\to
    H\otimes H` gives a unitary representation of `F` extending to `V` (`:192`).
  - `:241`--`:254` -- **coloured forests and skein presentation**: fix a set
    `S` of colours, colour each interior (n-valent) vertex; a **skein relation**
    is a pair `(u,u')` of coloured trees with the same number of leaves; a
    forest-skein category `\cF` is given by a **skein presentation** `(S,R)`.
  - `:247`--`:248` -- monochromatic case (`|S|=1`) recovers Thompson `F`;
    **with three or more colours the category never satisfies Ore's property**,
    so admits no calculus of fractions and produces no fraction group. (Key
    obstruction for multi-colour / mixed-radix refinements.)
  - `:281`--`:346` -- **non-binary / mixed-radix examples**: Stein carets of
    various degrees; `F_{3,1}` from ternary (monochromatic) trees is isomorphic
    to a bicoloured binary forest-skein group (`:329`); Brin `dV` (one caret per
    dimension); Cleary irrational-slope group (`:338`).
  - `:503`--`:529` -- Dehornoy left-cancellativity criterion; a two-colour
    one-relation forest-skein category satisfying Ore is an Ore category (hence
    left-cancellative) and its `X`-groups (`X=F,T,V,BV`) are of type
    `F_\infty`; the class `\cF_\tau` built from a family
    `\tau=(\tau_a: a\in S)` of monochromatic trees with equal leaf-count.
- **Why acquired:** the general (coloured, n-ary) abstraction of the forest
  composability calculus -- the closest existing formalization of "general
  `k\to l` refinements, everything composable" that the brief targets. Pins the
  precise coherence data (skein relations, Ore property) and the exact
  obstruction that blocks naive multi-radix composition.

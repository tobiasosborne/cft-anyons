## Even-Majorana local-net identification

- `LongoMartinettiRehren2009/source/LMR.tex:1165–1168` explicitly identifies
  the even real free-Fermi subnet with the c=1/2 Virasoro net.
- `Bockenhauer1994/source.tex:324–339` presents local observable algebras by
  Majorana bilinears; `:350–356` warns that the universal global algebra
  is not simply the even CAR algebra of the whole circle.
- `Bockenhauer1994/source.tex:511–521,563–588` gives the NS vacuum and its
  even/odd decomposition; the compiler uses the represented even cyclic H0.
- `CarpiKawahigashiLongo2007` was acquired while locating this bridge.
  Its discussion of three free Fermi fields at `source.tex:1864–1872`
  concerns the SU(2)_2 Bose subnet, so it was not used to claim the
  single-Majorana equality. LMR supplies the direct equality instead.

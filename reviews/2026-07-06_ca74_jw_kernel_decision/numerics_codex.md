# CA-74 numerics report: Jones-Wenzl kernel decision

## Headline

The L=4 Fibonacci dilute evaluation kernel is generated by the dense-corner
Jones-Wenzl element: the evaluated Wenzl projector `rho(iota(p4))` has direct
sum operator norm `2.3291288938163937e-15 <= 1e-12`, while the formal word
expansion keeps coefficient `1.0` on the corner-unit diagram.  Together with
`rank rho_4 = 322` and `dim dTL_4 = 323`, this identifies
`ker rho_4 = span{iota(p4)}`.

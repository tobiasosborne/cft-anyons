### (g) What SYNTHESIS.md concludes about the state of the art

Important caveat: SYNTHESIS.md is NOT about the current pipeline. It is dated 2026-04-27 (line 20; 630 papers in DB), written for the archived "Fock space for indeterminate-N anyons" project, and predates the 2026-05-30 reboot. Its claims about "our project" (lines 214, 221-245) describe the legacy Lean/Julia project (TensorCategories.jl, machine-precision isometry validation under D4 wavelets); they are not checked in the new repo. Treat as a literature map, not as a result.

Conclusions it does draw (with anchors):
- Two lineages that "almost never cite each other": dense categorical chains (fixed N) and itinerant-anyon/FQH (SYNTHESIS.md:25-28).
- Closest prior art: Poilblanc et al 2011/12 for itinerant non-abelian anyons (:96-103), Garjani-Ardonne 2016 as the variable-N seed, "pair-creation direction is essentially unexplored" (:108-113), Hollands 2022 "the most rigorous categorical formalisation to date ... Limitation: dense chains only — no mobile anyons, no hopping, no vacancies" (:57-65), Stottmeister 2022 + Osborne-Stottmeister for OAR continuum-limit: "Dense only; no variable N" (:66-72).
- Gap diagram (:202-217): no paper has arbitrary FC + variable N + multiplicities + OBC + braid-in-H + isometric fine-graining + continuum limit; "Cells filled with ✗ indicate a gap that our project closes for the first time" (:216-217). Continuum-limit column: golden chain/Poilblanc "implicit (CFT)", Hollands "implicit (CFT)", Stottmeister "✓ (Ising worked)", our project "✓ (OAR)" (:204-214).
- Pipeline steps known in literature: finite categorical chain Hilbert space and defect/MPO algebra (Hollands, dense, any UMTC); OAR continuum limits for free (quasi-free) lattice systems and Ising via braiding RG (Stottmeister); rigorous KS for free fermions (not in SYNTHESIS but in CFTFromLatticeFermions.txt:221-232, which SYNTHESIS does not cover); golden/anyonic chains identified with CFTs numerically/Bethe ansatz (SYNTHESIS.md:33-47).
- Open per SYNTHESIS: variable-N with general UFC, multiplicities, OBC; continuum limit for any non-free anyonic model; it recommends citing Poilblanc, GA, Hollands, Stottmeister, Shi-Zhang-Senthil, Nakajima, and validating sVec -> free fermions (:249-272).
The post-reboot repo's own, independently sourced assessment is in CA-62 (report/sections/62_categorical_bu_pipeline_vision.tex:113-177): stages 1-2 "mixed checked/proposal", stage 3 "proposal", stage 4 "sourced scaffold plus proposal", stage 5 "precedent plus question" (precedent = OAR + free-fermion KS + Stottmeister braiding RG + Zini-Wang template; fidelity DHR/defect content ≃ C? left open, citing Hollands). Scoping verdict: "only free-fermion KS convergence is proved"; "Confirmed absent from the literature: dilute KS, variable-N symmetry generators, categorical-symmetry -> generator recipe" (wl 012:207-214); "survey found no lattice-Virasoro construction for any dilute loop model" (wl 011:8-9). Rigorous category->CFT for general categories exists only conditionally: boundary/sewing sources all assume an existing CFT or net (categorical-reconstruction/SOURCES.md:10-30,62-109).

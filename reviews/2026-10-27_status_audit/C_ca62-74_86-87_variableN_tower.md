## 1. The construction for a general unitary fusion category C

**Input.** A pair (C,O): C is a unitary fusion category and O = 1 ⊕ X is a declared site object with an
isometric vacancy inclusion ι_1. Hard-core occupancy comes from the object itself (CONVENTIONS (r);
(am) widens this to pointed O whose X may contain unit summands).

**Anyonic word algebra (CA-65).**
- A_L = End_C(O^{⊗L}), left-bracketed, f* = f†; A_L ≅ ⊕_c M_{m_c(L)}, m_c(L) = dim Hom(X_c, O^{⊗L}).
  The Bratteli diagram is the fusion graph of O; placed matrix units are E(w',f,w) = w' f w†.
- A free tensor pre-layer BU_0 = T(⊕_j Hom(X_a,O)_j) is proposed, with a *kinematic* ideal J_fus = ker π.
  Fusion is never deferred to the state.

**Variable-N carrier (CA-66).**
- H_L = ⊕_c Hom(X_c, O^{⊗L}) ≅ ⊕_{N,|S|=N,c} Hom(X_c, X^{⊗S}), acted on by postcomposition.
- Raw-cup birth/death maps satisfy b†b = d_a P_11.

**Refinement (CA-68, CA-71).**
- An order-preserving injection φ:[k]→[l] gives an isometry V_φ that puts the vacuum at the slots outside
  im φ. V_φ is isometric, charge-preserving and strictly local.
- Composition is exact, V_ψ V_φ = V_{ψφ}; θ_φ = Ad V_φ is a *-isomorphism onto the corner P_φ A_l P_φ,
  never unital.

**GNS corner calculus (CA-72).**
- Every placement descends to an isometric GNS intertwiner J. Its vacuum defect is exactly
  ‖JΩ−Ω‖² = 2(1−√ω(P_φ)).
- The UCP completion Φ_χ = θ + χ(·)(1−P) has the exact defect cov_χ(S,T)(1−P).

**Categorical residual set (CA-73).**
- The qubit coboundary, boost and Witt/Virasoro residuals are ported verbatim to A_L (open chain, two
  central constants). They transport exactly through θ_φ via "stretched" densities, and a "coherent"
  residual family makes them descend by construction.

**Dilute TL and the dilute Koo–Saleur ansatz (CA-69, CA-70).**
- For self-dual simple X, there is a proposed planar evaluation ρ_L : dTL_L(d_X) → A_L.
- Dilute diagrams preserve the parity of the occupation number N, so ρ_L misses every parity-odd morphism.
  The fusion vertex is one example when N_XX^X ≠ 0.
- CA-70 transcribes the dense Koo–Saleur (KS) modes onto a *periodic* Tier-1 dilute density. The terms are
  E_n, the momentum P_n from i[h_j,h_{j+1}], L_n = ½(E_n + P_n/v) + c/24.

**JW kernel decision (CA-74, Fibonacci only).**
- ρ_4 maps onto the parity-preserving subalgebra (dimension 322), and ker ρ_4 = span{ι(p_4)}, the first
  negligible Jones–Wenzl projector in the dense corner. For L ≥ 5 the kernel is conjecturally the ideal
  generated by the corner Jones–Wenzl embeddings.

**Spacetime dictionary (CA-86).**
- For any C, the brick-wall Trotter product of H = −Σh_j, with h_j expanded in morphism tiles, is, to
  first order per plaquette, a sum over spacetime string-net histories.
- Each amplitude is Ev_C(Γ) times Π_p w(p). Ev_C(Γ) obeys Levin–Wen rules (2)–(4) automatically; the tile
  weights break only rule (1), isotopy.
- On an integrable line, the Hamiltonian, the isotropic point and the honeycomb point form one commuting
  family. The integrable line is calibrated for TL-type d = 2cos(π/(L+1)).

**What CA-87 proved.**
- Lemma 87.1 (general): if 1 ⊕ X ≅ Y ⊗ Ȳ, the dilute chain *is* the dense Y-chain with its sites paired.
- For Fibonacci (1+τ = τ⊗τ): the "vertex tile outside dTL" is a TL generator across a pair boundary,
  H = −Σe_i on L dilute sites is the golden chain on 2L sites, and there is no net gas beyond loops.
- The real net case is moved to non-product sites. The example given is Haagerup, 1 ⊕ ρ.

## 3. The settled conjectures

**What CA-69 conjectured (S/69:121-128).** For Fibonacci, ρ_L is an isomorphism onto the
parity-preserving subalgebra for small L, with a kernel appearing only from L=4.

**What commit 9e19d0a settled, and only at L=4 (Theorem 74.1, S/74:135-169).**
- Surjectivity: image of ρ_4 has dimension 322 = P(4). Kernel: exactly span{ι(p_4)}, the first negligible
  Jones–Wenzl projector in the fully occupied corner TL_4(φ). L ≤ 3: ranks M_{2L} = 9, 51, so ρ_2, ρ_3 are
  injective (numerically).

**Layer.** Open chain; L0 (Fibonacci fusion and F data, plus κ_τ = +1 and positive dimensions); finite.
It reaches no rung. CA-88 files it under the tower's R0^c row.

**Fibonacci only.** It depends on d = φ = 2cos(π/5), the ℓ = 5 Jones quotient (dim Q_4(5) = 13 = dim End(τ^{⊗4})),
the Fibonacci path counts and a numerical rank. No general-C statement is made. The obvious lift is: for TL-type X with d_X = 2cos(π/ℓ), the kernel
contains the corner p_{ℓ−1} embeddings. That lift is unstated.

CA-87 then recasts the whole question for Fibonacci. dTL_2(φ) is just the parity-preserving subalgebra of
End(τ^{⊗4}), the 13-dimensional Fibonacci quotient of TL_4. The "outside-dTL" vertex is a TL generator of
the blocked chain (S/87:83-89).

**Caveats on rigour.**
- The rank 322 is the dimension of the algebra generated by Float64 images of the Tier-1 generators: a
  Gram–Schmidt with tolerance 1e-8 that is robust from 1e-6 to 1e-10 (worklog/013:142-145;
  `src/CategoricalResiduals.jl:192-253`). It is not an exact rank of the map on the diagram basis.
- Equating it with rank ρ_4 assumes ρ_4 is a well-defined homomorphism. Only an anchored subset of the dTL
  relations is tested (T:1688ff).
- The kernel identity itself is analytic: ILZ radical plus a positivity / quantum-trace argument.

**The CA-74 decision (S/74:221-236; reviews/2026-07-06_ca74_jw_kernel_decision/ORCHESTRATION.md:45-75).**
- (i) The kernel is decided analytically and only surjectivity is left numerical.
- (ii) The Gram-rank "JW ↔ null-vector" dictionary (W2.3) is deferred. Gram data need a canonical
  fusion-tree orthonormal basis in each fixed-S sector, and the endpoint-closing ledger (W1.5) never
  supplied one. CA-74 is rank-only and basis-independent, which is why it could proceed.
- (iii) Any check at L ≥ 5 must be combinatorial, because the 2188-dimensional rank computation is over the
  laptop budget.
- (iv) The ILZ extraction line :666 is treated as an extraction artifact.

## 7. Honest assessment

For general unitary fusion C with O = 1 ⊕ X, the block itself sits at **R0^c on an open chain at L0**: local,
isometric, charge-preserving refinements that compose exactly, with an exact finite GNS and corner calculus.
CA-65's trailing inclusions give an R0^u tower that is unital but is not a refinement. Downstream, CA-90 and
CA-96 lift the dyadic insertion to R0^u (local and unital), needing a relative half-braiding (L0+H) or a
graded structure. The block reaches no R1: no limit state is selected. It reaches no R2: the dilute KS modes
are a periodic proposal with undefined g_L, and there are no categorical Ward or convergence estimates.
R3–R5 are untouched.

The strongest general statement proved here is Theorem 72.3 combined with CA-71's composition theorem.
- Every placement refinement descends to an isometric GNS intertwiner, whose vacuum defect is exactly
  2(1−√ω(P_φ)).
- Composition is exact and functorial over the placement category.
Lemma 87.1 (blocking) is the strongest structural lesson. Its message: for product sites, "dilute" is the
dense chain in disguise, so the Fibonacci work in CA-69 and CA-74 calibrates TL, not new physics.

The main obstruction is pointedness and state selection. P_φ pins **every** inserted site. Trace states then
give ω(P) = d_O^{k−l} → 0 (Proposition 77.7(3)), and Σq_n < ∞ is a global, exact-RG-strength demand
(CA-76 Caution). The block has no dynamics-compatible dressing U, no defined g_L flow, and no general-C
critical Hamiltonian. Its only certified critical points are TL-type calibrations, and every Julia check is a
Fibonacci instance.

The single next step: prove a summable vacuum residual ε_n/Δ_{n+1} (Theorem 76.6) for one explicit dressed
family W_n = U_n V_n, built from C data via the CA-90 or CA-96 lifts, on a TL-type blocked dense chain
(Lemma 87.1 / Theorem 77.5). That would give the tower its first R1 point with a known target, stated for
every TL-type C, not only Fibonacci.

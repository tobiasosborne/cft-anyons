### Why these choices

The first completed domain should actually consume categorical data and
produce a lattice recipe with a sourced local-net identification. Ising is
a positive calibration where all those interfaces can be made explicit.
Its proof uses existing continuum theorems rather than claiming finite
tests prove convergence. Matching modular data is the requested minimum;
it is not silently upgraded to equivalence of arbitrary categories.

The coset construction explores a different mechanism and reaches beyond
the Ising central charge. Its exact current-tail annihilation avoids an
unproved numerical tail fit. It still retains ambient fermion sectors, so
it remains a generator construction, not a new completed modular-input branch.
The orbifold route offers still broader continuum targets, but continuum
"lattice nets" are not microscopic lattice limits.

The code emits factorwise terms instead of exponentially large many-body
matrices. All work respects the existing Fibonacci L<=4 restriction; no new
Fibonacci computation occurred. Finite fermion matrices cap at dimension 64;
the separate exact sparse witness uses at most 96 occupation bits and only
96 intermediate states, with a hard 5000-state guard.

### Frictions, referee findings, and repairs

- Two Astra capacity errors required retries. The environment's total

  collaborators instead cross-refereed work they had not authored; nobody
  approved their own principal construction. Parent independently checked
  sources, proofs, code and the main suites.
- Source KS Eq. (197) removes spurious momentum wrap. The wrapped control
  cancels the central term; the implementation must not use periodic shifts
  and then hide the effect with an arbitrary Fermi-crossing filter.
- The first benchmark's generator square and compatible limiting sea were
  not nontrivial Wilson state rows. Referee feedback caused the actual finite
  Dirac-state extension. Its product-of-even-two-mode-block proof converts
  covariance error into a genuine dual-state-norm bound.
- The compiler uses the chiral sine Hamiltonian, whose compatible sea is
  actually its finite ground state; its Wilson rows are legitimately constant.
  It does not substitute that sea for the different Dirac ground state.
- Total fermion parity in a product retains odd-times-odd fields and enlarges
  the net. The compiler fixes parity independently in each factor. Conversely,
  retaining only the total stress would give too small a diagonal Virasoro
  subnet. It retains all factor stress families.
- A stress subnet inside a larger field theory was initially an insufficient
  fidelity bridge. Direct even-real-Fermi=Vir_{1/2} sourcing and a complete
  sampled-even-field local reconstruction resolve this for the Ising branch.
  Bockenhauer's universal-circle-algebra warning is kept explicit: the target
  is the NS vacuum represented interval net.
- Exact character T carries a phase outside Q(zeta16), so it is stored as
  an exact rational phase exponent plus twists. Referees also repaired a
  recipe-constructor bypass and the relabelled-product sector mapping.
- WT-2 now asks for a computable upper norm bound B; computable expectations

- The orbifold proposal now propagates its actual c_out=24k and checks any

- GKO's text extraction loses the 2 in 1/(2 beta). The original publisher

- Coset adjointness requires paired summands and a separate exact zero-mode

### Corrections to my briefs found by the reviewers

- **E2, antiunitary parity.** The brief's P ("site reflection composed with the
  dagger") is antiunitary; it maps T_n to T_n + O(|n|/N), preserves each
  chirality and constrains nothing (E2 REVIEW.md:179-187; CA-91 Remark 91.4).
  Part (a) is false for that P and true for a complex-linear P. The note's
  abstract also dropped the equal-ledger hypothesis (REVIEW.md:37-42).
- **E4, false A3 clause.** "A3 for any gate-intertwining refinement" is false
  when c* != 0 (R-N3', CA-89 Prop. 89.7); the brief's control
  K = 1 + (H - E_0)/v violates A2 (R-A2, Prop. 89.3). Only c* is a Ward output;
  c also records the declared zero-mode shift b.
- **E6, universality false.** No fixed density has a limit without tuning
  (94.1); the limit is not an extension of the minimal Laplacian (94.9); the
  correct scaling is hop eps^-2, contact eps^-1, fusion cross eps^-1/2, pair
  cross eps^0 (94.2). The brief's uniform buckets are inconsistent.
- **E3, pairing dependence.** Spatial RP is a property of (C, O, h, s), not of
  (C, O, h): the seam pairing has a gauge s ∘ W that can make non-vacuum blocks
  PSD, and witnesses change with s (an X-twist restores positivity of
  -Z ⊗ Z) (E3 REVIEW.md:87-107, :122-132). F ∘ dagger is antilinear and cannot
  be the crossing; the note's "refutation" of E3(a) was a misreading
  (REVIEW.md:67-81). The note's "L3 over L0" tag was wrong: s is a declaration.
- E5's review lowered the data layer from L2 + L4 to L0 + L4 / L1 + L4
  (REVIEW.md:196-203). E1 asked only for grading "up to a bounded shift"; the
  proof gives exact degree preservation (CA-90 Theorem 90.1(iii)).

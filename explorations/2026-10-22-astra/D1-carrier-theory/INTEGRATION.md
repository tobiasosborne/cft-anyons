## (d) Headline verdict

**Needs reformulation.** As stated, D1-C is precise, honestly labelled and
plausibly true. It is a real *analysis* target. As the headline of a programme aimed at
rigorous CFTs with category fidelity, it has three problems:
1. The category does nothing analytically. Only finite-dimensional fibres and two linear maps enter.
2. Its number-changing continuum exists only because of a tuned contact
   term `−2ε⁻²Σn_jn_{j+1}` hidden in `D_hop†D_hop`. With standard hard-core hopping the collisions are
   Dirichlet and the sectors decouple (REVIEW.md §2 R1, reviewer check).
3. It is a z=2 nonrelativistic limit that touches no Wilson, KS or fidelity layer.

A reformulation that matters for the integration would be: *on the D1-T6
carrier, construct coherent local unital refinements of a number-changing field
algebra, a categorical second quantisation, together with one state family satisfying
WT-1.* The alternative is to demote D1-C to a carrier-mechanism benchmark.

**First attackable lemma.** The discrete collision-trace lower bound for the two
lowest coupled sectors, N∈{0,1,2}, with general finite-dimensional b and v. Suppose
`K_εu_ε ⇀ ψ` with bounded discrete energy. Show
`liminf⟨u_ε,H_εu_ε⟩ ≥ q(ψ)` restricted to these sectors. Δ_1 and Δ_2 have no interior
multiple-collision corners, so this isolates the single-face trace estimate.

**Most uncertainty-reducing step.** Acquire and register a local source for Mosco
convergence of quadratic forms on varying Hilbert spaces. The reviewer has a
candidate from memory (Kuwae–Shioya) that must be verified before it is cited. Such a source would turn
NOTE.md:549–557 into a sourced theorem and pin down the exact form of the lower bound
needed. The cheapest computation is a laptop-scale comparison, with L≤40 and N≤2, of the low spectrum of
`H_ε` against a finite-element solution of the continuum sector-coupled Robin
problem for random b and v. It tests convergence before any proof effort. Separately,
the R3 lead (the unital tower is a Jones tower, and P_0 is a Jones projection under blocking) is
one exact finite check away from being a derived statement.

## (e) Hypotheses still to be synthesised from category data

1. The reaction data `b_α: X→X⊗X`, `v_β: 1→X⊗X` and the couplings `λ_α`, `κ_β`, `μ`. These are declared, not
   derived (NOTE.md:11–13).
2. Closedness of q on its maximal domain and a smooth finite-particle form core
   (NOTE.md:26–31). These are analytic inputs; the trace bounds suggest they can be removed (NOTE.md:544–547).
3. The discrete collision-trace lower bound (NOTE.md:518–524), which is the whole gap in D1-C.
4. The lattice contact discretisation. The Neumann-engineered `D_hop` is a regularisation
   choice that must be declared, and nothing in C selects it.
5. Coherent **unital** observable refinement maps on the carrier, as in WT-1 A1. D6 P1 gives
   an integer obstruction.
6. A selected physical state family with summable corner weights (`Σ√(1−p_n)<∞`, or
   D6 P2's bound) and WT-1 A3 drift bounds. Trace states are excluded (D1-T5b).
7. Braiding and ribbon marking of the tower whenever modular fidelity is the target (D1-T2,
   NOTE.md:181–182; Open problem 7).
8. The choice of site object, point η and splitting X, together with tensor generation. Tensor generation is checkable from
   fusion data; the choice itself is extra data (CONVENTIONS (u)).
9. Graph-norm KS mode estimates (VR-1 A1–A4) and conformal Ward data. D1 supplies none.

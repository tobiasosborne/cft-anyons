## (a) The theorem as established (LaTeX-ready)

```latex
\textbf{Theorem 90.1 [derived; E1 NOTE E1.1--E1.2, E1.5; REVIEW confirmed; tags (any; \(\mathsf L0\)+relative
half-braiding, in particular \(\mathsf L1\); \(\mathsf R0^{u}\))].}  \textsc{Assume} \(\Cc\) unitary fusion,
\((O,\eta)\) pointed as in (am), \(W_M=O^{\otimes M}\), \(\Alg_M=\operatorname{End}_\Cc(W_M)\), \(\Dc=\langle O\rangle\) the
full fusion subcategory generated by \(O\), and a unitary family \(t_Y:Y\otimes O\to O\otimes Y\) (\(Y\in\Dc\)) that is
natural, tensorial, unital and \emph{pointed}: \(t_Y(1_Y\otimes\eta)=\eta\otimes1_Y\).  (A unitary braiding gives
\(t_Y=c_{Y,O}\); no braiding of \(\Cc\) is needed.)  Let \(B_L\) interleave \(O^{\otimes L}\otimes O^{\otimes L}\) into
\(\mathrm{old}_1,\mathrm{new}_1,\dots,\mathrm{old}_L,\mathrm{new}_L\) by crossings \(t\), and
\(\alpha_L(a)=B_L(a\otimes1)B_L^\dagger\).  \textsc{Prove}: (i) \(\alpha_L\) is a unital injective \(*\)-map with
\(\alpha_L(a)V_L=V_La\) for the CA-68 dyadic insertion; (ii) \(\alpha_L(\Alg_{[j,k]})\subset\Alg_{[2j-1,2k-1]}\)
(interval-algebra membership; constant uniform in \(L\), \(n\), \(\Cc\)); (iii) \([N_{2L},\alpha_L(a)]=\alpha_L([N_L,a])\)
(occupation degree preserved exactly); (iv) the construction is natural under unitary equivalences carrying
\((O,\eta,t)\); (v) the dyadic composites satisfy WT-1 A1, and CA-76 Prop.~76.1(iv) gives
\(d_{k,n}\le p_ne_n+2(1-p_n)\).  \emph{Not proved:} WT-1 A3 for any non-trivial state family (the all-vacancy
product state is compatible with zero drift; alternating compatible families have drift \(2\) with \(p_n=1\)).
\textbf{Proposition 90.2 [derived; E1.6 + REVIEW R-1; (any; \(\mathsf L0\); \(\to\mathsf R0^u\) negative)].}  Pointed
relative half-braidings are exactly the solutions of a finite unitary system on the simples of \(\Dc\).  If
\(\Delta_{x,c}=\dim\Hom(c,O\otimes x)-\dim\Hom(c,x\otimes O)\neq0\) for a simple \(x\) of \(W_s\) and \(W_{M-s}\), then
\emph{no} unital \(*\)-map \(\Alg_M\to\Alg_{M+1}\) intertwining the one-gap insertion after site \(s\), fixing left
intervals and shifting right intervals, exists (e.g.\ \(\mathrm{Vec}_{S_3}\), \(O=\one\oplus g\oplus h\), \(gh\ne hg\)).
Dyadic lifts not of this one-gap form are not decided.
\textbf{Proposition 90.3 [derived; E1.7].}  With ancillas appended contiguously, a range-\(w\) depth-\(D\) circuit
implementing \(\alpha_L\) needs \(D(w-1)\ge L-1\); the nearest-neighbour shuffle attains \(L-1\).
```

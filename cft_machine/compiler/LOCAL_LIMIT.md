# Local even-observable reconstruction under sharp CAR refinement

This defines the local reconstruction used by the supported compiler.
It does not assert that sharp maps preserve lattice intervals, or identify
a maximal net formed from all conceivable scale-dependent bounded sequences.
It reconstructs the full NS vacuum even-Fermi interval net, rather than
selecting only a stress subnet from an unidentified larger observable theory.
Conventions: (w),(y),(z). Source abbreviations are those of `PROOF.md`.

## LL-1. Sampling gives local continuum fields

ASSUME h=L2([0,2pi],dx) with antiperiodic Fourier basis
 e_r(x)=exp(i*r*x)/sqrt(2pi), r half-integral. A proper interval I lies
inside a chart avoiding the antiperiodic seam. Let f be a real continuous
section with compact support inside I. Smooth sections suffice for the net.
At x_j=epsilon*j, epsilon=2pi/M, set v_M[j]=sqrt(epsilon)f(x_j).
Let U_M[j,r]=exp(i*r*x_j)/sqrt(M), and let F_M in h have the finite
Fourier coefficients U_M* v_M, extended by zero outside the momentum window.
PROVE F_M→f in h and the embedded CAR field Psi(F_M)→Psi(f) in norm.

<1>1. Every fixed Fourier coefficient of F_M tends to that of f.
  <2>1. It is epsilon/sqrt(2pi) times
  sum_j exp(-i*r*x_j)f(x_j), once the fixed r belongs to the window.
  <2>2. This is a Riemann sum for the Fourier coefficient integral.
  Continuity of f gives convergence.
  <2>3. QED BY <2>1–<2>2.
<1>2. The norms of F_M converge to the norm of f.
  <2>1. The discrete Fourier transform is unitary: distinct half-integer
  labels have integer nonzero differences, so their discrete scalar product
  is a finite geometric sum equal to zero.
  <2>2. Thus ||F_M||²=epsilon sum_j|f(x_j)|²→integral|f|².
  <2>3. QED BY <2>1–<2>2.
<1>3. F_M converges weakly to f.
  <2>1. Pairing against any finite Fourier polynomial converges BY <1>1.
  <2>2. Approximate an arbitrary h-vector by such polynomials, using their
  density and the uniform norm bound from <1>2 to bound both error pairings.
  <2>3. QED BY <2>1–<2>2.
<1>4. F_M converges strongly to f BY <1>2–<1>3 and
||F_M-f||²=||F_M||²+||f||²-2Re<F_M,f>.
<1>5. The CAR imply Psi(g)*Psi(g)<=||g||²1, hence ||Psi(g)||<=||g||.
Additivity and <1>4 imply the asserted operator-norm convergence.
<1>6. QED BY <1>4–<1>5.

`sample_local_field` implements the displayed coefficients. Its finite
support check checks the sampled values only. The function's continuity
and compact support are hypotheses of this proof, not properties certified
by finitely many evaluations of an arbitrary Julia Function.

The sample test uses f(x)=(1-(2(x-pi)/pi)²)² inside (pi/2,3pi/2), zero
outside, as a continuous finite arithmetic oracle. Its squared norm is
128pi/315: expand (1-t²)^4 and integrate its five monomials over [-1,1],
then multiply by pi/2. Exact rational grid values independently test the
sampling normalization, Fourier coefficient and Parseval identity at M=8,16.
The proof of convergence is LL-1, not those two numerical samples.

## LL-2. The reconstructed local algebra is the full even local CAR algebra

ASSUME the local limit algebra for I is defined to be the norm-closed unital
algebra generated by limits of sampled even field polynomials with real
smooth compactly supported test sections in I. Use the global NS even-vacuum
cyclic Hilbert space H0 for every interval, and take each represented weak
closure there; no interval-dependent Hilbert space is chosen.
PROVE this is the usual NS vacuum even real-Fermi local net at I.

<1>1. Products of two sampled fields converge to the corresponding product.
  <2>1. Add and subtract Psi(f)Psi(G_M) in the difference. Its norm is
  bounded by ||F_M-f|| ||G_M||+||f|| ||G_M-g||.
  <2>2. This tends to zero BY LL-1. Finite products follow inductively.
  <2>3. QED BY <2>1–<2>2.
<1>2. The limiting generators are precisely a norm-dense generating family
for the even local CAR algebra.
  <2>1. Every defined generator is an even polynomial in fields supported
  in I BY <1>1, giving one inclusion.
  <2>2. Smooth real compactly supported functions are dense in real L2(I).
  By CAR norm continuity, bilinears in those functions approximate arbitrary
  local bilinears. Complex sections are linear combinations of real ones.
  <2>3. Even CAR polynomials are products of such bilinears and the unit;
  BO:324–339 gives exactly this local-algebra presentation. Taking norm
  closures proves the reverse inclusion.
  <2>4. QED BY <2>1–<2>3.
<1>3. The compatible limiting covariance S=1_(r<0) is the NS vacuum state;
OS:2794–2811 and BO:511–521 give its annihilation characterization.
Restricting to the even algebra and taking its cyclic closure gives H0,
by the explicit NS decomposition BO:563–588.
<1>4. Taking the vacuum-represented weak closure of the equal algebras in
<1>2 yields the same interval von Neumann algebra. LMR:1165–1168 identifies
the resulting even real-Fermi net with Vir_1/2.
<1>5. QED BY <1>1–<1>4.

The proof initially treats intervals avoiding a chosen seam. Antiperiodic
sections in another chart carry the sign when continued across the old seam;
the limiting NS free-Fermi representation supplies the consistent circle net
and its conformal covariance. The present sampler implements one such chart,
not a routine for arbitrary overlapping chart transitions.
BO uses normalized angular measure dx/(2pi); multiplication by sqrt(2pi)
maps our h=L2(dx) test function to BO's normalization unitarily.
Their coordinate section conventions are not substituted for our AP basis.

## LL-3. Algebraic refinement, state restriction, and independent factors

ASSUME the k independent Majorana towers and states of IC-2, with the
local reconstruction of LL-2 and the independent parity group (Z2)^k.
PROVE the reconstructed observable net is the product of k Ising nets.

<1>1. Parity Psi→-Psi commutes with every CAR refinement and fixes the
quasi-free sea. For k factors do this separately in each factor.
<1>2. Finite-group averaging proves that the fixed-point subalgebra of the
strict inductive limit is the closure of the union of fixed-point stages
(the detailed proof is Lemma 2 in `research/realisation_and_fidelity.md`).
Therefore this restriction is performed on the lattice observable tower.
<1>3. The specified local reconstruction also restricts factorwise: apply
LL-1–LL-2 in each factor, and take products of its even bilinears.
The limits generate the tensor product of the even interval algebras.
<1>4. The invariant cyclic space is the tensor product of the even cyclic
spaces; averaging each factor parity projects precisely onto those spaces.
The odd-in-each-factor product Psi_1(f)Psi_2(g) is excluded, even though it
has even total parity. The code tests this distinction explicitly.
<1>5. QED BY <1>1–<1>4. This gives Vir_1/2 tensor powers, whose complete
DHR sector and modular identification is IC-1 in `PROOF.md`.

The global cutoff C*-algebra in one representation is not identified with
the universal global local-net algebra; BO:350–356 explicitly distinguishes
them. DHR sectors are recovered from the represented interval net by KL,
not by declaring the finite cutoff's matrix blocks to be those sectors.

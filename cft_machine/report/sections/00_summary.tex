\textbf{The objective.} Discover an algorithm, or functor, that takes fusion
or modular tensor category data to concrete lattice systems and microscopic
Virasoro generators, with an OAR limit through Wilson's triangle that is a
rigorous CFT with the starting modular data.

\textbf{What has been achieved.} An exact-input Ising compiler and declared
tensor-product branch; explicit lattice fermion and coset stress generators;
quantitative state, mode, product, resolvent and unitary convergence results;
a positive constrained finite-algebra OAR construction; reproducible exact
and numerical witnesses; and independent skeptical cross-reviews.

\textbf{What has not been achieved.} There is no general category-to-CFT
functor. The new coset descendant construction has not been identified with
a categorical fusion-space basis on which the Hamiltonian acts locally.
The tricritical modular-input compiler was proposed but not implemented.

\textbf{Locality, as clarified by Tobias.} Global admissibility constraints
may be fusion constraints built into the Hilbert space. They do not by
themselves make a model nonlocal. The continuing target is a local model of
fusion spaces, with those constraints incorporated automatically.

\section*{Executive synthesis}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive synthesis}

The strongest completed input-to-output branch begins with the standard
chiral Ising modular datum, or an explicit product of such data. Exact
cyclotomic arithmetic checks the input. The output contains actual Majorana
lattice Hamiltonians, ground states, parity-compatible CAR refinements and
corrected Koo--Saleur modes. A local reconstruction argument identifies the
NS even-Fermi interval net, and the sourced Ising-net sector theorem supplies
the complete modular data. This branch is a supported construction, not
evidence that arbitrary categorical input is realizable by the same code.

The main new alternative is the coset route. Finite flavored CAR currents
give explicit quartic Sugawara-difference stress polynomials. Their high-current
tail vanishes exactly on each fixed free-energy subspace; sufficiently large
microscopic windows reproduce each fixed mode and every fixed finite word.
Coordinated Fourier and current cutoffs then give smooth-stress core,
strong-resolvent and unitary convergence using a sourced energy bound.

There is also a decisive negative result: the raw finite quartic zero mode
is not positive. An explicit polarized state has expectation
$-(M-R)(M-R-1)/12$. Extracting finite vacuum-descendant spaces and adding
a positive complementary energy gives a different positive model. An
independent scalar algebra summand supplies genuine unital OAR maps and
compatible dynamics. These operations are constructive, but their current
presentation supplies no bounded-range fusion-space implementation.

The methodological results connect these branches. Summable Wilson-row drift
gives an effective state construction; graph-norm estimates control products
of unbounded generators; finite-group averaging transports invariant states
and represented algebras. Referees caught substantive mistakes and missing
hypotheses, including the difference between generator compatibility and
Wilson-state convergence, total versus factorwise parity, and the need to
distinguish a norm inductive limit from represented field limits.

The final direction is therefore specific: preserve the constructive OAR and
symmetry machinery, and find a categorical fusion-space description in which
the required Hamiltonians and refinement operations are local. Replacing
that task by arbitrary global projection would not complete the programme.

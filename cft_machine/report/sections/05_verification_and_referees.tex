\section{Implementation, computational evidence, and skeptical review}

\subsection{The executable record}

Every numerical headline in this report has a producing script and a run
bundle. Exact arithmetic is used for modular recognition, sparse CAR
calculations and Gram-coordinate OAR maps. Floating calculations are confined
to the declared finite fermion matrices and analytic-error witnesses, with
explicit tolerances. No full package test suite or large many-body
diagonalization was run.

The retained independent referee scripts add 13 modular phase/relabel
checks and 18 finite-boundary/zero-current checks. Those are distinguished
from repeated executions of the main suites. The earlier orientation also
reran the inherited CA-74 testset; it is not counted as new code coverage.

The fermion previews use one-particle matrices of dimension at most 64.
The sparse coset calculation uses up to 96 occupation bits, not a
$2^{96}$ allocation. The first coset suite reached 96 sparse intermediate
states; the polarized Hamiltonian checks reached 2744, under a hard 5000
state guard. Commands were capped at 60 seconds, with one Julia/BLAS thread.

\subsection{Actual outputs, rather than just acceptance flags}

The Ising producer emits four finite instances: one or two declared factors,
with 8 or 16 sites per factor. The run includes the exact modular matrices,
twists and character phase, sector-label transport, every quadratic mode's
terms and normal-order scalar, the spatial Hamiltonian, covariance and
refinement entries. It preserves the individual stress families as well as
their total sum.

The coset producer records exact rational descendant norms at $c=1/2$ and
$c=7/10$, with distinct flags for the general CR-2 and the smaller vacuum
CR-3 cutoff certificates. The Hamiltonian producer records the negative raw
expectations and extracted positive spaces. The scalar-OAR producer records
eight low-grade stages with exact Grams, positive complementary energies,
and verified algebra/state/dynamics identities.

Source hashes accompany all producers. They allow a future run to identify
which implementation generated the data, rather than relying on a worklog
claim or the appearance of a table.

All corruptions were restored before final verification. A useful late
referee finding was that the first scalar-character mutation did \emph{not}
fail: the chosen product pair accidentally made the vacuum functional
multiplicative on that pair. The sentinel was corrected to a pair with
$\omega(ab)\ne\omega(a)\omega(b)$, and the mutation then genuinely failed.
The report does not use the original green run as rejected-mutation evidence.

\subsection{Substantive referee corrections}

Three Astra collaborators performed research and cross-refereed work they
had not authored. The environment's four-thread limit prevented an additional
fresh referee thread, so roles were reassigned for independent cross-review.
The parent also inspected the current sources/code and reran the main checks.

The reviews made the following material changes to the scientific record:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Separated a generator-refinement square from Wilson rows of actual
microscopic states, and added the nontrivial Dirac-state construction.
\item Replaced an insufficient stress-subnet argument by the full even local
CAR reconstruction and direct even-Fermi/Ising source identity.
\item Required independent factor parities and retained each factor stress
family, avoiding both a larger observable extension and a smaller diagonal net.
\item Added the computable norm upper bound required by the effective
Wilson expectation algorithm.
\item Tracked the actual central charge of the holomorphic orbifold recipe;
modular matching alone does not satisfy an arbitrary prescribed lift.
\item Corrected finite coset adjointness at zero-current indices and sharpened
the support threshold without increasing compute budgets.
\item Distinguished the norm limit $K(\mathcal H)+\mathbb C1$ from its
represented local field limits, and the new constrained refinement from the
original unital CAR refinement.
\item Established the stronger bounded-local-WOT Ising upper inclusion,
while explicitly recording ultraviolet states excluded by the chosen RG.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Practical failures and what they taught}

Two transient Astra capacity errors were retried. A source extraction lost
the factor 2 in the GKO Sugawara coefficient; the original PDF was visually
checked. Author TeX was kept unchanged, including its whitespace and hashes.
Mutation logs were retained as text artifacts because ordinary LaTeX-style
\texttt{.log} files are ignored by the repository.

The first integrated descendant-matrix test timed out at the fixed 60-second
limit. It recomputed each sparse action once per matrix row. The corrected
code computes each necessary action once and uses the proved energy grading
to skip exactly vanishing columns; the operator is unchanged. The final
212-assertion integration suite passed within the original cap.

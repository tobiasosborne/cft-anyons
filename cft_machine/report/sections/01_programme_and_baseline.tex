\section{The programme, its input, and the inherited starting point}

\subsection{What a completed machine must produce}

The required output is more than a Hamiltonian with a plausible low-energy
spectrum. It must connect categorical input to a finite microscopic family,
its observable algebras, states and refinements, explicit symmetry generators,
a mathematically controlled OAR limit, and the modular data of the resulting
local CFT. The category-to-model construction may make choices, but those
choices must be visible. If it is called a functor, the objects, morphisms,
composition law and transported physical data must also be specified.

The working output tuple is
\[
 \bigl((A_N,H_N,\omega_N),\ \alpha_{N'}^N,\ L_{N,n},\
       (\mathcal A,\mathcal H,\Omega,U),\ \text{modular comparison}\bigr).
\]
Here the first entries are microscopic; the last two require analysis and
sector theory. The present code supplies this tuple on its declared Ising
domain. The later constrained coset construction supplies a different
finite-algebra OAR prescription with a local continuum reconstruction, while
its local fusion-space implementation remains open.

\subsection{Category, modular data, and additional conformal information}

A fusion category alone does not specify modular $S,T$ matrices. A braided
target, or a distinct defect/soliton target, must be declared. For modular
input the minimum fidelity requirement here is matching modular data under
a unit-preserving relabelling. A braided equivalence is a stronger assertion:
the registered Mignard--Schauenburg examples show that modular data do not
determine a modular category up to braided equivalence.

The twist diagonal $\Theta=\operatorname{diag}(\theta_a)$ and the character
matrix $T=e^{-2\pi i c/24}\Theta$ are kept distinct. A real central-charge
lift is not silently read off from topological data. Category data can admit
multiple conformal realizations; tensoring with a holomorphic net preserves
the representation category while changing conformal information. This does
not rule out an algorithm that makes an explicit choice of realization.

\subsection{Scope and proof status in this report}

\begin{description}
\item[Source theorem.] A result imported from a registered primary source,
with its hypotheses checked for the stated application.
\item[Local proof.] A derivation given in the repository using Lamport's
ASSUME/PROVE, hierarchical steps and explicit dependencies.
\item[Finite witness.] A reproducible computation asserting a known value
or invariant, with a producer, run bundle and regression checks.
\item[Proposal.] An unimplemented construction or a missing mathematical
interface. It is not promoted by a favorable finite test.
\end{description}

The independent referee reports assess particular claims under particular
hypotheses. They are evidence of scrutiny, not formal proof certificates.
Every distinction becomes important below: a strong operator limit need not
be a norm inductive limit; a finite stress difference need not be a positive
Hamiltonian; and a globally constrained space need not already be a
categorical fusion space with local operators.

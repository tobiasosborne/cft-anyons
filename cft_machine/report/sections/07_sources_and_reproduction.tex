\subsection{Primary mathematical sources}

All external mathematical claims use local source files. The corresponding
manifests retain bibliography, legal retrieval URLs, retrieval date, hashes
and extraction details. The following map identifies the sources carrying
the central claims in this report.

\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[OS: Osborne--Stottmeister.]
\emph{Conformal field theory from lattice fermions}, arXiv:2107.13834.
Local extraction: \path{references/text/CFTFromLatticeFermions.txt}.
Theorem A and the full mode-convergence theorem; Definitions 3.1 and 3.9;
normal-ordering/cocycle formulas; modified KS equations (197)--(199);
strong-resolvent, unitary and correlation limits. Exact ranges used appear
in the individual proof notes and report source comments.

\item[Wavelet OAR framework.]
arXiv:2010.11121, local file
\path{literature/md/2010.11121/2010.11121.md}.
Lines 101--111 and 126--180 define the strict observable maps, Wilson rows,
compatible limiting states and GNS representations. These definitions are
the baseline for deciding whether a proposed construction is actually OAR.

\item[Soft inductive-limit framework.]
arXiv:2306.16063, local file
\path{literature/md/2306.16063/2306.16063.md}.
Lines 185--208 and 568--593 provide the contractive-map and norm-null
definitions. The distinction from a strong-star-null quotient is explicit.

\item[GKO: Goddard--Kent--Olive.]
\emph{Unitary representations of the Virasoro and super-Virasoro algebras},
Communications in Mathematical Physics 103 (1986), 105--119,
DOI 10.1007/BF01464283. Original and extraction:
\path{references/cft-machine/virasoro/GoddardKentOlive1986/}.
Printed pages 107--108 were visually checked because the extraction loses
a coefficient factor. Equations (2.5)--(2.20) fix the current/Sugawara and
diagonal-coset normalization.

\item[CW: Carpi--Weiner.]
\emph{On the uniqueness of diffeomorphism symmetry in Conformal Field Theory}.
arXiv:\path{math/0407190}; Communications in Mathematical Physics 258 (2005),
203--221, DOI 10.1007/s00220-005-1335-4. Local source:
\path{references/cft-machine/coset-symmetry/CarpiWeiner2004/source.tex}.
Lines 721--788 give the mode-energy and Fourier bounds; 864--976 provide
essential self-adjointness and resolvent/unitary control.

\item[KL: Kawahigashi--Longo.]
\emph{Classification of Local Conformal Nets. Case $c<1$},
arXiv:math-ph/0201015. Local source:
\path{references/cft-machine/realisation/KawahigashiLongo2002/source.tex}.
Lines 403--407 give Haag duality; 647--669 give minimal fusion and weights;
1008--1024 identify the diagonal coset; 1027--1157 give complete rationality,
all sectors, twists and modular data.

\item[KLM: Kawahigashi--Longo--M\"uger.]
\emph{Multi-interval Subfactors and Modularity of Representations in
Conformal Field Theory}, arXiv:math/9903104. Local source:
\path{references/cft-machine/realisation/KawahigashiLongoMuger1999/source.tex}.
Lines 969--977 and 1518--1551 supply the hypotheses and completeness of
tensor-product sectors used by the product Ising branch.

\item[BO and LMR: the Ising observable bridge.]
B\"ocken-hauer, \emph{Localized Endomorphisms of the Chiral Ising Model},
arXiv:hep-th/9407079; Longo--Martinetti--Rehren,
\emph{Geometric modular action for disjoint intervals and boundary
conformal field theory}, arXiv:0912.1106. Sources under
\path{references/cft-machine/realisation/}.
BO lines 324--355 and 563--588 distinguish local even CAR, the NS vacuum
space and the universal-circle caveat. LMR lines 1165--1168 directly identify
the real-Fermi even subnet with $\Vir_{1/2}$.

\item[RSW: Rowell--Stong--Wang.]
\emph{On classification of modular tensor categories}, arXiv:0712.1377.
Local source:
\path{references/category-theory/RowellStongWang2009Classification/source/RSWfinal3.tex}.
Lines 689--690 give balancing; 2441--2474 give the exact Ising modular datum.

\item[Realization alternatives and fidelity counterexamples.]
Evans--Gannon, arXiv:1804.11145; Bischoff, arXiv:1803.04949;
Mignard--Schauenburg, arXiv:1708.02796. Local sources and exact anchors are
registered in \path{references/cft-machine/realisation/SOURCES.md}.
Their continuum constructions and counterexamples are not silently promoted
to microscopic lattice/OAR theorems.

\item[C*-algebras, weak closures, and proof presentation.]
Landsman, arXiv:math-ph/9807030, source under
\path{references/cft-machine/coset-observables/}; Fewster--Rejzner source
under \path{references/qft/FewsterRejzner2019/}; Lamport,
\emph{How to Write a Proof}, author source under
\path{references/cft-machine/proofs/}. These support the explicit algebraic
quotient/GNS statements and the structured proof notation.
\end{description}

\subsection{Reproduce the code and artifacts}

Run commands from the repository root. The test scripts use the current
Julia project and mostly only standard libraries. Keep one thread and the
explicit per-command cap; the complete historical \texttt{Pkg.test()} suite
remains suspended by the laptop policy.

\begin{verbatim}
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 JULIA_NUM_THREADS=1 timeout 60 \
  julia --project=. cft_machine/benchmarks/fermion/test.jl
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 JULIA_NUM_THREADS=1 timeout 60 \
  julia --project=. cft_machine/benchmarks/fermion/test_wilson.jl
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 JULIA_NUM_THREADS=1 timeout 60 \
  julia --project=. cft_machine/compiler/test.jl
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 JULIA_NUM_THREADS=1 timeout 60 \
  julia --project=. cft_machine/benchmarks/coset/test.jl
\end{verbatim}

The same prefix runs the test scripts under
\path{cft_machine/benchmarks/coset_hamiltonian/} and
\path{cft_machine/benchmarks/unitized_oar/}; the latter has both
\texttt{test.jl} and \texttt{test\_descendants.jl}. Each benchmark's
\texttt{run.jl} regenerates its own dated TOML bundle. The two independent
scripts are \path{cft_machine/reviews/check_compiler.jl} and
\path{cft_machine/reviews/check_coset.jl}.

Rebuild this synthesis with pdflatex twice for the contents and hyperlinks:
\begin{verbatim}
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error \
  -output-directory=cft_machine cft_machine/report.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error \
  -output-directory=cft_machine cft_machine/report.tex
\end{verbatim}
The project-wide lab book has its own mandatory commands:
\begin{verbatim}
make check-report-shards
make report
\end{verbatim}

The main scientific record remains the sources, proofs, implementations,
tests, manifests and run artifacts together. Reproducibility is not supplied
by the PDF alone; the PDF points to the complete record.

\subsection{The KS Prescription Is a Tile Prescription}

\textbf{Setting.}  \(O\in\Cc\) a site object, \(h_{N,j}\in\operatorname{End}_\Cc(O\otimes O)\)
self-adjoint, \(H^{\mathrm{raw}}_N=-\sum_j h_{N,j}\) (CONVENTIONS (ak)), a given velocity
\(v_N>0\), and the velocity-one density \(k_j:=-h_{N,j}/v_N\), which is the \(h\) of (af).
Declared extra data: a periodic representation on \(N\ge4\) sites with a unitary
translation \(U_N\), \(U_N^N=1\), \(U_Nk_jU_N^*=k_{j+1}\), and disjoint-support commutativity.
The forward current is \(\mathcal J_j:=i[k_j,k_{j+1}]\) (three-site support).
With \(z=e^{2\pi i/N}\), \(a_N=N/4\pi\), a scalar density subtraction \(\varepsilon_N\)
(the \(e_n\) of (af), renamed to avoid the cup-cap tile \(e_j\) of (ak)) and a
real zero-mode shift \(b_N\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:79-ks}
  T_{N,n}=a_N\sum_j z^{nj}(k_j-\varepsilon_N-\mathcal J_j)+b_N\delta_{n,0},\qquad
  \bar T_{N,n}=a_N\sum_j z^{-nj}(k_j-\varepsilon_N+\mathcal J_j)+b_N\delta_{n,0}.
\end{equation}
Expanding \(h_j=\sum_\alpha c_\alpha D^{(j)}_\alpha\) in a dagger-stable tile basis (CA-86)
gives the forward chiral tile
\(-\sum_\alpha\frac{c_\alpha}{v_N}D^{(j)}_\alpha-\varepsilon_N
-\frac{i}{v_N^2}\sum_{\alpha,\beta}c_\alpha c_\beta[D^{(j)}_\alpha,D^{(j+1)}_\beta]\):
both powers \(1/v_N\), \(1/v_N^2\) matter.  The categorical evaluation fixes
recoupling; the coefficients fix dynamics.  No isotropy, velocity or Ward identity
follows.

\textbf{Lemma 79.1 (D4-T) [derived].}  \(i[\sum_lk_l,k_j]=\mathcal J_{j-1}-\mathcal J_j\),
\(T_{N,n}^*=T_{N,-n}\), and
\([\sum_jz^{mj}k_j,\sum_jz^{nj}k_j]=-i(z^n-z^m)\sum_jz^{(m+n)j}\mathcal J_j\).
\textbf{Proof.}  Only neighbouring densities fail to commute (interchange law); the
neighbouring pair carries coefficient \(z^{mj}z^{n(j+1)}-z^{m(j+1)}z^{nj}=z^{(m+n)j}(z^n-z^m)\);
\(k_j,\mathcal J_j\) are self-adjoint.  \(\square\)  If \(\varepsilon_N=E_0(N)/(Nv_N)\) then
\(T_{N,0}+\bar T_{N,0}=\frac{N}{2\pi v_N}(H^{\mathrm{raw}}_N-E_0(N))+2b_N\); replacing
\(T_n\mapsto T_n+d\,\delta_{n,0}\) maps \(c\mapsto c+24d\) and fixes \(c^*\) [derived], so
a wrong cubic anomaly is never repaired by an energy origin (cf.\ GS:3224--3229).  \emph{Amendment (2026-10-22):} with
\(K=1+(H^{\mathrm{raw}}_N-E_0)/v_N\) this identity makes A2 fail at \(n=0\) whenever the spectral width of \(K-1\) stays bounded
below (CA-89, Proposition 89.3); this is one reason the regularity control of Theorem 79.2 is an auxiliary \(Q^p\).

\subsection{A Spectral-Shell Certificate for VR-1}

\textbf{Setting.}  A tower of finite Hilbert spaces \(H_r\) (\(N=N_r\to\infty\)), isometries
\(J_r\) (the GNS maps of the proposed refinement), modes \(T_{r,n}\) of \eqref{eq:79-ks},
and an \emph{auxiliary} grading \(Q_r\ge1\).  Physical energy
\(E_N=\frac{N}{2\pi v_N}(H^{\mathrm{raw}}_N-E_0)\) almost never intertwines exactly with a
refinement, so interpretation (\(E_N\)) and regularity (\(Q_r\)) are separated; their uniform
comparison is a further obligation.  Shells \(E_{r,a}=1_{[2^a,2^{a+1})}(Q_r)\), cutoffs
\(P_r(\Lambda)=1_{[1,\Lambda]}(Q_r)\); constants below are independent of \(r\); each
statement applies to both chiralities and to the mixed residuals \([T_{r,m},\bar T_{r,n}]\).  \emph{Amendment (2026-10-22):}
under equal ledgers the mixed residuals reduce to the zero-mode commutator \([T_{r,0},\bar T_{r,0}]\) (CA-91, Theorem 91.5);
a complex-linear parity supplies equal ledgers (Theorem 91.1).

\textbf{Property P [hypothesis; not synthesised from \(\Cc\)].}
\textbf{P0.} \(Q_{r+1}J_r=J_rQ_r\), \(T_{r,n}^*=T_{r,-n}\).
\textbf{P1.} For every \(d>0\): \(\|E_{r,a}T_{r,n}E_{r,b}\|\le C_{n,d}\,2^b2^{-d|a-b|}\),
full shell blocks with all multiplicities.
\textbf{P2.} With \(z_{mn}=\delta_{m+n,0}(m^3c^*-mc)/12\) and
\(R_{r,m,n}=[T_{r,m},T_{r,n}]-(m-n)T_{r,m+n}-z_{mn}\): \(\|Q_r^tR_{r,m,n}P_r(\Lambda)\|\to0\)
for each fixed \(\Lambda\) and real \(t\ge0\).
\textbf{P3.} \(\Delta_{r,n}=T_{r+1,n}J_r-J_rT_{r,n}\) obeys
\(\|Q_{r+1}^t\Delta_{r,n}P_r(\Lambda)\|\le A_{n,t}2^{-\alpha_{n,t}r}\Lambda^{\gamma_{n,t}}\),
\(\alpha_{n,t}>0\), for all \(\Lambda\ge1\) (full output, low input).

P1 is the uniform lattice analogue of the Carpi--Weiner energy bound
\(\|L_nv\|\le r(1+|n|^{3/2})\|(1+L_0)v\|\) (CW:727--747), with \(Q\) in the role of
\(1+L_0\) and off-diagonal shell decay added [source-local comparison].  The only local
evidence on an interacting chain is numerical and fixed-window: on a nonintegrable chain
the KS modes' low-window overlaps outside the CFT selection rule were fitted to decay as
\(1/N^2\) for \(N=13,\dots,22\) (MV:115, :567, :573) [source-local; not a shell bound].

\textbf{Theorem 79.2 (D4-P) [conditional].}
\textbf{ASSUME:} P0--P3; a fixed integer \(p>1\).  \textbf{PROVE:} \(K_r=Q_r^p\) satisfies
VR-1 A1--A4 (\path{virasoro_routes.md}:55--80); hence modes and all finite words converge
on \(\bigcap_t\operatorname{Dom}(Q^t)\).  Genuine Virasoro needs in addition \(c^*=c\).
\begin{itemize}
\item[\(\langle1\rangle1\).] The \((a,b)\) block of \(Q^tT_nQ^{-t-1}\) is
\(\le2^tC_{n,d}2^{-(d-t)|a-b|}\) (\(d>t\)); a Schur test on row/column sums bounds the
operator norm by \(M_{n,t}=2^tC_{n,d}\frac{1+2^{-(d-t)}}{1-2^{-(d-t)}}\), with no shell-dimension factor.
\item[\(\langle1\rangle2\).] A1 by functional calculus from P0.  A2 at \(t=ps\):
\(K^sT_nK^{-s-1}=(Q^{ps}T_nQ^{-ps-1})Q^{-(p-1)}\).
\item[\(\langle1\rangle3\).] Inserting \(Q^{-t-1}Q^{t+1}\):
\(\|Q^tR_{mn}Q^{-t-2}\|\le B_{mn,t}:=M_{m,t}M_{n,t+1}+M_{n,t}M_{m,t+1}+|m-n|M_{m+n,t}+|z_{mn}|\).
\item[\(\langle1\rangle4\).] Splitting the input at \(\Lambda\), the \(K\)-weighted residual is
\(\le\|Q^tRP(\Lambda)\|+B_{mn,t}\Lambda^{-2(p-1)}\) at \(t=ps\); take \(\Lambda\) large, then \(r\) large (P2): A4.
\item[\(\langle1\rangle5\).] Exact \(Q\)-transport and \(\langle1\rangle1\) give
\(\|Q_{r+1}^t\Delta_{r,n}Q_r^{-t-1}\|\le2M_{n,t}\); the same split gives
\(e_{r,n,s}\le A2^{-\alpha r}\Lambda^{\gamma}+2M_{n,ps}\Lambda^{-(p-1)}\) (P3 constants at \(t=ps\)); \(\Lambda=2^{\theta r}\),
\(\theta\gamma<\alpha\), makes it summable: A3.
\item[\(\langle1\rangle6\).] QED by \(\langle1\rangle1\)--\(\langle1\rangle5\) and VR-1.  The powers \(ps\) are cofinal, so the smooth domain is unchanged.
\end{itemize}
The spare power \(p>1\) is exactly the ``stronger auxiliary regularity weight'' the referee
requires before a fixed-core estimate may be advertised as a graph-norm certificate
(referee:155--161; REVIEW:51).

\textbf{Proposition 79.3 (D4-B) [conditional].}  \textbf{ASSUME:} P1 and
\(\|E_{r,a}R_{r,m,n}E_{r,b}\|\to0\) for each fixed \((a,b)\).  \textbf{PROVE:} P2.
\(\langle1\rangle1\). Products obey an order-two envelope: summing two P1 bounds over the
intermediate shell with exponent \(D>d+2\) gives \(C_d2^{2b}2^{-d|a-b|}\).
\(\langle1\rangle2\). For fixed \(\Lambda\) only finitely many \(b\) occur; the geometric output
tail is uniformly small.  \(\langle1\rangle3\). The finite rectangle tends to zero.  QED.

\textbf{Proposition 79.4 (D4-I) [conditional].}  \textbf{ASSUME:}
\(\|\operatorname{ad}_{Q_r}^k(T_{r,\pm n})E_{r,b}\|\le A_{n,k}2^{(k+1)b}\) for all \(k\ge0\),
uniformly in \(r\).  \textbf{PROVE:} P1.  \(\langle1\rangle1\). For \(a\ge b+2\) the shells are
separated by \(g\ge2^{a-1}\), and \(Y\mapsto\int_0^\infty e^{-sQ_a}Ye^{sQ_b}ds\) inverts
\(X\mapsto Q_aX-XQ_b\) with norm \(\le1/g\).  \(\langle1\rangle2\). Applying it \(k\) times gives
\(2^kA_{n,k}2^b2^{-k(a-b)}\); adjoints give \(a<b\); take \(k>d\).  QED.  Lieb--Robinson
bounds give no such \emph{relative} estimate by themselves: the rescaling in \(E_N\) is extensive.

\textbf{Example 79.5 [negative; spare power is needed].}  On orthonormal pairs \(a_j,b_j\)
with \(Q=2^j\) on the \(j\)-th pair, \(T_{r,1}=2^r\ket{a_r}\bra{b_r}\), \(T_{r,-1}=T_{r,1}^*\),
other modes \(0\), \(c=c^*=0\): P1 holds and all fixed blocks vanish eventually, but
\(R_{r,1,-1}=2^{2r}(\ket{a_r}\bra{a_r}-\ket{b_r}\bra{b_r})\) has \(\|Q^tRQ^{-t-2}\|=1\) while
\(\|K^sRK^{-s-2}\|=2^{-2(p-1)r}\).  (It isolates A4; it does not satisfy A3.)

\subsection{KS Modes under Categorical Refinement}

\textbf{Setting.}  Any isometry \(V:H_N\to H_M\) (a CA-71 placement is one instance),
\(P=VV^*\), \(P^\perp=1-P\), fine densities \(k'_a\), compression \(B_a=V^*k'_aV\), leakage
\(F_a=P^\perp k'_aV\).

\textbf{Lemma 79.6 (D4-R) [derived].}
\(V^*\mathcal J'_aV=i([B_a,B_{a+1}]+F_a^*F_{a+1}-F_{a+1}^*F_a)\);
\(\Delta_n:=T'_{M,n}V-VT_{N,n}=P^\perp T'_{M,n}V+V(V^*T'_{M,n}V-T_{N,n})\) with orthogonal
ranges; \(V^*T'_{M,n}V=a_M\sum_az_M^{na}(B_a-\varepsilon_M-V^*\mathcal J'_aV)+b_M\delta_{n,0}\).
If local stretched representatives \(S_j\) satisfy \(S_jV=Vk_j\), then
\(\Delta_n=\{a_M\sum_az_M^{na}(k'_a-\varepsilon_M-\mathcal J'_a)-a_N\sum_jz_N^{nj}(S_j-\varepsilon_N-i[S_j,S_{j+1}])\}V
+(b_M-b_N)\delta_{n,0}V\): fine-versus-stretched tiles, Fourier phase/scale/velocity, scalar origin.
\textbf{Proof.} Insert \(1=P+P^\perp\) between \(k'_a\) and \(k'_{a+1}\); use \(V^*V=1\). \(\square\)
Compressed currents are not currents of compressed densities; the \(F^*F\) terms are the
missing part.  One inserted vacancy shifts the phase of every later site, so a local density
error does not give a local KS error; \(O(N_r^{-\epsilon})\) is summable on geometric \(N_r\),
\(O(1/N)\) on \(N\to N+1\) is not.  \emph{Support remark [derived]:} the corner image is
\(\theta(k_j)=Vk_jV^*=S_jP\), which pins \emph{every} inserted slot; CA-73:169--171 should be
read through the local representative \(S_j\) (REVIEW:50).

\textbf{Lemma 79.7 (D4-V) [derived; negative].}  \textbf{ASSUME:} fine energy
\(\sum_ak'_a\) nearest-neighbour; \(V\) the bare dyadic insertion of CA-68 (one \(\iota_\one\) after each
site, no dressing).  \textbf{PROVE:} \(V^*(\sum_ak'_a)V\) is a sum of one-site morphisms in
\(\operatorname{End}_\Cc(O)\).  \(\langle1\rangle1\). Each fine bond meets one coarse factor and one
\(\iota_\one\); its compression is \((\mathrm{id}\otimes\iota_\one^\dagger)k'(\mathrm{id}\otimes\iota_\one)\) or
the mirror.  \(\langle1\rangle2\). Sum over bonds.  QED.  \emph{Consequence:} exact covariance of both
\(T_0\) and \(\bar T_0\) would make \(V^*(T'_0+\bar T'_0)V=T_0+\bar T_0\), i.e.\ coarse \(H^{\mathrm{raw}}_N\)
a one-site sum plus scalar; whenever it is not, exact covariance fails for this \(V\).
Leakage in Lemma 79.6 can still mediate two-site terms; dressings change the premise.

\textbf{Theorem 79.8 (D4-N) [derived; negative].}  \textbf{ASSUME:} nonzero finite \(H_r\), A1,
A4, adjoint pairing, and \(T_{r+1,n}J_r=J_rT_{r,n}\) for all \(n\) on all of \(H_r\).
\textbf{PROVE:} \(c^*=0\), \(T_{r,n}=0\) for \(n\ne0\), \(T_{r,0}=\frac c{24}1\).
\(\langle1\rangle1\). Covariance transports \(R_{r,m,n}\) isometrically to every later stage, where A4
sends it to zero on each vector: \(R_{r,m,n}=0\).  \(\langle1\rangle2\). With \(D=\dim H_r\), traces of
the \((1,-1)\), \((2,-2)\) brackets give \(0=2\Tr T_0+(c^*-c)D/12\) and \(0=4\Tr T_0+(8c^*-2c)D/12\);
so \(c^*D/2=0\).  \(\langle1\rangle3\). \([T_0,T_n]=-nT_n\) kills \(T_n\) beyond the spectral diameter
of \(T_0\); the bracket of \(T_a,T_{k-a}\) then gives \((2a-k)T_k=0\).  \(\langle1\rangle4\). The
\((1,-1)\) bracket gives \(T_0=\frac c{24}\).  QED.
\emph{Prior art:} trace cyclicity against a finite-\(N\) central term (GS:2489); finite-matrix
central terms are coboundaries (LC-2 Prop.~5); no exact implementer for \(c\ne0\) (CA-67:104--109,
NG5).  New here: \(\langle1\rangle1\) and the zero-representation conclusion.  Hence A3 must be
approximate; exact \(Q\)-covariance, exact categorical relations and eventual equality on
fixed low cores are not excluded.

\subsection{Extended Fields Are Declared Data}

\textbf{Proposition 79.9 (D4-G) [conditional].}  \textbf{ASSUME:} finitely many convergent unitary
sector characters \(\chi_a(\tau)=\Tr_{V_a}q^{L_0-c/24}\), nonnegative, transforming by the declared
modular \(S\) with \(S_{\one a}>0\); weight-zero vacuum; \(c>1\).  \textbf{PROVE:} \(V_\one\) is not
a finite sum of Virasoro highest-weight modules.
\(\langle1\rangle1\). \(M\) modules would give \(\chi_\one(i\beta/2\pi)\le Me^{\beta c/24}\prod_n(1-e^{-\beta n})^{-1}\) (PBW).
\(\langle1\rangle2\). \(\log\prod\le\sum_k[k(e^{\beta k}-1)]^{-1}\le\pi^2/6\beta\).
\(\langle1\rangle3\). \(S\) and the positive vacuum row give \(\chi_\one(i\beta/2\pi)\ge S_{\one\one}e^{\pi^2c/6\beta}\).
\(\langle1\rangle4\). As \(\beta\to0\) this beats \(\langle1\rangle2\) when \(c>1\).  QED.
The threshold is sharp for this argument.  LC-12 \S5's bound \(\sum k^{-2}<2\) needed
\(c>12/\pi^2\) and was applied at one \(c\) (D5 REVIEW:105--106); D5-H reaches the same \(c>1\).
This answers the general-\(c\) question of CA-75:108--111.

\textbf{Position 79.10 [derived argument; assumptions as stated].}  \textbf{ASSUME} the
hypotheses of Proposition 79.9 for the declared target.  Then (i) Virasoro modes alone, with
finitely many families per sector label, cannot carry \(V_\one\): additional strong generators
and their singular OPE coefficients are part of the declared target data, not output of
\(\Cc\).  (ii) Any \(D\) commuting with every \(h_j\) (closed topological defects do, in the
self-dual multiplicity-free setting of AFM-T:186--212, AFM-F:175--184) commutes with every
\(T_n,\bar T_n\); if \(D=W_n\) with \([T_0,W_n]=-nW_n\), then \(nW_n=0\).  So defect operators
and their polynomials are zero modes, never nonzero chiral generators.  (iii) Endpoint/junction
fields, higher conserved densities and microscopic currents are \emph{proposals} for avatars;
their weights, OPEs and positivity are extra equations.  Transport of such avatars under
VR-1-type bounds with finite losses (D4-X) remains a conditional proof sketch in the note.

\subsection{Headline Conjecture and First Attackable Lemma}
\label{sec:ks-shell-conjecture}

\textbf{Conjecture (D4-C) [conjectural; review verdict: overstated as a statement about
\(\Cc\)].}  From an effective positive rational sewing completion \(\mathcal S\) of a UMTC
\(\Cc\) one can effectively build finite categorical chains, nearest-neighbour tile
Hamiltonians, local refinements and states whose standard KS modes satisfy P, which satisfy
WT-1, and whose limit is unitarily the diagonal theory of \(\mathcal S\) with full fidelity to
\(\Cc\).  Verdict (REVIEW:167--192): the carrier admits unit multiplicities and growing site
objects, hence \emph{any} spin chain; the input \(\mathcal S\) is the full CFT; site growth is
unconstrained relative to locality.  It is an inverse-discretisation (lattice-realisability)
conjecture strengthening ZW Conj.~4.3 and Rmk.~9 (ZW:871--895) and the route of
\path{virasoro_routes.md}:41, which the note does not cite.  \textbf{Proposal:} restate with a
pointed carrier \(O=\one\oplus X\) and bounded support growth, separating the KS-certificate
part from fidelity (REVIEW:231--235).

\textbf{First attackable lemma [proposal].}  Prove P0--P3 for the sourced quasi-free
calibration (\path{cft_machine/benchmarks/fermion/PROOF.md}:12--76; its source-modified
modes) with \(Q=1+{}\)normal-ordered one-particle \(|\)momentum\(|\): zero padding transports
\(Q\) exactly and a mode shifts it by at most \(|n|\) (finite band, P1); the fixed-core defect
\(O(M^{-2}|r|^3)\) on fixed cores (PROOF.md:50--64) suggests P3 with \(\gamma\approx3\).  Through
Theorem 79.2 this would be the first VR-1 instance (CA-75 row (4)) and answer the referee
boundary.  The next general lemma is Proposition 79.4's relative commutator bound for an
interacting density.

\textbf{What this does not claim.}  No instance of P, free or interacting; no
selection of \(h\), \(v_N\), \(\varepsilon_N\), \(b_N\) from \(\Cc\); no periodic categorical
representation or unital OAR map; no positivity, self-adjointness, integration or local-net
identification; no fidelity.  The no-wrap filter of the note is not adopted.

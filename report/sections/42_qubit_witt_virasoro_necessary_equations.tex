\subsection{Exact Density-Mode Algebra}

On a periodic chain of length \(N\), set \(z_n=\exp(2\pi in/N)\) and
\[
  H_n=\sum_jz_n^jh_j,\qquad
  C_j=i[h_j,h_{j+1}],\qquad
  J_n=\sum_jz_n^jC_j .
\]
Then \(H_n^*=H_{-n}\), \(J_n^*=J_{-n}\), and the nearest-neighbour identity is
\[
  [H_m,H_n]=-i(z_n-z_m)J_{m+n}.
  \tag{42.1}
\]
This is the exact lattice antecedent of the Koo-Saleur \(H(f),P(f)\)
commutator.  In Pauli coefficients \(C_j\) is the tensor \(p\) from (39.1).

Define higher-current densities
\[
  D_{r,j}=i[h_{j+r},C_j]\quad(r=0,1,2),
  \qquad
  F^{(r)}_j=i[C_j,C_{j+r}]\quad(r=1,2).
\]
Using the Jacobi cancellation of the \(r=-1\) term,
\[
  i[H_m,J_n]
  =
  \sum_jz_{m+n}^j
  \left(D_{0,j}+z_mD_{1,j}+(z_m^2-z_n)D_{2,j}\right),
  \tag{42.2}
\]
and
\[
  i[J_m,J_n]
  =
  \sum_jz_{m+n}^j
  \left((z_n-z_m)F^{(1)}_j
       +(z_n^2-z_m^2)F^{(2)}_j\right).
  \tag{42.3}
\]
The \(D\)-coefficients are degree three polynomials in \(h_{\alpha\beta}\);
the \(F\)-coefficients are degree four.

\subsection{Chiral Residuals}

A Koo-Saleur-style chiral ansatz has the form
\[
  \ell_n=A_n{:}H_n{:}_\omega+B_n{:}J_n{:}_\omega+\mu\delta_{n0}I,
\]
\[
  \bar\ell_n=A_n{:}H_n{:}_\omega-B_n{:}J_n{:}_\omega+\mu\delta_{n0}I,
\]
with \(A_{-n}=\overline{A_n}\) and \(B_{-n}=\overline{B_n}\).  The necessary
Virasoro residuals for a chosen finite mode set are
\[
  R^{++}_{mn}=
  [\ell_m,\ell_n]-(m-n)\ell_{m+n}
  -\frac{c}{12}m(m^2-1)\delta_{m+n,0}I,
  \tag{42.4}
\]
\[
  R^{--}_{mn}=
  [\bar\ell_m,\bar\ell_n]-(m-n)\bar\ell_{m+n}
  -\frac{c}{12}m(m^2-1)\delta_{m+n,0}I,
  \tag{42.5}
\]
and
\[
  R^{+-}_{mn}=[\ell_m,\bar\ell_n].
  \tag{42.6}
\]
For example,
\[
\begin{aligned}
  [\ell_m,\ell_n]
  &=
  -iA_mA_n(z_n-z_m)J_{m+n}
  -iA_mB_n\mathcal D_{mn}\\
  &\quad
  +iB_mA_n\mathcal D_{nm}
  -iB_mB_n\mathcal F_{mn},
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(\mathcal D_{mn}=i[H_m,J_n]\) and
\(\mathcal F_{mn}=i[J_m,J_n]\) are given by (42.2)--(42.3).

\subsection{Necessary Filter}

At finite size, the Koo-Saleur source explicitly warns that the lattice
quantities need not close.  Therefore the safe condition is not ``finite
Virasoro symmetry''.  The safe condition is:
for the chosen ansatz and mode set, every Pauli coefficient of
\(R^{++},R^{--},R^{+-}\) outside the named span
\[
  \operatorname{span}\{I,H_{m+n},J_{m+n}\}
\]
must either vanish, be a registered finite-size correction, or be annihilated in
the chosen low-energy/state restriction.  CA-43--CA-44 turn the last option into
moment constraints.

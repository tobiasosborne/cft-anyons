\subsection{Status, Sources, and Dependencies}

the review bundle) no Koo--Saleur construction exists for any dilute loop
model or the dilute Temperley--Lieb algebra; this shard fixes the candidate
generators and their proof obligations.  Nothing here is a convergence claim.

\subsection{What the Dense Template Requires}

The sourced dense-TL recipe takes: (i) a critical Hamiltonian
\(H_N=\sum_j h_j\) with density built from algebra generators, normalized so
the sound velocity is one; (ii) the ground-state energy density \(e_\infty\)
subtracted termwise; (iii) a momentum density from the nearest-neighbour
commutator of Hamiltonian densities; (iv) periodic boundary conditions; and
(v) the \(c/24\) zero-mode shift.  The generators are Fourier modes of the
subtracted density plus the current correction.  The known frontier is
recorded in the same source: even in the dense case, the limit of commutators
agrees with the commutator of limits only up to a modified central term, and
parts of that statement remain conjectural.

\subsection{The Dilute Ansatz}

\textbf{Proposal.}  Let \(h_j\) be a self-adjoint, translation-invariant
two-site density in the dilute algebra: a real linear combination of
occupancy projectors, the dense exchange \(u_j=b_j^tb_j\), hopping
\(a_j+a_j^t\), and pair creation/annihilation \(b_j+b_j^t\) (all dilute
diagrams, hence occupied-parity-preserving by CA-69).  On the periodic
chain --- the algebraic setting being the registered periodic dilute
Temperley--Lieb algebra --- define
\[
  E_n^{(N)}=\frac{N}{2\pi v}\sum_{j=1}^{N}e^{inj2\pi/N}\,(h_j-e_\infty),
  \qquad
  p_j=i[h_j,h_{j+1}],
\]
\[
  P_n^{(N)}=\frac{N}{2\pi}\sum_{j=1}^{N}e^{in(j+\frac12)2\pi/N}\,p_j,
\]
\[
  L_n^{(N)}=\tfrac12\bigl(E_n^{(N)}+\tfrac1v P_n^{(N)}\bigr)
  +\tfrac{c}{24}\,\delta_{n,0},
  \qquad
  \bar L_{-n}^{(N)}=\tfrac12\bigl(E_n^{(N)}-\tfrac1v P_n^{(N)}\bigr)
  +\tfrac{c}{24}\,\delta_{n,0},
\]
where \(v\) and \(e_\infty\) are the model's sound velocity and ground-state
energy density, and \(c\) its central charge.  This is the direct dilute
transcription of the sourced dense formula; every ingredient except the
algebra membership of \(h_j\) is inherited.  The candidate modes live in the
even (parity-preserving) dilute ideal, consistently with CA-69.

\subsection{Two Sourced Critical Anchors}

\emph{Integrable anchor (dilute O(n)/Izergin--Korepin).}  The registered
sources give exact branch central charges,
\(c=1-\frac{3\phi^2}{\pi(\pi-2\lambda)}\) (branches 1, 2) and
\(c=\frac32-\frac{3\phi^2}{2\pi\lambda}\) (branches 3, 4), and the periodic
dilute algebra carries the loop transfer matrices.  The explicit quantum-chain
Hamiltonians (the Hamiltonian limits of these transfer matrices, with their
\(v\) and \(e_\infty\)) are in a journal-only source flagged above; until it
is registered, the integrable anchor supports regime selection but not a
shard-ready density.  A recorded warning: at least one regime of the
\(A_2^{(2)}\) model has a \emph{non-compact} continuum limit (registered
source VernierJacobsenSaleur2014), so regime selection is part of the input
data, not a detail.

\emph{Anyonic anchor (itinerant t-J).}  The locally sourced anyonic t-J
models are dilute in exactly the CA-69 sense: anyon hopping plus a dense
projector exchange term, with hard-core occupancy --- an \(h_j\) in the image
of the dilute generators under the CA-69 evaluation map, acting on the CA-66
Fibonacci sectors.  Their central charges are numerically identified
(\(c=7/10\) or \(4/5\) for Fibonacci, \(1/2\) for Ising; entanglement gives
\(1+c\) with the charge sector).  This anchor is shard-ready on the input
side but its criticality is numerical, not Bethe-exact.

\subsection{Proof Obligations}

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
1.&e_\infty,\ v,\ c\ \text{must be sourced (integrable anchor) or estimated}\\
  &\text{with recorded finite-size protocols (anyonic anchor);}\\
2.&\text{the central-term/limit gap is inherited from the dense case and}\\
  &\text{remains conjectural there; no dilute claim may be stronger;}\\
3.&\text{periodic dilute algebra: the CA-69 evaluation map must be extended}\\
  &\text{to the periodic setting before } L_n^{(N)}\text{ acts on word sectors;}\\
4.&\text{gaplessness alone is insufficient: a dilute-looking density can be}\\
  &\text{gapless with dynamical exponent } z\neq1\text{ (no local source yet;}\\
  &\text{registered as an external caution for CA-71 acceptance tests);}\\
5.&\text{diagnostics run through CA-64: normalized GNS residuals } \nu_N\\
  &\text{of the dilute Witt relations on long-wavelength windows.}
\end{array}
\]

\subsection{Validation Plan (CA-71 Tier)}

Exact diagonalization in the Fibonacci \(O=\one\oplus\tau\) charge sectors
(bases already implemented and tested for CA-65--CA-68): build the dense
projector from the sourced unitary \(F\)-matrix, hopping across vacancies,
and raw-cup birth/death; estimate \(e_\infty\), \(v\), and \(c\) from
finite-size spectra; construct \(L_n^{(N)}\) and test low-energy commutator
closure and the CA-64 residuals.  Acceptance: dilute relations hold exactly;
sourced or estimated \((e_\infty,v,c)\) reproduce the anchor values; current
modes do not collapse; residuals decrease on the named window.  Failure
witnesses: current collapse, wrong central charge, residuals bounded away
from zero, or non-conformal (\(z\neq1\)) finite-size scaling.

\subsection{Boundary}

No convergence theorem, no continuum Virasoro representation, and no claim
that the ansatz closes even at the level of the dense case's conjectures.
The integrable anchor's Hamiltonian is a registered acquisition gap.  The
anyonic anchor's criticality is numerical input, not a theorem.  The
extension of the CA-69 evaluation map to the periodic dilute algebra is
future work.

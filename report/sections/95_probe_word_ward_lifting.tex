\subsection{Physical Probe Bounds and the Conditional Geometric Interface}
\textbf{Lemma 95.1 [derived; any; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:49--111; REVIEW:51--58).}
\textbf{Assume} finite positive quotients \(H_r\), \(K_r\ge1\), dense linear spans \(D_r\) of reflected probes;
\(\rho_r(u,v)=\langle u,R_rv\rangle\) descends through both null spaces. Pairings are linear in the second slot (al).
Write \(|R|_{s,2}=\|K^sRK^{-s-2}\|\) as in (ar). For each integer \(s\ge0\), fix \(0\le\alpha_s<1\) independently of \(r\).
Require, uniformly for \emph{all linear combinations} \(u,v\in D_r\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:95-probe}
 |\rho_r(K_r^su,e^{-tK_r}v)|\le b_{r,s}t^{-s-1-\alpha_s}\|u\|\|v\|,\qquad 0<t\le1.
\end{equation}
\textbf{Prove} \(|R_r|_{s,2}\le C_{s,\alpha_s}b_{r,s}\), where
\(C_{s,\alpha}=((s+1)!(1-\alpha))^{-1}+\sum_{j=0}^{s+1}1/j!\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Finite dimensionality gives \(D_r=H_r\); the dual norm identity turns
\eqref{eq:95-probe} into \(\|K_r^sR_re^{-tK_r}\|\le b_{r,s}t^{-s-1-\alpha_s}\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Spectral diagonalisation and integration by parts give
\(K^{-s-2}=((s+1)!)^{-1}\int_0^\infty t^{s+1}e^{-tK}\,dt\). On \((0,1]\) the bound integrates to
\(b/((s+1)!(1-\alpha))\); on \([1,\infty)\), factor \(e^{-tK}=e^{-K}e^{-(t-1)K}\), giving
\(b\int_1^\infty t^{s+1}e^{1-t}dt/(s+1)!=b\sum_{j=0}^{s+1}1/j!\). Triangle inequality proves the claim.
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). For an independent probe basis with Gram \(G\) and \emph{form} matrix \(B\), physical
unit vectors have coordinates \(G^{-1/2}x\); the form norm is \(\|G^{-1/2}BG^{-1/2}\|\).
Quotient null coordinates first. This is not D2-L9's operator-coordinate transformation. No ambient-state faithfulness is needed.

\textbf{Theorem 95.2 [conditional; full per family; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:115--166, 175--183; REVIEW:59--64).}
\textbf{Assume} 95.1's quotient, \(N=N_r\to\infty\), geometric modes
\(S_{r,m}=N^{-1}\sum_j f_m(j)Q_{r,j}+b_r\delta_{m0}\), \(f_m(j)=e^{2\pi imj/N}\), as in (ar).
Put \(\ell_N=2\pi/N\), \(d_N(m)=\sin(m\ell_N)/\ell_N\), \(\beta_N=2d_N(m)-d_N(m+n)\), \(N>|m+n|\).
Declare a finite sewn-diagram sequence \(F_0,\ldots,F_L\) on the \emph{same} quotient, with:
\textbf{G1} \(F_L-F_0=\langle u,[S_m,S_n]v\rangle+\rho_{\rm end}(u,v)\), representing both orderings;
\textbf{G2} each step is its Ward contact plus residual, and the transported contact sum is
\(\langle u,(\beta_NS_{m+n}+z^{(r)}_{mn}I)v\rangle+\rho_{\rm chart}(u,v)\), where
\[
 z^{(r)}_{mn}=\delta_{m+n,0}\{\kappa_{3,r}d_N(m)^3+(\kappa_{1,r}-2b_r)d_N(m)\}.
\]
\textbf{G3} all errors obey \eqref{eq:95-probe} with the same \(\alpha_s\), after quotient, port transport,
Fourier factors and insertion counts; \(B_{r,m,n,s}:=b_{\rm end}+b_{\rm chart}+\sum_i|a_i|b_i\to0\).
A directly proved smaller bound on the assembled form may replace this absolute sum.
\textbf{Prove} W2: \(|\mathcal R_{r,m,n}|_{s,2}\le C_{s,\alpha_s}B_{r,m,n,s}\to0\), with
\(\mathcal R_{r,m,n}=[S_{r,m},S_{r,n}]-\beta_NS_{r,m+n}-z^{(r)}_{mn}I\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Sum successive diagram differences: internal diagrams cancel.
G1--G2 give \(\langle u,\mathcal R_{r,m,n}v\rangle=\sum_i a_i\rho_i+\rho_{\rm chart}-\rho_{\rm end}\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). G3 and the triangle inequality give \eqref{eq:95-probe} with constant \(B\).
Apply 95.1. This identifies the actual residual; it does not derive G1 or G2 from density or D2's plane identity.

\subsection{Two Obstructions and the Precise Analytic Converse}
\textbf{Negative 95.4 [negative; any; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:185--201; REVIEW:65--67).}
Fixed-word decay cannot replace \eqref{eq:95-probe}: take \(H_r=\Span(e_0,\ldots,e_r)\), \(K_r=I\),
\(\Omega=e_0\), inclusion refinements and \(R_r=\ket{e_r}\bra{e_r}\). Finite matrix-unit probes acting on \(\Omega\)
span all finite-support vectors. Each fixed probe pair has eventually zero residual, also after heat collaring;
yet \(R_re_r=e_r\), so \(|R_r|_{s,2}=1\). This refutes only word-dependent constants, not a uniform bound.

\textbf{Negative 95.5 [negative; full, both KS families; category-free; trivial \(\mathsf R2\)]\newline (NOTE:205--340; REVIEW:68--82).}
A1--A4, including dense cyclic probes, do not imply vanishing subcritical collar constants for the same residual.
\textbf{Construct} \(N_r=8\cdot2^r\), \(a_r=e^{N_r^2}\), \(\lambda_r=a_r^2N_r^3\),
\(H_r=(\mathbb C\ket0\oplus\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes N_r}\), with active projection \(P\).
On the active block \(X=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
\(Y=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\), \(Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)\); all vanish on \(\ket0\).
Set \(k_j=a_r(P_jP_{j+1}+X_jZ_{j+1})\), \(h_j=-k_j\), \(v_r=1\), \(\varepsilon_r=b_r=0\).
With \(z=e^{2\pi i/N}\), use exactly
\(T_n=\frac N{4\pi}\sum_jz^{nj}(k_j-\mathcal J_j)\),
\(\bar T_n=\frac N{4\pi}\sum_jz^{-nj}(k_j+\mathcal J_j)\), \(\mathcal J_j=i[k_j,k_{j+1}]\).
Let \(U\) shift one site; \(J_r\) inserts vacancies at odd \emph{zero-based} fine sites, retaining old sites at even ones.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). The active density is \(a(I+X\otimes Z)\ge0\), with eigenvalues \(0,2a\); other occupation
blocks vanish. The vacancy vacuum has energy zero and is cyclic for product matrix units, giving dense probes.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Set \(K_0=1\) on the vacuum and \(\lambda_0\) off it. At the next scale let
\(F_+=\Ran J_r\), \(F_-=U_{r+1}\Ran J_r\): their intersection is the vacuum line and their nonvacuum parts are orthogonal.
Transport \(K_r\) by \(J_r\) on \(F_+\), by \(U_{r+1}J_r\) on \(F_-\); set \(K_{r+1}=\lambda_{r+1}I\) on
\((F_++F_-)^\perp\). The prescriptions agree at the vacuum. Since \(U_{r+1}^2J_r=J_rU_r\), induction gives
\(K\ge1\), \(\min\operatorname{spec}K=1\), \([K,U]=0\), \(K_{r+1}J_r=J_rK_r\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Every density kills \(F_++F_-\); self-adjointness makes the complement invariant.
All mode support therefore has \(K_r=\lambda_r I\). Direct multiplication gives
\(\mathcal J_j=-2a^2X_jY_{j+1}Z_{j+2}\) and \(\sup_n\|T_n\|,\sup_n\|\bar T_n\|\le L_N:=N^2a^2/\pi\).
Thus adjoint pairing holds, A2 is bounded by \(1/(\pi N)\), and, with \(B_{mn}=2|m|+|m+n|\),
\[
 |[T_m,T_n]-\beta_NT_{m+n}|_{s,2}
 \le\frac{2L_N^2+B_{mn}L_N}{\lambda_N^2}
 \le\frac2{\pi^2N^2}+\frac{B_{mn}}{\pi a_N^2N^4}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Replacing \(\beta_N\) by \(m-n\) proves A4 with \(c=c^*=0\), also for the barred family;
the mixed bracket is at most \(2L_N^2/\lambda_N^2\). Since \(T_{r+1,n}J_r=0\), the A3 defect is
\(-J_rT_{r,n}\), bounded by \(1/(\pi N_r)\), summable in \(r\). Every limiting mode is zero.
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). On the all-active block, the coefficient of \(X_jY_{j+1}Y_{j+2}Y_{j+3}Z_{j+4}\)
in \(E=[T_1,T_{-1}]-\beta_NT_0\) is \(c_N=N^2a_N^4\sin(4\pi/N)/\pi^2\).
Only distance-two current pairs contribute: their commutator is \(8ia^4XYYYZ\), multiplied by
\((N/(4\pi))^2(z^{-2}-z^2)\). Normalized Pauli trace gives \(\|E\|\ge|c_N|\ge8Na_N^4/\pi^2\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle5\). Fix \(s\ge0\), \(0\le\alpha<1\), \(p=s+1+\alpha\).
For \emph{sufficiently large} \(N\), \(t=p/\lambda_N\le1\); a proposed collar bound forces
\[
 b_N\ge p^pe^{-p}|c_N|\lambda_N^{-1-\alpha}
 \ge\frac{8p^pe^{-p}}{\pi^2}e^{2(1-\alpha)N^2}N^{-2-3\alpha}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
This control is nonphysical and stress collapses: physical-energy comparison, noncollapse and the full D2 certificate are not refuted.

\textbf{Proposition 95.6 [derived; any; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:344--365, 383--388; REVIEW:83--84, 86).}
For finite \(R_r\), \(K_r\ge1\), \(q_{r,s}=|R_r|_{s,2}\to0\), W2 is equivalent to the uniform assembled pairing bound
\[
 |\langle u,R_rv\rangle|\le q_{r,s}\|K_r^{-s}u\|\|K_r^{s+2}v\|,
 \qquad \|K_r^sR_re^{-tK_r}\|\le A_{s+2}q_{r,s}t^{-s-2},\quad A_p=(p/e)^p.
\]
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Factor the pairing through \(K^sRK^{-s-2}\); uniform duality proves the converse.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Factor the heat operator similarly and maximize \(x^pe^{-x}\) at \(x=p\).
The heat exponent is critical (\(\alpha=1\)); its gamma-integral majorant diverges logarithmically at zero.
Finite fixed Fourier sums obey \(|\sum f_mg_nR_{r,m,n}|_{s,2}\le\sum|f_mg_n|q_{r,m,n,s}\to0\);

\textbf{Lemma 95.7 [conditional; any; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:367--381; REVIEW:85).}

\subsection{Spectral Calibration and the First Categorical Input}
\textbf{Proposition 95.8 [derived; AP chiral CAR; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\)]\newline (NOTE:390--504; REVIEW:87--94, 153--159).}

For even \(M\ge8\), \(\epsilon=2\pi/M\), \(r=-M/2+1/2,\ldots,M/2-1/2\), use (w)'s
\(A_ne_r=\cos^2(\epsilon n/4)\sin(\epsilon(r-n/2))e_{r-n}/\epsilon\), deleting transitions outside the window.
Let \(L_n={:}d\Gamma(A_n){:}\) in the complex sea \(1_{r<0}\), \(Q=\sum_{\rm exc}|r|\), \(K=1+Q\).
For \(n=1,2,3\), put \(R_{n,-n}=[L_n,L_{-n}]-2nL_0-(n^3-n)I/12\),
\(C_n=[A_n,A_{-n}]-2nA_0\), \(\delta_n=c_S(A_n,A_{-n})-(n^3-n)/12\). Then, for every integer \(s\ge0\),
\begin{gather*}
 |R_{n,-n}|_{s,2}\le U_n(\epsilon):=C_n^{\rm bd}\epsilon+B_n^{\rm bd}\epsilon^2,\qquad
 C_n^{\rm bd}=\pi(n+7n^3/24)+\frac{(n-1)^2}{\pi(4.5-n)^2},\\

 \quad(B_1^{\rm bd},B_2^{\rm bd},B_3^{\rm bd})=(0,7/24,35/12),\\
 \|K^sR_{n,-n}e^{-tK}\|\le\sqrt{A_{s+2}U_n(\epsilon)H_nA_{s+1}}\,t^{-s-3/2},\quad

\(C_n(r)=2n\sin(\epsilon r)[\cos(\epsilon r)\cos^4(\epsilon n/4)\operatorname{sinc}(\epsilon n)-1]/\epsilon\),

\subsection{The Actual Construction Target}

\textbf{Programme.} Discover an algorithm, or functor, from categorical
data to concrete lattice systems and microscopic Virasoro generators,
whose limit through operator-algebraic renormalisation (OAR) and Wilson's
triangle is a rigorous CFT with the input modular data. Indeterminate-number
anyon spaces are one mechanism; they do not restrict the search.

A successful output names the microscopic algebras and Hamiltonians,
refinement maps, renormalized states, symmetry generators, limiting local
CFT, and modular-data comparison. Any additional conformal input must be
explicit. Matching a finite spectrum, producing limiting commutators,
or checking a finite moment problem does not supply the complete output.

The new workspace investigates a source-controlled fermion seed, a
graph-norm convergence criterion, and equivariant fixed-point constructions
alongside the anyonic tower. The source-grounded comparison and scoped
claims are in \texttt{cft\_machine/research/}.

\subsection{A Quantitative Wilson-State Theorem}

\textbf{Theorem WT-1 [local derivation].}
\textbf{Assume} unital C*-algebras $A_k$ and coherent unital injective
$*$-maps $\alpha_n^k:A_k\to A_n$ for $n\ge k$. Let $\omega_n$ be
microscopic states. Suppose for each fixed $k$ there are nonnegative
$d_{k,n}$ with
\[
 \|\omega_{n+1}\circ\alpha_{n+1}^k-
       \omega_n\circ\alpha_n^k\|\le d_{k,n},\qquad
 \sum_{n\ge k}d_{k,n}<\infty.
\]
The norm is the dual operator norm. Set $t_k(n)=\sum_{j\ge n}d_{k,j}$.
\textbf{Prove} there is a unique state $\omega$ on the inductive algebra
whose restrictions are the horizontal row limits, and
\[
 \|\omega\circ i_k-\omega_n\circ\alpha_n^k\|\le t_k(n).
\]

\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] Every row is norm Cauchy.
\textsc{By} telescoping adjacent differences and summability; for $m>n$
the difference is bounded by $\sum_{j=n}^{m-1}d_{k,j}$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] Its limit $\omega_k$ is a state with the
stated tail bound. \textsc{By} completeness of the dual, closure of
positivity and normalization under norm limits, and $\langle1\rangle1$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle3$.] The limiting family is compatible:
$\omega_l\circ\alpha_l^k=\omega_k$. \textsc{By} coherence, the analogous
equality holds for every microscopic scale $n\ge l$; take row limits.
\item[$\langle1\rangle4$.] Define $\omega(i_k(a))=\omega_k(a)$ on the
dense algebraic union. It is well-defined, bounded, positive and unital.
\textsc{By} $\langle1\rangle2$--$\langle1\rangle3$; continuous extension
gives existence and density gives uniqueness.
\item[$\langle1\rangle5$.] The GNS maps $[a]_k\mapsto[\alpha_l^k(a)]_l$
are compatible isometries preserving cyclic vectors. \textsc{By}
multiplicativity, unitality, and
$\omega_l(\alpha_l^k(a^*a))=\omega_k(a^*a)$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle6$.] QED \textsc{by}
$\langle1\rangle2$--$\langle1\rangle5$.
\end{description}

\textbf{Effective corollary WT-2.} If expectations and refinements are
computable and the tail has a computable modulus, an observable $a\in A_k$
with a supplied computable bound $B\ge\|a\|$ can be evaluated in the
limit to error $\eta$: choose $n$ with $t_k(n)B<\eta/2$ and compute its
row expectation to error $\eta/2$. The modulus is substantive analytic
input; finitely sampled decay does not provide it.

\subsection{The Generator Contract and Its Boundary}

\textbf{Conditional local theorem VR-1.} The companion proof in
\path{cft_machine/research/virasoro_routes.md} constructs limiting
modes and all their finite products under four explicit hypotheses:
compatible isometric refinements with a positive auxiliary control operator,
uniform bounds between successive graph norms, summable refinement defects
in those norms, and vanishing weighted closure residuals. Its proof uses
complete graph spaces and a direct telescoping identity for products.

The finite identity embeds as a projection $P_N$, with $P_N\to1$ on the
common smooth domain. A corner embedding is not identified with a unital
observable map. Both central coefficients of CONVENTIONS (s) remain explicit.
Adjoint pairing on the domain does not prove essential self-adjointness.

These two contracts must use the \emph{same} state and representation
system. WT-1 alone constructs no dynamics; VR-1 alone constructs no local
OAR algebras. Positive energy, integration of smeared modes, local-net
axioms, rationality, and modular fidelity need separate proofs or carefully
verified applications of existing theorems.

\textbf{Numerical scope.} The fermion benchmark separately checks a
generator-refinement square and the pullbacks of finite Dirac ground-state
covariances. Its compatible limiting chiral sea is not substituted for
these nontrivial Wilson rows. The corresponding source and run paths are
listed in \texttt{INDEX.md} and the benchmark README.

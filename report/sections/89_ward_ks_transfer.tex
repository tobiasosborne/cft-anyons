\subsection{Setting and the Conjecture as Posed}

A tower \((H_r,J_r,K_r)\), \(N=N_r\to\infty\), satisfies VR-1 A1: finite Hilbert spaces, isometries \(J_r\),
\(K_r\ge1\), \(K_{r+1}J_r=J_rK_r\).  The modes \(T_{r,n}\) are the standard KS modes \eqref{eq:79-ks} of (ap),
\(T_{r,n}=\frac N{4\pi}\sum_jz^{nj}(k_j-\varepsilon_r-\mathcal J_j)+b_r\delta_{n0}\), \(k_j=-h_{r,j}/v_r\),
\(\mathcal J_j=i[k_j,k_{j+1}]\), with no change of prescription.  Graph norms (v):
\(|X|_{s,d}^{t\leftarrow r}:=\|K_t^sXK_r^{-s-d}\|\) (scale superscripts omitted within one scale).  VR-1 A2:
\(|T_{r,n}|_{s,1}\le C_{n,s}\); A3: \(e_{r,n,s}:=|T_{r+1,n}J_r-J_rT_{r,n}|_{s,1}\) summable in \(r\); A4:
\(|[T_m,T_n]-(m-n)T_{m+n}-z_{mn}1|_{s,2}\to0\), \(z_{mn}=\delta_{m+n,0}(m^3c^*-mc)/12\), constants kept separate
as in (s).  \emph{Geometric modes} \(S_{r,m}=N^{-1}\sum_jf_m(j)Q_{r,j}+b_r\delta_{m0}\), \(f_m(j)=e^{2\pi imj/N}\), are
built from boundary metric insertions \(Q_{r,j}\) on the positive quotient; \(D_{r,n}:=T_{r,n}-S_{r,n}\),
\(d_{r,n,s}:=|D_{r,n}|_{s,1}\).  A \emph{periodic translation} is a unitary \(U_r\), \(U_r^{N}=1\), commuting with
\(K_r\) and the time gates, with \(U_rT_{r,n}U_r^*=e^{-2\pi in/N}T_{r,n}\) (declared; see Status).

\textbf{Conjecture E4 [conjectural as posed; full, \(\mathsf L0\), \(\mathsf R2\); brief:66--77].}  If the gate metric
derivative equals \(h\) minus a continuity current with residual summable in the graph norm of
\(K_N=1+(H_N-E_0)/v\) (C1), and discrete Ward equations hold with convergent contact coefficients (C3), then (a)
the KS modes satisfy A4 with \(z_{mn}\) fixed by the contacts, (b) they satisfy A3 for \emph{any} refinement that
intertwines the gates up to a summable error, and (c) the central charge is the Ward contact coefficient.
\emph{Verdict (REVIEW:226--240).}  (a) is not proved: its whole content sits in W2 (Lemma 89.5, Remark F1).
(b) is \textbf{false} whenever \(c^*\neq0\) (Proposition 89.7), and real gate errors cannot give A3 (89.6).
(c) holds only for \(c^*=12\kappa_3\); \(c\) also records the declared zero-mode shift \(b\) (89.1(iii)).  Its
literal control \(K_N\) violates A2 (89.3).

\textbf{Theorem 89.1 [conditional; full, one chiral family at a time; category-free; \(\to\mathsf R2\);
INTEGRATION:9--34].}  Let \((H_r,J_r,K_r)\) satisfy A1, with \(\min\operatorname{spec}K_r=1\), and a periodic translation.
(i) \emph{Transfer.} If \(S_{r,n}\) satisfy A2 (\(|S_{r,n}|_{s,1}\le M_{n,s}\)) and A4, and \(d_{r,n,s}\to0\), then
\(T\) satisfies A2 and A4 with the same central term, with bound \eqref{eq:89-transfer}.
(ii) \emph{Refinement.} If holomorphic metric families \(B_{r,n}(\eta)\) with \(B_{r,n}'(0)=S_{r,n}\) obey
\eqref{eq:89-holo}, \(J_r\) and \(K_r\) are independent of \(\eta\), and \(d\) is summable, then \(T\) satisfies A3 with
bound \eqref{eq:89-A3}.
(iii) \emph{Contact cocycle.} If \(T\) satisfies A2 and A4-type residuals with convergent scalars
\(z^{(r)}_{mn}\to z_{mn}\) vanish in \(|\cdot|_{s,2}\), then \(z_{mn}=\delta_{m+n,0}(m^3c^*-mc)/12\) with
\(c^*=2(a_2-2a_1)\), \(c=2a_2-16a_1\), \(a_m:=z_{m,-m}\).  If the scalar contacts are first- and third-derivative
Ward contacts \(\kappa_1,\kappa_3\) (cylinder chart) with zero-mode shift \(b\), then \(c^*=12\kappa_3\),
\(c=24b-12\kappa_1\).

\subsection{The Contact Cocycle: Theorem 89.1(iii)}

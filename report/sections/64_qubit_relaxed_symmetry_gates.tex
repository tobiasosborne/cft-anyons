\subsection{Coefficient Norm and Gauge}

Let \(V_L\) be the Pauli coefficient space of \(L\)-site densities, with
\[
  \langle r,s\rangle_\tau=\sum_a\overline{r_a}s_a.
\]
This is the normalized tracial Hilbert--Schmidt inner product.  Padding by
trailing identities is denoted \(\iota_{L,N}:V_L\to V_N\).

The coboundary map of CONVENTIONS.md (n) is
\[
  (Du)_{a_0\cdots a_L}
  =u_{a_0\cdots a_{L-1}}\delta_{a_L0}
   -\delta_{a_00}u_{a_1\cdots a_L}.
\]
Residuals are distances to affine subspaces in this norm.  Scalar witness
components are gauge directions; they are fixed to zero for reported witnesses,
but the distance itself is gauge-invariant.

\subsection{Algebraic Profiles}

The current gate remains hard for the nonzero-speed first-moment route:
\[
  \|p\|_\tau=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{fail this route}.
\]
For \(A=i[h_j+h_{j+1},p_j]\), define the conservation profile
\[
  \epsilon_c(L;h)
  =
  \operatorname{dist}\bigl(\iota_{3,n}A,
  \iota_{L+1,n}\operatorname{im}D_L\bigr),
  \qquad n=\max(3,L+1).
\]
The reported dimensionless value is
\[
  \widehat\epsilon_c(L;h)
  =
  \epsilon_c(L;h)/\max(\|A\|_\tau,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{mach}}).
\]

For candidates with exact conservation at support \(L\), define the
exact-conservation boost profile
\[
  \epsilon_b^0(L;h,\lambda,e)
  =\min_{u,w:\,D_Lu=A}
  \|\iota B-\iota u-\lambda\,\iota(h-eI)-D_Lw\|_\tau ,
\]
with \(B=i[p_j,h_{j+1}]+2i[p_j,h_{j+2}]\) and \(\lambda=v^2\).  Letting \(u\)
float freely in the boost residual is wrong: it can absorb boost error while no
longer witnessing the conservation coboundary.  For candidates without exact
conservation, use the joint profile
\[
  \epsilon_{cb}^2
  =
  \min_{u,w}
  \widehat{\|A-Du\|}_\tau^2
  +
  \widehat{\|B-u-\lambda(h-eI)-Dw\|}_\tau^2 .
\]
The raw profiles are nonincreasing in \(L\), because a support-\(L\) witness is
also a support-\(L+1\) witness after padding by an identity.  The speed
\(\lambda\) is optimized only by external scan or bounded quadratic
programming, with \(\mu=\lambda e\) as the linear scalar variable; the sign of
the optimizing \(\lambda\) is a recorded diagnostic, not an assumption.

\subsection{State-Level Residuals}

For a fixed residual density \(R\), the GNS quantity is
\[
  \|\pi_\omega(R)\Omega_\omega\|^2=\omega(R^*R).
\]
At moment level \(N\), define
\[
  v_N(R)=\min_y \omega_y(R^*R)
\]
subject to the CA-43--CA-44 moment PSD, translation-invariance, normalization,
and optional stationarity constraints.  For fixed \(R\), this is an SDP because
\(\omega_y(R^*R)\) is affine in \(y\).  Normalize by
\[
  \nu_N(R)=v_N(R)/\tau(R^*R),
\]
when \(\tau(R^*R)>0\); the tracial value \(\tau(R^*R)=\sum_s|r_s|^2\) is
computable exactly from Pauli coefficients.  A dual (certified) lower bound
\(\nu_N(R)\ge\delta>0\) is an exclusion certificate for the relaxed route;
\(\nu_N(R)\) small is survival, never symmetry evidence.

Do not optimize \(u,w,\lambda,e\) jointly with the moments in this SDP tier.
For \(R(a)=R_0+\sum_i a_iR_i\), the expression
\(\omega(R(a)^*R(a))\) is quadratic in \(a\) with coefficients affine in \(y\),
hence bilinear jointly.  That joint minimization is not a plain SDP.
Alternation gives upper-bound candidates; certified lower bounds require fixed
witnesses or a bounded polynomial/sum-of-squares lift recorded as a new
convention before use.

\subsection{Scaling Reading}

A continuum claim is not a fixed-\(N\) claim.  A scaling statement must name:
state sequence, renormalisation maps, vacuum subtraction, and operator
topology, as required by CA-14 and CA-15.  The target form is that normalized
GNS residuals vanish on a growing long-wavelength window:
\[
  \sup_{|m|,|n|\le K_N}
  \nu_N(r^{\sigma\sigma'}_{mn})\to0,
  \qquad K_N/N\to0,
\]
applied to the CA-42 Witt/Virasoro residual densities
\(r^{++}_{mn},r^{--}_{mn},r^{+-}_{mn}\).  Modes near the Brillouin scale are
not expected to close first; long-wavelength modes are the diagnostic window.

\subsection{Sentinel Expectations}

These are predictions to be checked by the relaxed re-scan, not results.  The
self-dual TFIM point is currentful, has an exact conservation witness, and
fails the exact boost gate with recorded residual norm 1.8257418583505538; the
relaxed verdict should therefore be survival into a GNS/scaling queue rather
than terminal exclusion.  Fully on-site and classical commuting densities
still fail the hard current gate.  Generic dense samples must show actual
profile decay to survive; a single small value at one support is not a pass.

\subsection{Boundary}

No continuum theorem is claimed.  The profiles and SDP objectives are finite
diagnostics; converting decay of \(\nu_N\) into a continuum symmetry statement
requires the scaling payload of CA-15 and remains open.  The longer-support
TFIM profile values computed during design are not quoted here because their
run bundle is not yet registered; only the recorded scan artifact is cited.

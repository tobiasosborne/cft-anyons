\subsection{Vacuum Insertion}

\textbf{Definition [choice].}  Fix \(O=\one\oplus X\).  Let
\(\iota_{\one}:\one\to O\) be the empty-site summand inclusion.  The canonical
site-doubling refinement is
\[
  V_L:O^{\otimes L}\longrightarrow O^{\otimes 2L},
  \qquad
  \text{old site }j\mapsto 2j-1,\quad \text{fine site }2j\mapsto\one .
\]
Equivalently, in each two-site fine cell the local factor is
\(\operatorname{id}_O\otimes\iota_{\one}:O\to O\otimes O\), with the fixed
left-associated bracketing of CONVENTIONS.md (r).

\textbf{Lemma [derived].}  \(V_L\) is an isometry:
\(V_L^\dagger V_L=\operatorname{id}_{O^{\otimes L}}\).  Each inserted factor
contributes \(\iota_{\one}^\dagger\iota_{\one}=\operatorname{id}_{\one}\), and
tensor products of isometries are isometries.

\textbf{Lemma [derived].}  The induced map on charge sectors
\[
  J_{L,c}:\Hom(X_c,O^{\otimes L})\to\Hom(X_c,O^{\otimes 2L}),
  \qquad
  w\mapsto V_L\circ w,
\]
is isometric and preserves the total charge \(c\).  It is strictly local: the
cellwise factors act on disjoint fine two-site cells.

\subsection{Algebra Map}

\textbf{Observation [derived].}  Let \(P_L=V_LV_L^\dagger\).  The map
\[
  \theta_L(T)=V_LT V_L^\dagger,\qquad
  T\in \Alg_L:=\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O^{\otimes L}),
\]
is a *-isomorphism from \(\Alg_L\) onto the corner \(P_L\Alg_{2L}P_L\).  It is
not a unital embedding into \(\Alg_{2L}\), because \(\theta_L(1)=P_L\neq1\).

\textbf{Boundary [open].}  Strict OAR, as locally sourced, wants unital
injective *-morphisms of algebras.  Thus \(\theta_L\) is not yet a strict OAR
scaling map on the full \(\Alg_{2L}\).  A unital extension needs complement
data that this shard does not canonically supply; the honest alternatives are
a unital completely positive completion inside a \emph{soft} C*-system with
asymptotic multiplicativity (CA-67), or the state/GNS corner formalism.

\subsection{State Consistency}

\textbf{Exact corner consistency [derived].}  If a state \(\omega_{2L}\) on
\(\Alg_{2L}\) satisfies \(\omega_{2L}(P_L)=1\), then
\(\omega_L:=\omega_{2L}\circ\theta_L\) is a state on \(\Alg_L\).  For the
CA-66 charge-diagonal states, exact consistency is obtained by choosing fine
minimal projections \(e_c^{(2L)}=V_Le_c^{(L)}V_L^\dagger\).

\textbf{Dynamical-vacuum condition [open].}  A physical scaling vacuum should
satisfy exact or asymptotic GNS consistency \(J_L\Omega_L\approx\Omega_{2L}\).
Bare vacuum insertion supplies a candidate reference subspace, not a
dynamical-vacuum theorem.

\subsection{Density Reading}

\textbf{Observation [derived].}  A word with \(N\) occupied sites refines to a
word with the same \(N\) occupied sites on \(2L\) fine sites: the site-filling
fraction halves while the \emph{physical} particle number at fixed physical
volume is unchanged as the lattice spacing halves.  Dense chains cannot do
this: every refined site must carry the chain anyon, which is the CA-67
diagnosis of why the braiding-RG route does not port.

\textbf{Boundary [open].}  This reading fits fixed-particle and finite
physical-density sectors.  Whether a \emph{critical} vacuum sector is
reachable depends on the dressed state and coupling flow below.

\subsection{Virasoro Compatibility Target}

\textbf{Target [open].}  Let \({:}L^{(L)}_{k,\pm}(g_L){:}\) denote
normal-ordered Koo--Saleur approximants at renormalized coupling \(g_L\)
(vacuum subtraction per CA-14).  A refinement family \(W_L\) should satisfy,
on a low-energy GNS core and a growing long-wavelength window,
\[
  \sup_{|k|\le K_L}
  \bigl\|
    {:}L^{(2L)}_{k,\pm}(g_{2L}){:}\,W_L\psi
    -W_L\,{:}L^{(L)}_{k,\pm}(g_L){:}\,\psi
  \bigr\|\longrightarrow0,
  \qquad K_L/L\to0.
\]
This is the CA-67 asymptotic-intertwining condition specialised to Koo--Saleur
modes, the anyonic analogue of the sourced free-fermion convergence theorem.
Per CA-67 there is no exact-implementer formulation to aim for when
\(c\neq0\); asymptotic intertwining in a soft system is the target statement.

\textbf{Proposal [open].}  Bare \(V_L\) is not expected to satisfy the target
for a fixed local Hamiltonian.  Use a dressed family
\[
  W_L=U_LV_L,
\]
with \(U_L\in\Alg_{2L}\) a bounded-depth, charge-preserving local circuit,
scale- and coupling-dependent.  \(W_L\) remains local, isometric, and
charge-preserving; the role of \(U_L\) is to create the short-range
entanglement that bare empty-site insertion lacks, playing the part of the
wavelet low-pass filter (and of the braiding disentangler in the dense
setting) so that NG1--NG3 of CA-67 are avoided.

\subsection{Dead Prior Art: the Deficit/Pair-Creation Precedent}

The archived predecessor project (dead, not canonical; re-derive before use)
validated two fine-graining isometries numerically for Fibonacci/Ising at
small size under D4 wavelets: a \emph{normalised product map} (spread each
anyon through a one-particle wavelet isometry, column-normalise the diagonal
Gram matrix) and an experimental \emph{number-changing isometry} \(V_0+V_2\)
in which the isometry deficit \(I-V_0^\dagger V_0\) of the raw fixed-\(N\)
wavelet map is filled by a pair-creation channel into the \(N{+}2\) sector.
\textbf{Reading [derived].}  The deficit phenomenon is structural evidence for
the present programme: fixed-\(N\) wavelet refinement fails isometry exactly
where indefinite anyon number can repair it, using the CA-66 birth maps.  The
archived construction was never reconciled with any algebraic framework,
never connected to Virasoro data, and its existence conditions were not
proved; those are re-derivation targets for this block, not inherited results.

\subsection{Fibonacci \(H_2\to H_4\) Test}

\textbf{Data [derived].}  For Fibonacci \(O=\one\oplus\tau\),
\((m_{\one}(2),m_{\tau}(2))=(2,3)\) and
\((m_{\one}(4),m_{\tau}(4))=(13,21)\).

\textbf{Matrix target [derived].}  In the occupied-subset path basis, \(V_2\)
sends \(S\subseteq\{1,2\}\) to \(2S-1\subseteq\{1,2,3,4\}\): the \(\one\)-block
maps \(\emptyset\mapsto\emptyset\), \(\{1,2\}\mapsto\{1,3\}\); the
\(\tau\)-block maps \(\{1\}\mapsto\{1\}\), \(\{2\}\mapsto\{3\}\),
\(\{1,2\}\mapsto\{1,3\}\).  The charge-block matrices are \(13\times2\) and
\(21\times3\) zero-one isometries.  Julia invariants: \(V_c^\dagger V_c=I\)
on both charge blocks, \(P=VV^\dagger\) idempotent self-adjoint, occupation
covariance \(Vn_j=n_{2j-1}V\) and \(n_{2j}V=0\).

\textbf{Birth covariance [derived].}  For the CA-66 adjacent birth
\(b^a_{j,j+1}\),
\[
  V_Lb^a_{j,j+1}=\widetilde b^{\,a}_{2j-1,2j+1}V_L,
\]
where \(\widetilde b\) creates the pair on fine sites \(2j-1,2j+1\) and keeps
fine site \(2j\) in the vacuum.  The naive adjacent-fine-site covariance is
false for this insertion convention; a mutation test must pin this.

\subsection{Comparison and Boundary}

The braiding RG refines dense chains by increasing string density through
braiding; the present family refines a vacuum-capable word algebra by adding
empty sites.  This shard does not claim to evade all rotation obstructions:
it avoids the specific fixed TL semicontinuous Hilbert space of the no-go,
and any future discrete-rotation action on this tower must be checked against
the CA-67 checklist, including the registered Kliesch--Koenig necessary
condition.  The expected algebraic home of the Fibonacci word tower --- a
dilute Temperley--Lieb-type diagram algebra --- and any Koo--Saleur
construction for \emph{dilute} loop models are open research targets; the
dilute-algebra sources are registered but not yet used.  No continuum limit,
Virasoro representation, or category-fidelity theorem is proved here.  The
output is a local, isometric, charge-preserving refinement candidate, its
dressing proposal, and its proof obligations.

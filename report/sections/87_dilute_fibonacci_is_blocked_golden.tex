\subsection{Blocking: When Dilute Is Dense in Disguise}

\textbf{Lemma 87.1 [derived].}  Let \(\Cc\) be a unitary fusion category and
\(O=\one\oplus X\) the site object of (r).  If there is a simple \(Y\) with
\(Y\otimes\bar Y\cong\one\oplus X\), then for every \(L\)
\[
  O^{\otimes L}\;\cong\;(Y\otimes\bar Y)^{\otimes L},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O^{\otimes L})\;\cong\;\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}\big((Y\bar Y)^{\otimes L}\big),
\]
and a nearest-neighbour operator on \(O\)-sites \(j,j+1\) is a range-four
operator on the alternating \(Y\bar Y\) chain, aligned to pairs.  For
self-dual \(Y\) the right-hand side is the dense \(Y\)-chain on \(2L\) sites.

\textbf{Proof.}  Tensor the isomorphism \(O\cong Y\otimes\bar Y\) \(L\) times;
\(\operatorname{End}\) is functorial; a morphism on \(O\otimes O\cong
(Y\bar Y)^{\otimes 2}\) has support on four consecutive \(Y\)-sites.  \(\square\)

\textbf{Instances.}  Fibonacci: \(\tau\otimes\tau=\one\oplus\tau\), so the
dilute \(\one\oplus\tau\) chain of \(L\) sites is the golden chain of \(2L\)
sites.  The dimensions agree: CA-66 gives \(\dim\mathcal H_L=F_{2L+1}\) for the
dilute chain and \(\dim\operatorname{End}(O^{\otimes L})=F_{4L-1}\), which are
the fusion-path count and \(\sum_c m_c^2\) of \(\tau^{\otimes 2L}\).
Ising: \(\sigma\otimes\sigma=\one\oplus\psi\).  \(SU(2)_k\):
\(\tfrac12\otimes\tfrac12=0\oplus1\), so the dilute spin-1 chain is the dense
spin-\(\tfrac12\) chain.  Haagerup \(H_3\) with \(X=\rho\): the sourced rule
\(\rho^2=\one+(\one+a+a^2)\rho\) gives \(d_\rho^2=1+3d_\rho\), so
\(d_\rho=(3+\sqrt{13})/2\) and the invertibles have dimension 1; no simple
\(Y\) has \(d_Y^2=1+d_\rho=(5+\sqrt{13})/2\), so no such \(Y\) exists.

\textbf{Consequence for CA-69/CA-74.}  The correct two-site algebra of the
dilute Fibonacci chain is \(\operatorname{End}(\tau^{\otimes4})\), the Fibonacci
quotient of \(\mathrm{TL}_4(\varphi)\), of dimension 13; the dilute algebra
\(\mathrm{dTL}_2(\varphi)\) of dimension 9 is its parity-preserving subalgebra.
The ``vertex tile outside dTL'' of CA-74 is a Temperley--Lieb generator
acting across a pair boundary.  For Fibonacci there is no net gas beyond
loops: every trivalent history is a loop history of the blocked chain.

\subsection{The Temperley--Lieb Generators in Tile Form}

Write \(\mathrm{TL}_{2L}(\varphi)\), \(e_i^2=\varphi e_i\),
\(e_ie_{i\pm1}e_i=e_i\), on the dilute chain as
\[
  e_{2j-1}=\varphi\,P_0^{(j)}\ (j=1,\dots,L),\qquad
  e_{2j}=F_{j,j+1}\ (j=1,\dots,L-1),
\]
with \(P_0^{(j)}\) the vacancy projector of site \(j\) (the pair
\(\tau_{2j-1}\tau_{2j}\) fused to \(\one\)) and \(F\in\operatorname{End}(O\otimes O)\)
unknown.  In the pair basis \(\{\ket{\one\one},\ket{\tau\tau;\one}\}\oplus
\{\ket{\one\tau},\ket{\tau\one},\ket{\tau\tau;\tau}\}\) of (ad) the relations
\(e_{2j\pm1}e_{2j}e_{2j\pm1}=e_{2j\pm1}\) force the vacancy-diagonal entries
\(\langle\one\one|F|\one\one\rangle=\langle\tau\one|F|\tau\one\rangle=\langle\one\tau|F|\one\tau\rangle=\varphi^{-1}\);
\(F^2=\varphi F\) with reflection symmetry then forces rank one in each block.

\textbf{Theorem 87.2 [derived; checked].}  Up to the sign of the vertex
entry, the unique reflection-symmetric solution is
\[
  F=\varphi\,\ket{u}\!\bra{u}\;\oplus\;\varphi\,\ket{w}\!\bra{w},\qquad
  u=(\varphi^{-1},\ \varphi^{-1/2}),\qquad
  w=(\varphi^{-1},\ \varphi^{-1},\ \pm\varphi^{-3/2}),
\]
with the cup sign \(+\) tied to the \(F\)-gauge of (b).  All Temperley--Lieb
relations, including \([F_{j,j+1},F_{j+1,j+2}]=0\), which involves the
\(F\)-move, hold to machine precision for \(L=3,\dots,6\); the opposite cup
sign fails at \(7.6\times10^{-1}\); a 1\% mutation of the vertex entry fails
at \(3\times10^{-3}\).  Moreover \(H=-\sum_ie_i\) on the dilute chain of
\(L\) sites and the golden chain \(-\varphi\sum_jP_{\one}^{(j,j+1)}\) on
\(2L\) sites have identical dimension \(F_{2L+1}\) and identical spectra:

in both representations, digit for digit (run bundle).  This is Lemma 87.1

\subsection{The Isotropic Net Gas Is the \(Q=\varphi+1\) Potts Model}

\textbf{Proposition 87.3 [derived from sourced identities].}  With the
vertex normalisation (ad), the isotopy-invariant evaluation of a planar
Fibonacci net \(N\) with \(V_3\) trivalent vertices is
\[
  \langle N\rangle=\varphi^{-2}\,\varphi^{\frac34V_3(N)}\,\chi_{\widehat N}(\varphi+1),
\]
\(\chi_{\widehat N}\) the chromatic polynomial of the dual graph.  Hence the
isotropic net gas with edge fugacity \(x\) and vertex fugacity
\(y=\varphi^{-3/4}\) is
\[
  \sum_N x^{E(N)}y^{V_3(N)}\langle N\rangle=\varphi^{-2}\,Z_{\mathrm{Potts}}(Q=\varphi+1,\ K=x),
\]
the low-temperature expansion of the \(Q=\varphi+1\) Potts model with domain
walls on the net lattice and spins on its dual.

\textbf{Proof.}  The sourced formula reads \(\langle G\rangle=\varphi^{-5}\varphi^{\frac32F(G)}\chi_{\widehat G}(\varphi+1)\)
for a connected net, \(F(G)\) its number of faces.  Euler with \(3V_3=2E\)
gives \(F=2+\tfrac12V_3\), so \(\langle G\rangle=\varphi^{-2}\varphi^{\frac34V_3}\chi_{\widehat G}\).
Checks: a loop (\(V_3=0\), \(\chi=Q(Q-1)=\varphi^3\)) gives \(\varphi\); a
theta (\(V_3=2\), \(\chi=Q(Q-1)(Q-2)=\varphi^2\)) gives \(\varphi^{3/2}\),
the theta value of (ad).  For \(k\) components \(\langle N\rangle\) is
multiplicative while \(\widehat N\) is a \(k\)-fold one-point union, so
\(\chi_{\widehat N}=\prod_i\chi_{\widehat N_i}/Q^{k-1}\); the factors
\(\varphi^{-2k}\) and \(Q^{k-1}=\varphi^{2(k-1)}\) combine to \(\varphi^{-2}\).
The sourced low-temperature expansion \(Z_{\mathrm{Potts}}=\sum_NK^{E(N)}\chi_{\widehat N}(Q)\)
then matches term by term at \(y=\varphi^{-3/4}\).  \(\square\)

Since \(Q=\varphi+1<4\), the model has a critical point, in the universality
class of completely packed loops with weight \(\sqrt Q=\varphi\), i.e.\ the
dense branch of CA-86 with \(c=7/10\).  The sourced condition
\(e^{\beta_cJ}-1=\sqrt Q\) is the square-lattice self-dual value; the
triangular-lattice value for honeycomb domain walls needs a local source
(open).  Two remarks.  Chromatic polynomials at non-integer \(Q\) take both
signs, so the net-gas weights are not positive: positivity lives in the
fusion-path basis of the blocked chain, not in the net basis, exactly as the
strange-correlator source warns.  And the critical net gas is, by Lemma 87.1,
the golden chain again, with the Potts critical point as its self-dual point.

\subsection{What This Does to the Principle}

Every calibrated critical point of the Fibonacci \(\one\oplus\tau\) chain is
now a point of the pair-aligned range-four golden chain family:
\[
\begin{array}{l|l|c}
\text{point} & \text{identity} & c\\\hline
\text{dense, antiferromagnetic} & \text{golden chain} & 7/10\\
\text{dense, ferromagnetic} & Z_3\text{ parafermion (sourced)} & 4/5\\
\text{dilute } A_2^{(2)}\text{ branch 1 (CA-86)} & \text{range-four integrable point} & 4/5\\
\text{dilute } A_2^{(2)}\text{ branch 2 (CA-86)} & \text{range-four integrable point} & 7/10\\
\text{Potts / isotropic net gas} & \text{golden chain, self-dual} & 7/10\\
\text{hard-hexagon strange correlator (sourced)} & \text{tau-projected string-net} & 4/5
\end{array}
\]
Two universality classes, both minimal models with Fibonacci defect lines.
For Fibonacci the trivalent vertex adds nothing that Temperley--Lieb does not
already contain, and Intuition~3's ``forced'' creation and annihilation is
the statement that pairs of \(\tau\) fuse to the vacuum.  The net-gas
question is therefore not void in general but void for Fibonacci.

\textbf{Proposal.}  The genuine net case is a category whose site object is
not a product: Haagerup with \(O=\one\oplus\rho\), for which
\(\operatorname{End}(O\otimes O)\) is not a Temperley--Lieb quotient and the
vertex tiles \(\rho\otimes\rho\to\rho\), \(\alpha\rho\), \(\alpha^2\rho\) carry
three independent couplings.  The blocked-chain trick is unavailable, no
integrable line is known, and the isotropic net evaluation is not a
chromatic polynomial.  This is the concrete form of the acid test: the first
computation is the Haagerup analogue of Theorem 87.2, namely whether any
tile combination in \(\operatorname{End}(O\otimes O)\) generates a known
algebra with a critical Hamiltonian, and the second is the lattice
holomorphicity condition of the sweep.  Open items: Julia port of the run
bundle; a local source for the triangular-lattice Potts critical coupling;
and whether the range-four golden-chain family has any critical point
outside \(\{7/10,4/5\}\).

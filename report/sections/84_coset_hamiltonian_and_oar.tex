\subsection{The Raw Finite Zero Mode Is Not Positive}

For $k=1$, fill flavor 1 at every momentum in both copies and leave flavor 2
empty. Let $v$ be this normalized polarized occupation vector. For even $M$
and $0\le R<M$, the raw coset polynomial of CA-83 obeys
\[
 \langle v,C_{M,0,R}v\rangle=-\frac{(M-R)(M-R-1)}{12}.
\]
\textbf{Proof CH-1 [structured calculation].}
\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] In each copy,
$\sum_a\|J_0^a v\|^2=M^2/4+M/2$: the third current has eigenvalue
$M/2$, and each of the other two flips one of $M$ flavors with amplitude
magnitude $1/2$. The two-copy diagonal current has squared norm $M^2+M$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] The zero-mode Sugawara difference is
$2(M^2/4+M/2)/3-(M^2+M)/4=-M(M-1)/12$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle3$.] For $1\le p\le R$, each copy has $M-p$
retained flavor-flip transitions. Counting the two normally ordered terms
gives a coset contribution $(M-p)/6$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle4$.] Sum $\langle1\rangle3$ and add
$\langle1\rangle2$. QED by finite summation.
\end{description}
Thus the sea, whose raw energy is zero, is not a ground state when $R<M-1$.
No diagonalization is needed. Exact checks give
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
M & R=3 & R=4\\\hline
8&-5/3&-1\\16&-13&-11
\end{array}
\]
The witness has auxiliary energy $M^2/2$, so it does not contradict
stabilization on a fixed low-energy subspace as $M$ grows.

\subsection{Computable Vacuum-Descendant Spaces}

\textbf{Construction CH-2.} Let $V_b$ be the span of the vacuum and words
$C_{-n_1}\cdots C_{-n_t}\Omega$ with $n_j\ge1$ and $\sum_jn_j\le b$.
Construct them using the finite CAR polynomials at
\[
 R_b=2b+1,\qquad M_b=16(b+1).
\]
There are finitely many words. At every intermediate grade at most $b$,
CR-2's conditions hold: $R_b>2b$ and
$M_b/2-1/2=8b+15/2>b+2R_b$. Thus the finite vectors agree exactly
with their continuum representatives.

Exact Gram elimination removes linear dependencies. If the retained column
vectors form $U_b$ and $G_b=U_b^*U_b$, then
$P_b=U_bG_b^{-1}U_b^*$ is their orthogonal projector. Refinement identifies
the same words across scales, so the isometries $J_{Bb}:V_b\to V_B$
compose exactly. On these spaces both $E$ and $C_0$ act by total grade.
The grade-zero space is the vacuum line.

The implemented preview through grade three uses
$(\Omega,C_{-2}\Omega,C_{-3}\Omega)$ when present, with exact Gram diagonal
$(1,c/2,2c)$ and grades $(0,2,3)$. It uses the smaller CR-3 vacuum windows
at $M=8,16$, not a large computation of the general schedule.

\textbf{Positive Hamiltonians CH-3.} The ambient operator
\[
 H_b=P_bEP_b+(b+1)(1-P_b)
\]
is positive and has unique vacuum ground state: its first block has
nonnegative grades and one zero vector, while its other block is strictly
positive. This is a different Hamiltonian from the raw quartic zero mode.
Its global projection currently has no proved local fusion-space realization.

\subsection{An Independent Scalar Gives Genuine Unital OAR}

Define $A_b=B(V_b)\oplus\mathbb C$ and, for $B\ge b$,
\[
 \alpha_{Bb}(a,\lambda)=
 \bigl(J_{Bb}aJ_{Bb}^*+\lambda(1-J_{Bb}J_{Bb}^*),\lambda\bigr).
\]
The scalar is independent of $a$; replacing it with a state expectation
would generally preserve only complete positivity, not multiplication.

\textbf{Proof CO-1.}
\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] The two first-coordinate summands have
orthogonal supports. Multiplication and adjoints are preserved, and
$(1,1)$ maps to $(1,1)$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] Compressing by $J_{Bb}^*$ recovers $a$;
the second coordinate recovers $\lambda$. The map is injective.
\item[$\langle1\rangle3$.] The complementary projection splits into
the newly added shell and the transported previous shell. Substitution
proves $\alpha_{DB}\alpha_{Bb}=\alpha_{Db}$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle4$.] The states
$\omega_b(a,\lambda)=\langle\Omega,a\Omega\rangle$ are compatible.
Their Wilson rows are constant. The norm inductive limit is
$K(\mathcal H_{\rm vac})+\mathbb C1$, and the vacuum GNS is
$\mathcal H_{\rm vac}=\overline{\bigcup_bV_b}$, since finite-rank operators
act cyclically on $\Omega$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle5$.] QED by $\langle1\rangle1$--$\langle1\rangle4$.
\end{description}
The finite Hamiltonian $(E|_{V_b},b+1)$ is positive. Its dynamics intertwine
exactly: the old subspace and the new shell are energy-reducing, and the
embedded coarse algebra is scalar on the new shell. The Hamiltonians
themselves need not intertwine.

\subsection{What the Construction Does Not Identify}

This is a new constrained observable prescription. It is generally not
the restriction of the original unital CAR refinement, which retains all
new-mode multiplicities. The new map instead inserts the selected sea
when it transports the matrix block.

Likewise the ambient penalized resolvents tend to
$P(E+i)^{-1}P$, where $P$ projects onto the limiting vacuum module. This
has a kernel on the discarded ambient sectors and is not a self-adjoint
resolvent on that entire ambient Hilbert space. The new vacuum GNS
representation is essential.

Global constraints can be intrinsic fusion constraints. Their presence is
not an obstruction to locality. What remains unproved is the identification
of these descendant spaces with categorical fusion spaces, together with a
local realization of the Hamiltonian and refinement operations on them.

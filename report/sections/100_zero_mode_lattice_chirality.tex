 &+e^S_{k,n,s}C^L_{m,s+1}+C^S_{n,s}e^L_{k,m,s+1}\big)\longrightarrow0.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Telescope the words \(LS\), \(SL\), replacing one letter at a time; A1 transports each

\subsection{Parity-Free Balance on the Common Domain}

\textbf{Lemma 100.2 [conditional; full; category-free; \(\mathsf R2\) restriction] (NOTE:149--219; REVIEW:117--125).}
\textbf{Assume} common A1--A4 for the standard KS families. Put \(C_{mn}=[L_m,S_n]\), \(X_q=C_{q0}\),

\begin{equation}\label{eq:100-mixed}
 C_{mn}-C_{nm}=\delta_{m+n,0}\gamma_m I,\qquad

\end{equation}
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). At finite scale \([E_m,E_n]=\kappa_{mn}B_{m+n}\),
\(\kappa_{mn}=-ia_N(z^n-z^m)\) (79.1). Subtract the internal brackets from \([L_m+S_m,L_n+S_n]\):
\(C^{(r)}_{mn}-C^{(r)}_{nm}=(4\kappa_{mn}-2(n-m))B_{r,m+n}-F^L_{r,mn}-F^R_{r,-m,-n}
+\delta_{m+n,0}\gamma_m I\), where \(F^L,F^R\) are the unbarred/barred A4 residuals.
The coefficient has modulus \(\le2\pi(m^2+n^2)/N\); A2 for \(B=(S-L)/2\), A4 and word convergence give the first identity.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). L-Jacobi and cyclic summation give
\((m-n)C_{m+n,k}+(n-k)C_{n+k,m}+(k-m)C_{k+m,n}=0\): the leftover commutators have scalar arguments.

\((m-n)(X_{m+n}-C_{mn})-n\delta_{m+n,0}\gamma_m I=0\), giving the second identity off the diagonal.
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). For \(n\ne0\), the triple \((p,n-p,n)\), \(p\notin\{0,n,n/2\}\), gives

\textbf{Negative 100.5 [negative; full; \(\mathsf L0\); trivial \(\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:102--147; REVIEW:102--115).}

and complex-linear bond-centred reflection \(P_r\). All F2(a) hypotheses hold and ZC is identically zero,
but every self-adjoint logarithm \(e^{2\pi ip_r/N_r}=U_r\) satisfies \(|D_r-p_r|_{s,1}\ge N_r/2\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Unit multiplicities give the carrier for any \(\Cc\) (Penneys:219--237,442);

\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). The one-excitation basis obeys \(Ue_j=e_{j+1}\); its Fourier vectors include phase \(-1\).
Every logarithm has an eigenvalue in \(N(1/2+\mathbb Z)\) on that block. Since \(D=0,K=I\), the bound follows. QED.
This refutes automatic current--logarithm identification, not ZC. Only a \emph{spectral function} \(p(U)\) automatically commutes with every operator commuting with \(U\); arbitrary logarithms on degenerate blocks need not.

\textbf{Negative 100.6 [negative; full Fourier grading; category-free; formal, no Hilbert \(\mathsf R2\)] (NOTE:365--453; REVIEW:150--161).}
There is a formal \(*\)-Lie algebra satisfying Jacobi, dagger, linear parity and spatial grading with \(X_0\ne0\).
Its vector space is \(\Span\{L_n,S_n:n\in\mathbb Z\}\oplus V\), \(V=\bigoplus_q\mathbb C[t]X_q\), finite sums only; \(V\) is abelian. Define zero-central internal brackets as above and
\[
 [L_m,S_n]=X_{m+n},\quad [L_p,f(t)X_q]=(t+p)f(t+p)X_{q+p},\quad
 [S_p,f(t)X_q]=(t+q)f(t)X_{q+p}.

\(L_n^\dagger=L_{-n}\), \(S_n^\dagger=S_{-n}\), \((f(t)X_q)^\dagger=-\overline f(-t)X_{-q}\), where the bar
conjugates coefficients; \(RL_n=S_{-n}\), \(RS_n=L_{-n}\), \(R(f(t)X_q)=-f(t-q)X_{-q}\), with \(R\) linear.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). On \(fX_q\), the L-action commutator has coefficient

\subsection{Commutation Range, Cut Current and Spatial Support}

\textbf{Proposition 100.7 [derived; periodic; category-free; finite] (NOTE:455--504; REVIEW:163--175).}

\subsection{Corner Weight to Wilson Drift}

\textbf{Proposition 76.1 [derived; D6 P2 = D3-1, found independently; confirmed].}  \textsc{Assume} \(W\) is an isometry
implementing \(\theta:\Alg\to\mathcal B\) between finite multi-matrix algebras, \(P=WW^\dagger\), \(\sigma\) a state on
\(\mathcal B\), \(p=\sigma(P)>0\), \(q=1-p\), \(\tau=\sigma\theta/p\), \(\rho\) a state on \(\Alg\).  \textsc{Prove}
\begin{align*}
 &\text{(i) } \|\sigma\theta-\rho\|\le p\|\tau-\rho\|+q, \qquad
  \text{(ii) } \|\sigma\Phi_\chi-\rho\|\le p\|\tau-\rho\|+2q,\\
 &\text{(iii) if } \alpha \text{ is a unital } *\text{-map with } \alpha(a)W=Wa\ \forall a:\
  \|\sigma\alpha-\rho\|\le p\|\tau-\rho\|+2q,\\
 &\text{(iv) if each } \alpha_n \text{ intertwines } W_n:\ d_{k,n}\le p_ne_n+2q_n,\qquad
  q_n=\delta_n^2-\tfrac14\delta_n^4,\ \ 2q_n=(1+\sqrt{p_n})\,\delta_n^2\le2\delta_n^2 .
\end{align*}
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). \(\sigma\theta=p\tau\), so \(\sigma\theta-\rho=p(\tau-\rho)-q\rho\); the triangle inequality gives (i).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Lemma~72.6: \(\sigma\Phi_\chi=p\tau+q\chi\); subtract \(\rho=p\rho+q\rho\), use \(\|\chi-\rho\|\le2\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Intertwining for \(a\) and \(a^*\) gives \(P\alpha(a)=WaW^\dagger=\alpha(a)P\), so
\(\alpha(a)=\theta(a)+(1-P)\alpha(a)(1-P)\) and \(\sigma\alpha=p\tau+q\eta\), \(\eta\) a state if \(q>0\); conclude as in
\(\langle1\rangle2\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). \(\omega_{n+1}\alpha^k_{n+1}-\omega_n\alpha^k_n=(\omega_{n+1}\alpha_n-\omega_n)\alpha^k_n\), and
pullback along a unital \(*\)-map is contractive; apply \(\langle1\rangle3\) with \(\sigma=\omega_{n+1}\),
\(\rho=\omega_n\).  The identities follow from \(\sqrt{p_n}=1-\delta_n^2/2\).  \(\square\)

The bound is attained (\(p=9/25\), drift \(2q=32/25\): D6 checks \path{raw_output.txt}:5).  No function of \(q_n\)
alone bounds \(d_{k,n}\): even \(p_n=1\) leaves \(e_n\) free.  \textbf{Caution [D6 REVIEW:155--161].}  Since \(P_n\) pins
every inserted site, \(\sum_nq_n<\infty\) is a \emph{global} fidelity demand of exact-RG strength on an extensive chain.

\textbf{Proposition 76.2 [derived; D3-1 (2.2)--(2.3), generalising CA-84; confirmed].}  For any isometries \(W_n\),
\(\hat\Alg_n=\Alg_n\oplus\mathbb C\), \(\hat\alpha_n(a,z)=(\theta_n(a)+z(1-P_n),z)\), \(\hat\omega_n(a,z)=\omega_n(a)\)
define a strict unital injective \(*\)-tower (cross terms vanish; composites obey
\(\theta^n_m(1-P^k_n)+(1-P^n_m)=1-P^k_m\)) with exact adjacent drift
\(\hat D_n=\|p_n\tau_n-\omega_n\|+q_n\in[\,2q_n,\ p_ne_n+2q_n\,]\) (the dual norm of a direct sum is the sum).  If
\(\omega_n(P^k_n)\to0\) for each fixed \(k\), the limit on row \(k\) is the character \((a,z)\mapsto z\) (\emph{scalar
collapse}).  \textbf{Boundary:} \(\hat\alpha_n\) is generally nonlocal through \(P_n\); for it, as for a bare corner,
every local approximant \(b_B\) of \(\theta(a)\) has \(\|\theta(a)-b_B\|\ge\|a\|/2\) (D3 REVIEW:60--61).

\textbf{Proposition 76.3 [derived; D6 P1; confirmed].}  \textsc{Assume} \(\Alg=\bigoplus_aM_{d_a}\),
\(\mathcal B=\bigoplus_bM_{e_b}\), \(\theta\) an injective corner \(*\)-map with \(\theta(1)=P\) in which block \(a\) occurs
in block \(b\) with multiplicity \(r_{ba}\).  \textsc{Prove}: a unital \(*\)-map \(\alpha\) with \(P\alpha(x)P=\theta(x)\),
\([P,\alpha(x)]=0\) exists iff there are integers \(q_{ba}\ge0\) with \(e_b-\sum_ar_{ba}d_a=\sum_aq_{ba}d_a\) for all \(b\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Diagonal matrix units of \(M_d\) map to equal-rank projections, so every representation has
dimension in \(d\,\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\); restricting \(\alpha\) to \((1-P)\mathcal B(1-P)\) blockwise gives the condition.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Conversely, \(q_{ba}\) defining copies on the complement give \(\beta\) with \(\beta(1)=1-P\);
set \(\alpha=\theta+\beta\).  \(\square\)  By \(\langle1\rangle3\) of 76.1 every \(\alpha\) intertwining \(W\) is such an
extension; block ranks, hence the condition, are invariant under unitary dressing.

\textbf{Corollary 76.4 [derived; reviewer remark R2, D6 REVIEW:142--151].}  For vacuum insertion
\(\Alg_L\to\Alg_{2L}\) the condition of 76.3 holds for \emph{every} \(\Cc\).  \emph{Proof.}  Block \(c\) maps to block
\(c\) with \(r_{cc}=1\); with \(m_c(L)=\dim\Hom(c,O^{\otimes L})\) and \(O^{\otimes2L}\cong O^{\otimes L}\otimes
O^{\otimes L}\) the complement is \(m_c(2L)-m_c(L)=\sum_a(N^c_{a,O^{\otimes L}}-\delta_{ac})m_a(L)\), with nonnegative
coefficients because \(\one\subset O^{\otimes L}\).  \(\square\)  So the arithmetic never obstructs vacuum insertion;
the open requirement is \textbf{locality} (and coherence) of the unital lift.

\subsection{Dynamics: Vacuum Residual and Gap}

\textbf{Theorem 76.6 [conditional; D3-4; proof checked, grade conditional-ok].}  \textsc{Assume} \(K_n\ge0\)
(\(K_n=H_n-E_n\), \(E_n\) a certified ground energy) with unit \(\Omega_n\), \(K_n\Omega_n=0\),
\(\omega_n=\langle\Omega_n,\cdot\,\Omega_n\rangle\); a unique gapped fine vacuum in the declared comparison carrier,
\(K_{n+1}\ge\Delta_{n+1}(1-\ket{\Omega_{n+1}}\bra{\Omega_{n+1}})\); a residual
\(\|(K_{n+1}W_n-\lambda_nW_nK_n)\Omega_n\|\le\epsilon_n\), \(\lambda_n>0\); and either coherent \(\alpha_n\) intertwining
\(W_n\) or the tower of 76.2.  \textsc{Prove} \(d_{k,n}\le\min(2,2\epsilon_n/\Delta_{n+1})\) and
\(q_n\le\min(1,(\epsilon_n/\Delta_{n+1})^2)\) on every row; hence \(\sum_n\epsilon_n/\Delta_{n+1}<\infty\) gives WT-1 A3.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Put \(\psi=W_n\Omega_n\), \(Q=1-\ket{\Omega_{n+1}}\bra{\Omega_{n+1}}\).  As \(K_n\Omega_n=0\) the
residual is \(K_{n+1}\psi=K_{n+1}Q\psi\), so \(\|Q\psi\|\le\epsilon_n/\Delta_{n+1}\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Two pure states with overlap modulus \(u\) are at distance \(2\sqrt{1-u^2}=2\|Q\psi\|\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). The trial state \(\sigma_n=\langle\psi,\cdot\,\psi\rangle\) obeys \(\sigma_n\alpha_n=\omega_n\) (and
\(\hat\sigma_n\hat\alpha_n=\hat\omega_n\) for \(\hat\sigma_n(b,z)=\sigma_n(b)\), as \(\psi\in\Ran P_n\)); pullback is contractive.
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). \(\psi\in\Ran P_n\) gives \(|\langle\Omega_{n+1},\psi\rangle|^2\le p_n\), so
\(q_n\le\|Q\psi\|^2\).  \(\square\)
\par Energy variant: \(E^{\rm tr}_n=\langle\psi,K_{n+1}\psi\rangle\) gives \(d_{k,n}\le2\sqrt{E^{\rm tr}_n/\Delta_{n+1}}\),
\(q_n\le E^{\rm tr}_n/\Delta_{n+1}\); the overlap inequality is source-local (\path{latticefermions.tex}:487--491).
Degenerate ground spaces need a selected sector; zero variance does not select the ground state (D3 checks
\path{output.txt}:13).

\textbf{Corollary 76.7 [conditional; reviewer non-collapse corollary, D3 REVIEW:65--68].}  Under 76.6 let
\(s_k=\sqrt2\sum_{j\ge k}\epsilon_j/\Delta_{j+1}\le\sqrt2\).  Then \(\omega_n(P^k_n)\ge(1-s_k^2/2)^2\) for all \(n>k\), so
scalar collapse (76.2) cannot occur, and D1-T5b's sufficient condition \(\sum_n\sqrt{1-p_n}<\infty\) (D1 NOTE:383--385)
holds.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Fix phases with \(c=\langle\Omega_{j+1},W_j\Omega_j\rangle\ge0\); then
\(\|W_j\Omega_j-\Omega_{j+1}\|^2=2-2c\le2(1-c^2)\le2(\epsilon_j/\Delta_{j+1})^2\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Telescoping through isometries, \(\|W^k_n\Omega_k-\Omega_n\|\le s_k\); so
\(\omega_n(P^k_n)\ge(\mathrm{Re}\langle W^k_n\Omega_k,\Omega_n\rangle)^2\ge(1-s_k^2/2)^2\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). \(\sqrt{q_n}\le\epsilon_n/\Delta_{n+1}\) by 76.6.  \(\square\)

\textbf{Remark 76.8 [derived arithmetic on conditional inputs; D3 (4.3), (5.1)].}  If \(\Delta_n\ge c\,a_n^z\) and
\(\epsilon_n\le Ca_n^{z+y}\), \(y>0\), \(a_n=2^{-n}\), then \(d_{k,n}\le C'2^{-yn}\), a geometric tail.  A circuit-conjugacy
comparison with at most \(cN_{n+1}\) mismatch terms of norm \(\eta_n\le Ca_n^u\) gives \(\epsilon_n\le cN_{n+1}\eta_n\)
plus an energy-origin cost \(2\zeta_n\); summability needs \(u>1+z\).  Dyadic spacing does not sum \(O(1)\) or
\(O(1/n)\) per-step errors.

\subsection{Polar Heat Refinement}

\textbf{Proposition 76.9 [derived; D3-5; confirmed, also on a constrained multiplicity-two carrier].}  \textsc{Assume}
finite \(L\), \(H=\sum_Zh_Z\) Hermitian categorical interval terms on the fine open chain, \(V=V_L\), \(P=VV^\dagger\),
\(P_Z\) the product of vacancy projectors on inserted sites in \(Z\), \(G(t)=V^\dagger e^{-2tH}V\) and
\(W(t)=e^{-tH}V\,G(t)^{-1/2}\).  \textsc{Prove} \(W(0)=V\), \(W(t)^\dagger W(t)=1\),
\(\dot W(0)=-(1-P)HV=\sum_Z[P_Z,h_Z]\,V\) with each \([P_Z,h_Z]\) anti-Hermitian, supported in \(Z\), of norm
\(\le2\|h_Z\|\), and \(\tfrac{d}{dt}W^\dagger HW|_{0}=-2V^\dagger H(1-P)HV\le0\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). \(e^{-tH}\) is invertible, so \(G>0\) and \(W^\dagger W=G^{-1/2}GG^{-1/2}=1\) in every charge block.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). \(\dot G(0)=-2V^\dagger HV\) and \(\tfrac{d}{dt}G^{-1/2}|_0=V^\dagger HV\), so \(\dot W(0)=-HV+PHV\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). \(h_Z\) acts trivially on inserted sites outside \(Z\): \(Ph_ZV=P_Zh_ZV\) and \(P_ZV=V\), hence
\([P_Z,h_Z]V=-(1-P)h_ZV\).  No braiding and no Hilbert tensor factorisation is used.
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). Differentiating \(W^\dagger HW\), both terms equal \(-X^\dagger X\), \(X=(1-P)HV\).  \(\square\)
\par Supplements [derived; confirmed]: \(V^\dagger h_Z(1-P)h_YV=0\) unless \(Z,Y\) share an inserted site; a local tangent
\(D=\sum_Ix_IV\) with \(V^\dagger D+D^\dagger V=0\) extends to anti-Hermitian
\(a_I=x_IP_I-P_Ix_I^\dagger-\tfrac12P_I(x_I-x_I^\dagger)P_I\) supported in \(I\), \(\|a_I\|\le3\|x_I\|\),
\(\sum_Ia_IV=D\) (D3 (6.3)).  For \(t>0\), \(\dot W=-(1-P_t)HW+WK_{\rm frame}\) with \(P_t=WW^\dagger\) not a product of
local projectors and \(K_{\rm frame}\) generally nonzero (D3 (6.2)): this is the all-orders difficulty.

\(\operatorname{diam}Z\le R\), \(\sup_x\sum_{Z\ni x}\|h_{L,Z}\|\le J\); no gap, uniqueness or translation invariance.

expansion is the plausible route, but no such source is local, so nothing is asserted.  D3-C is a \emph{UV locality

zero summably for a critical vacuum.  First attackable lemma: a locally summable generator for the \(t^2\) coefficient,
via (6.3) and pair locality.

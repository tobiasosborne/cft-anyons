\subsection{Files}

\texttt{inputs.toml} records the Pauli basis, indexing rule, family inventory,
source-kind inventory, and sample count.  \texttt{summary.toml} records verdict
counts and notable sourced points.  \texttt{results.toml} records one row per
sample, including coefficients, parameters, terminal gate, residual norms, and
scope.

99 samples, 9 current-collapsed, 27 conservation failures, and 63 exact
boost-witness failures.

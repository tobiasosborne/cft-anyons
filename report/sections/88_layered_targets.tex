\subsection{Three Axes}

Throughout, \(\Cc\) is a unitary fusion category.  Microscopic choices (site object \(O\), tiles \(h\in\operatorname{End}_\Cc
(O\otimes O)\), boundary and seam, refinements, states; D5's E5, NOTE.md:124--127) are \emph{declarations at every
layer}, not a data layer: they are part of a construction, not of its input.

\textbf{Definition 88.1 [proposal; setting axis \(\mathsf S\)].}
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{ch}}\): an \emph{open} chain with declared endpoints, targeting a \emph{chiral} conformal net \(\Alg\)
(one stress family).  Fidelity means: the sector category \(\mathrm{DHR}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Alg)\) is braided equivalent to a
declared chiral UMTC \(\mathsf B\) (\(\mathsf B=\Cc\) for modular \(\Cc\)), as in Theorem~80.9.
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\): a \emph{periodic} chain targeting a full two-dimensional local net with chiral subnets
\(\Alg_L,\Alg_R\), two stress families (\(\Vir\times\overline{\Vir}\)) and a declared gluing (extension; for the diagonal
case the Cardy invariant \(M=1\), D5 P6).  Fidelity means: the lattice \(\Cc\)-symmetry, i.e.\ the periodic defect
operators that commute with the transfer matrix and fuse by \(N_{ab}^c\) (AFM-D:163--212), converges into the full
theory's symmetry (defect) data, and the declared identifications hold: \(\Rep(\Alg_L)\simeq\Cc\) for modular \(\Cc\) (D6
REVIEW:94--98), with two-chiral bulk data \(\Cc\boxtimes\overline{\Cc}\simeq Z(\Cc)\) (Bischoff:661--664), the sector category of the
doubled chiral net or the centre of the defect category \(\Cc\), \emph{not} the DHR category of the full diagonal extension,
which is trivial (Corollary 93.9, Proposition 93.10); for fusion-only
\(\Cc\) the braided target is \(Z(\Cc)\), with \(\Cc\) recovered as defects/solitons (Bischoff:384--397).
\emph{Warning (PLAN.md:244--247):} the periodic lattice algebra's scaling limit is the interchiral algebra, not
\(\Vir\times\overline{\Vir}\); the open/periodic choice is part of the setting, not a boundary detail.
\emph{Source gap (Law~1):} no local source settles whether a 1D local tower can have a chiral limit with \(c\neq0\)
(\(c_L\neq c_R\)); the nearest is 1903.12334:76 (D6 REVIEW:86--90).  \emph{Delineation (CA-91; full; category-free;
\(\mathsf R2\) [cond.]):} a complex-\emph{linear} unitary parity commuting with the control, with both standard KS families
VR-1-certified on one system, forces \(c_L=c_R\), \(c^*_L=c^*_R\) (Theorem 91.1) and excludes an absent right stress
(Corollary 91.10); an antiunitary (dagger-type) reflection preserves each chirality and constrains nothing (Remark 91.4).
Parity-odd perturbations exist iff the total current is nonzero: never on the fixed carrier \(O=\one\) (Negative 91.12),
always after \(O\otimes(\one\oplus\one)\) (91.13) [\(\mathsf L0\), finite].  The general lattice-chirality question remains a
source gap, so \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{ch}}\) is neither excluded nor established; it is a declared tag.

\textbf{Definition 88.2 [proposal; data ladder \(\mathsf L\)].}
\(\mathsf L0\) \emph{fusion}: \(\Irr\Cc\), \(N_{ab}^c\), \(F\), dagger, pivotal/spherical structure, \(d_a>0\).
\(\mathsf L1\) \emph{braided}: in addition \(R\) obeying the hexagons.
\(\mathsf L2\) \emph{modular}: \(R\) nondegenerate (Bischoff:655--658); hence \(S\), twists \(\theta_i\), and \(c\bmod 8\) (EG:334).
\(\mathsf L3\) \emph{enriched} \(\mathsf E\): a real lift \(c\) (\(c_L,c_R\) in \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\)), weights \(h_i\) with
\(e^{2\pi ih_i}=\theta_i\), and characters or a finite presentation of the vacuum chiral algebra (D5 E2--E3,
NOTE.md:107--118; D6 E.energy/E.fields, NOTE.md:14--17); in \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\) also the sewing data E4
(NOTE.md:119--123).  \(\mathsf L4\) \emph{declared target theory}: an effective sewing completion \(\mathcal S\) (D4-C's input,
D4 NOTE.md:11--21) or a sourced rigorous net (CA-82).
The ladder is not fully linear: for fusion-only \(\Cc\), \(\mathsf L0\) already determines the modular \(Z(\Cc)\)
(Bischoff:384--386), which is why \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\) reaches modular-type targets from fusion input, whereas
\(\mathsf L3\) is never determined by \(\mathsf L2\): \(c\) only mod 8, and tensoring with a holomorphic net preserves the
category (Bischoff:374--381; \path{realisation_and_fidelity.md}:39--44).

separately obtained \(\mathsf R1\) and \(\mathsf R2\) certificates do not compose.

\subsection{The Six Headline Conjectures, Restated}
\label{sec:layered-conjectures}

Each entry gives the tags \((\mathsf S;\mathsf L;\) rung reached \(/\) rung claimed\()\) of the headline \emph{as written},
then a restatement [proposal] at the most general layer where it is plausibly true, the first attackable lemma, and
the next layer's demand.  All six were graded ``needs reformulation'' (INTEGRATION.md \S(d)).

\textbf{D1-C} (open; \(\mathsf L0\) + declared reaction morphisms and contact tuning; carrier (77.8) plus an off-ladder
\(z{=}2\) resolvent limit reached (CA-94) \(/\) a \(z=2\) resolvent limit, off the conformal ladder).  \emph{Restated (\(\mathsf L0\), any, \(\mathsf R0^u+\mathsf R1\)):}
on the pointed configuration carrier of 77.8, for any unitary fusion \(\Cc\) and \(O=\one\oplus X\), there are coherent
local unital refinements of a number-changing field algebra compatible with one state family satisfying WT-1
(D1 INTEGRATION:102--105; CA-77 open problem).  The collision-sewing version is now \textbf{derived} for the tuned family
(CA-94, Theorem 94.6), and the tuning is necessary: a fixed pure-hop density has no full-Fock limit after any scalar
subtraction (Proposition 94.1); hard-core hopping gives Dirichlet collisions and decoupled sectors (CA-77 \S Conjecture).
The former first lemma (collision-trace bound for \(N\le2\), INTEGRATION:107--111) is superseded by Lemma 94.5 for all \(N\).
\emph{First lemma (restated target, open):} a unital local lift of the dressed refinement \(R=U(\mathrm{id}\otimes\eta)\)
(CA-77:209--212) commuting with \(\hat N\) (E6 INTEGRATION:87--89; CA-90 gives the bare-insertion version).  \emph{Next layer:} \(\mathsf R2\) needs KS/Ward data D1
does not supply; \(\mathsf R4\) needs braided/ribbon marking of the tower (\(\mathsf L1\)/\(\mathsf L2\); INTEGRATION (e)7, (e)9).

\textbf{D2-C} (full; \(\mathsf L0\) + declared certificate C1--C6; none reached \(/\) \(\mathsf R3\)).  Split in two.
\emph{(i) Ward-to-KS transfer lemma (category-free, full, \(\to\mathsf R2\)):} the operator lift W2 of the discrete contour
identity, plus A2 for an admissible control and differentiated, coordinate-resolved matching, implies VR-1 A3/A4 for
the standard KS modes of \(k\) on the same GNS core (Theorem 89.1); A3 for arbitrary gate-intertwining refinements is
false when \(c^*\neq0\) (Proposition 89.7).  \emph{(ii) Selection proper:} which \(h\in\operatorname{End}_\Cc(O\otimes O)\) carries a certificate; at
\(\mathsf L0\) it is category-blind (\(\Cc=\mathrm{Vec}\) admits every qudit chain, D2 REVIEW:79) and remains a question.
\emph{First lemma:} the probe-word Ward-to-residual Lemma 89.L, tested first on the in-repo free-fermion benchmark with
\(K=1+E_N\) (D2 INTEGRATION:117--121).
\emph{Next layer:} chiral-sector equality with \(\Cc\) is not in D2-C (NOTE.md:40--45); it is \(\mathsf R4\) at
\(\mathsf L2\), plus a \(c\) lift at \(\mathsf L3\) (INTEGRATION (e)13).

\textbf{D3-C} (open; \(\mathsf L0\), pointed \(O\); first order reached by 76.9 \(/\) uniform locality, \(\to\mathsf R0\)).
\emph{Restated (\(\mathsf L0\), open, \(\to\mathsf R0\)):} a UV locality lemma: the polar heat refinement has a
quasi-local generator uniformly in \(L\) for \(0\le t\le t_0\) (Conjecture 76.10 as stated).  The IR question is
separate and is the dichotomy 76.12: (G) global fidelity at rate \(o(a_n^{1+z})\), or (L) a local unital lift with

\textbf{D4-C} (full, diagonal; \(\mathsf L4\); \(\to\mathsf R2\) by 79.2 [cond.] \(/\) \(\mathsf R4\)).  \emph{Restated, relative
(\(\mathsf L4\), full, \(\mathsf R4\)):} given a modular \(\Cc\) and a supplied effective completion \(\mathcal S\), a

\emph{Attackable core (\(\mathsf L0\), any, \(\to\mathsf R2\)):} the shell certificate P0--P3 of Theorem 79.2.  \emph{First
lemma:} P0--P3 for the sourced quasi-free calibration (INTEGRATION:103--111).  \emph{Next layer:} complete sector
correspondence and braided equivalence with \(\Cc\) (INTEGRATION (e)12).

\textbf{D6-C} (chiral; \(\mathsf L2\) input, selects \(\mathsf L3\); none \(/\) \(\mathsf R5\)).  \emph{Restated} as three

\subsection{How to Read a Statement under the Layering Principle}

Every statement carries three tags: \((\mathsf S;\ \mathsf L;\ \mathsf R)\), with \(\mathsf S\in\{\text{ch},\text{full},
\text{any}\}\), \(\mathsf L\in\{\text{none},\mathsf L0,\dots,\mathsf L4\}\) and \(\mathsf R\) the rung it \emph{reaches}; a
conjecture also states the rung it \emph{claims}.  Microscopic declarations (\(O\), \(h\), boundary, refinements, states)
are listed beside the tags.  A statement that silently fixes a layer (one chirality, one enrichment, one target) is
incomplete until it names the layer and the data (AGENTS.md:113--115).  Two working rules follow:
state the result at the lowest \(\mathsf L\) and broadest \(\mathsf S\) at which its proof runs, and record the extra
data the next rung needs as part of the statement, as the restatements above do.

\subsection{Three Axes}

Throughout, \(\Cc\) is a unitary fusion category.  Microscopic choices (site object \(O\), tiles \(h\in\operatorname{End}_\Cc
(O\otimes O)\), boundary and seam, refinements, states; D5's E5, NOTE.md:124--127) are \emph{declarations at every
layer}, not a data layer: they are part of a construction, not of its input.

\textbf{Definition 88.1 [proposal; setting axis \(\mathsf S\)].}
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{ch}}\): an \emph{open} chain with declared endpoints, targeting a \emph{chiral} conformal net \(\Alg\)
(one stress family).  Fidelity means: the sector category \(\mathrm{DHR}_{\mathrm{fin}}(\Alg)\) is braided equivalent to a
declared chiral UMTC \(\mathsf B\) (\(\mathsf B=\Cc\) for modular \(\Cc\)), as in Theorem~80.9.
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\): a \emph{periodic} chain targeting a full two-dimensional local net with chiral subnets
\(\Alg_L,\Alg_R\), two stress families (\(\Vir\times\overline{\Vir}\)) and a declared gluing (extension; for the diagonal
case the Cardy invariant \(M=1\), D5 P6).  Fidelity means: the lattice \(\Cc\)-symmetry, i.e.\ the periodic defect
operators that commute with the transfer matrix and fuse by \(N_{ab}^c\) (AFM-D:163--212), converges into the full
theory's symmetry (defect) data, and the declared identifications hold: \(\Rep(\Alg_L)\simeq\Cc\) for modular \(\Cc\) (D6
REVIEW:94--98), with two-chiral bulk data \(\Cc\boxtimes\overline{\Cc}\simeq Z(\Cc)\) (Bischoff:661--664); for fusion-only
\(\Cc\) the braided target is \(Z(\Cc)\), with \(\Cc\) recovered as defects/solitons (Bischoff:384--397).
\emph{Warning (PLAN.md:244--247):} the periodic lattice algebra's scaling limit is the interchiral algebra, not
\(\Vir\times\overline{\Vir}\); the open/periodic choice is part of the setting, not a boundary detail.
\emph{Source gap (Law~1):} no local source settles whether a 1D local tower can have a chiral limit with \(c\neq0\)
(\(c_L\neq c_R\)); the nearest is 1903.12334:76 (D6 REVIEW:86--90).  So \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{ch}}\) is neither excluded nor
established; it is a declared tag.

\textbf{Definition 88.2 [proposal; data ladder \(\mathsf L\)].}
\(\mathsf L0\) \emph{fusion}: \(\Irr\Cc\), \(N_{ab}^c\), \(F\), dagger, pivotal/spherical structure, \(d_a>0\).
\(\mathsf L1\) \emph{braided}: in addition \(R\) obeying the hexagons.
\(\mathsf L2\) \emph{modular}: \(R\) nondegenerate (Bischoff:655--658); hence \(S\), twists \(\theta_i\), and \(c\bmod 8\) (EG:334).
\(\mathsf L3\) \emph{enriched} \(\mathsf E\): a real lift \(c\) (\(c_L,c_R\) in \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\)), weights \(h_i\) with
\(e^{2\pi ih_i}=\theta_i\), and characters or a finite presentation of the vacuum chiral algebra (D5 E2--E3,
NOTE.md:107--118; D6 E.energy/E.fields, NOTE.md:14--17); in \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\) also the sewing data E4
(NOTE.md:119--123).  \(\mathsf L4\) \emph{declared target theory}: an effective sewing completion \(\mathcal S\) (D4-C's input,
D4 NOTE.md:11--21) or a sourced rigorous net (CA-82).
The ladder is not fully linear: for fusion-only \(\Cc\), \(\mathsf L0\) already determines the modular \(Z(\Cc)\)
(Bischoff:384--386), which is why \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\) reaches modular-type targets from fusion input, whereas
\(\mathsf L3\) is never determined by \(\mathsf L2\): \(c\) only mod 8, and tensoring with a holomorphic net preserves the
category (Bischoff:374--381; \path{realisation_and_fidelity.md}:39--44).

the \emph{same} refinement, state and representation system (CA-81, ``same state and representation system'');
separately obtained \(\mathsf R1\) and \(\mathsf R2\) certificates do not compose.

\subsection{The Six Headline Conjectures, Restated}

\textbf{D1-C} (open; \(\mathsf L0\) + declared reaction morphisms and analytic hypotheses; only the carrier
(77.8) reached \(/\) a \(z=2\) resolvent limit, off the conformal ladder).  \emph{Restated (\(\mathsf L0\), any, \(\mathsf R0^u+\mathsf R1\)):}
on the pointed configuration carrier of 77.8, for any unitary fusion \(\Cc\) and \(O=\one\oplus X\), there are coherent
local unital refinements of a number-changing field algebra compatible with one state family satisfying WT-1
(D1 INTEGRATION:102--105; CA-77 open problem).  The collision-sewing version stays a conjecture with its caveat: the
limit exists only with the tuned contact term \(-2\varepsilon^{-2}\sum n_jn_{j+1}\); hard-core hopping gives Dirichlet
collisions and decoupled sectors (CA-77 \S Conjecture).  \emph{First lemma:} the discrete collision-trace lower bound
for \(N\in\{0,1,2\}\) with general \(b,v\) (INTEGRATION:107--111).  \emph{Next layer:} \(\mathsf R2\) needs KS/Ward data D1
does not supply; \(\mathsf R4\) needs braided/ribbon marking of the tower (\(\mathsf L1\)/\(\mathsf L2\); INTEGRATION (e)7, (e)9).

\subsection{How to Read a Statement under the Layering Principle}

Every statement carries three tags: \((\mathsf S;\ \mathsf L;\ \mathsf R)\), with \(\mathsf S\in\{\text{ch},\text{full},
\text{any}\}\), \(\mathsf L\in\{\text{none},\mathsf L0,\dots,\mathsf L4\}\) and \(\mathsf R\) the rung it \emph{reaches}; a
conjecture also states the rung it \emph{claims}.  Microscopic declarations (\(O\), \(h\), boundary, refinements, states)
are listed beside the tags.  A statement that silently fixes a layer (one chirality, one enrichment, one target) is
incomplete until it names the layer and the data (AGENTS.md:113--115).  Two working rules follow:
state the result at the lowest \(\mathsf L\) and broadest \(\mathsf S\) at which its proof runs, and record the extra
data the next rung needs as part of the statement, as the restatements above do.

\subsection{What This Does Not Claim}

No rung is reached here.  The grid does not decide chirality: \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{ch}}\) versus \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{full}}\) is a
declared tag pending the source gap above.  The full-setting fidelity notion (defect data plus \(\Cc\boxtimes\overline{\Cc}
\simeq Z(\Cc)\) bulk identification) is a definition, not a theorem about any lattice limit.  The minimal-data column of
Definition 88.3 records current evidence; a lower layer may suffice for \(\mathsf R0^u\).

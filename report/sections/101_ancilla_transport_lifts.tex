\subsection{Setting and Ancilla Transport}

Throughout, \(\Cc\) is arbitrary unitary fusion, \(\eta:\one\to O\) is an isometry, \(O\cong\one\oplus X\),
\(\mathcal D=\langle O\rangle\) full, \(W_L=O^{\otimes L}\), \(W_0=\one\), and \(\Alg_L=\operatorname{End}_\Cc(W_L)\).
Use left bracketing and fixed unitary associators/unitors; \(V_L\) inserts \(\eta\) at even sites.
The occupation operator is \(N_L=\sum_j(1-\eta\eta^\dagger)^{(j)}\), as in (am),(as).
\(\Alg_{[j,k]}=1_{W_{j-1}}\otimes\operatorname{End}_\Cc(W_{k-j+1})\otimes1_{W_{L-k}}\): support is membership.
A lift means unital injective \(*\)-homomorphisms \(\alpha_L:\Alg_L\to\Alg_{2L}\) with \(\alpha_L(a)V_L=V_La\), all \(L\), and
\(\alpha_L(\Alg_{[j,k]})\subset\Alg_{[2j-1-r,2k-1+r]}\) for one finite slack \(r\), endpoints clipped.
Write \(m_c(L)=\dim\Hom(c,W_L)\). \(\Lambda_{c,d}(L)\) counts input-block \(c\) copies in output charge block \(d\),
not projection ranks; only occurring blocks are used. A point-fixing gauge is \(\operatorname{Ad}_U\),
\(U\in\Alg_{2L}\) unitary, \(UV_L=V_L\); without a support bound it is global (NOTE:\allowbreak 21--35).

\textbf{Theorem 101.1 [derived; any; \(\mathsf L0+(K,\kappa,t,u)\); \(\mathsf R0^u\)] (NOTE:\allowbreak 37--89; REVIEW:\allowbreak 51--54,128--132).}
\textbf{Assume} a pointed ancilla \(\kappa:\one\to K\), \(\kappa^\dagger\kappa=1\), unitaries
\(t_Y:Y\otimes K\to K\otimes Y\) for all \(Y\in\mathcal D\), and a unitary \(u:O\otimes K\to O\otimes O\), with
\[\begin{gathered}
 t_\one=1_K,\quad t_Z(f\otimes1_K)=(1_K\otimes f)t_Y,\quad t_{Y\otimes Z}=(t_Y\otimes1_Z)(1_Y\otimes t_Z),\\
 t_Y(1_Y\otimes\kappa)=\kappa\otimes1_Y,\qquad u(1_O\otimes\kappa)=1_O\otimes\eta .
\end{gathered}\]
Here \(f:Y\to Z\) is arbitrary; \(K\in\mathcal D\) follows since \(K\subset O\otimes K\cong O^2\).
\textbf{Prove} the following all-\(L\) lifts, with exact \(V_L\), slack one and coherent dyadic composites:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:101-ancilla}
 \alpha_L(a)=T_L(a\otimes1_{K^{\otimes L}})T_L^\dagger,\quad
 T_L=u^{\otimes L}B_L^K,\quad
 \Lambda^K_{c,d}(L)=\dim\Hom(d,c\otimes K^{\otimes L}).
\end{equation}
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Insert \(K_i\) after old site \(i\), crossing only the old suffix: CA-90 Lemma 90.0's
heterogeneous argument gives \(B_L^K:W_L\otimes K^{\otimes L}\to(O\otimes K)^{\otimes L}\).
Naturality and the tensor axiom give interval membership from old \(2j-1\) to old \(2k-1\); no \(K\)-\(K\) crossing occurs.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Pointedness gives \(T_L(1_{W_L}\otimes\kappa^{\otimes L})=V_L\).
Conjugation gives a unital \(*\)-map; compression gives intertwining and injectivity.
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Exterior \(u\)-cells cancel, so \(\alpha_L(\Alg_{[j,k]})\subset\Alg_{[2j-1,2k]}\).
Composition gives the dyadic cocycle, not coherence across arbitrary placements.
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). Orthogonal semisimplicity decomposes \(\Hom(d,W_L\otimes K^{\otimes L})\) as
\(\bigoplus_c\Hom(c,W_L)\otimes\Hom(d,c\otimes K^{\otimes L})\); restriction multiplicities give the formula. QED.
CA-90 is \(K=O,u=1\), with its sharper slack zero. Existence of the datum is an assumption.

\subsection{Gauge Invariants and Extraction Boundaries}

\textbf{Proposition 101.2 [derived; any; \(\mathsf L0\); finite \(\to\mathsf R0^u\)] (NOTE:\allowbreak 130--161,295--327; REVIEW:\allowbreak 57--60,134--139).}
For any two lifts of the same \(V_L\), equality of \(\Lambda\) is equivalent to global point-fixing unitary conjugacy.
It is invariant under any output unitary, and under input/output \(*\)-automorphisms up to block relabelling.
Every CA-96 transport lift, regardless of its block size, has
\(\Lambda^{\rm tr}_{c,d}(L)=\dim\Hom(d,c\otimes O^{\otimes L})\).
Thus a mismatch with \eqref{eq:101-ancilla} excludes every such transport lift up to output unitary gauge.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Conjugation preserves representation multiplicities; automorphisms only relabel blocks.
CA-96's inserted blocks and tail total \(O^{\otimes L}\), so its map is \(Q_L(a\otimes1_{W_L})Q_L^\dagger\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). \(P=V_LV_L^\dagger\) reduces both lifts (76.1); on \(P\) both equal \(V_LaV_L^\dagger\).
Equal \(\Lambda\) gives equal complement multiplicities \(\Lambda_{c,d}-\delta_{c,d}\). Choose blockwise unitary
intertwiners \(W\) on \(1-P\); \(U=P+W\) conjugates the lifts and fixes \(V_L\). QED. No locality bound on \(U\) follows.

\textbf{Lemma 101.4 [derived; any; \(\mathsf L0\); finite \(\to\mathsf R0^u\)] (NOTE:\allowbreak 295--338; REVIEW:\allowbreak 67--69,145).}

\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Unitality gives the dimension sum; the reducing vacuum corner supplies the diagonal copy.

\subsection{Finite Equations and Further Obstructions}

\textbf{Proposition 101.6 [derived; any; \(\mathsf L0\)+finite associator/dagger data; finite \(\to\mathsf R0^u\)] (NOTE:\allowbreak 361--408; REVIEW:\allowbreak 71--73,147--149).}
Fix \(b\ge1\), \(P=O^{\otimes b}\), \(\xi=\eta^{\otimes b}\). For simples \(s_i\) of full \(\mathcal D\), choose complete
orthonormal embeddings \(f_{ij;k,\mu}:s_k\to s_i\otimes s_j\). Pointed transport is equivalent to the finite system
\[
 z_i:P\otimes s_i\to s_i\otimes P,\quad z_i^\dagger z_i=1,\quad z_i z_i^\dagger=1,\quad
 z_\one=1_P,\quad z_i(\xi\otimes1_i)=1_i\otimes\xi,
\]
\[
 (f_{ij;k,\mu}\otimes1_P)z_k=(1_i\otimes z_j)(z_i\otimes1_j)(1_P\otimes f_{ij;k,\mu}),
\]
with fixed associators inserted; \(z_Y=t_Y^{-1}\), consistently with (90.5).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Necessity and sufficiency follow from 90.2(a): extend over orthogonal simple summands;
Schur gives naturality, and summing against \(f^\dagger\) gives the tensor axiom. Real/imaginary coordinates are polynomial.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). A pointed unitary tensor equivalence and its quasi-inverse transport solutions at the same \(b\)
(96.8). Changing \(\eta\) conjugates by a unitary on the unit multiplicity space of \(O\), tensored \(b\) times.
Thus the admissible set and its minimum (\(\infty\) if empty) are invariant. QED.
This minimum can be \(\infty\) with slack-one lifts (101.3); it is no lift criterion or bound from \(r\).
Finite equations at each fixed \(b\) imply no decision procedure for some \(b\): no terminating bound or general exact
encoding of complex input is supplied. No decidability or undecidability claim is made.

\textbf{Negative 101.7 [negative; any; \(\mathsf L0\); \(\to\mathsf R0^u\)] (NOTE:\allowbreak 10--19; REVIEW:\allowbreak 119--124,151--152).}

\textbf{Negative 101.8 [negative; any; \(\mathsf L0\); \(\to\mathsf R0^u\)] (NOTE:\allowbreak 87--89; REVIEW:\allowbreak 154--165).}
Existence of a blocked ancilla datum is not universal either. For arbitrary \(\Cc\), such a datum at block size \(b\)
necessarily supplies a positive integral class \(k=[K]\) central in \(K(\mathcal D)\), with \([O]^b k=[O]^{2b}\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). \(K\subset O^b\otimes K\cong O^{2b}\) lies in \(\mathcal D\); transport gives centrality and

\subsection{Non-Collapse}

\textbf{Theorem 104.1 [derived; any; category-free; \(\mathsf R2\) nontrivial given VR-1] (NOTE:\allowbreak 56--118; REVIEW:\allowbreak 155--164).}
Under A1--A4: A5 holds iff some \(T_n\) (\(n\neq0\)) is nonzero on \(D\); A5 then makes \emph{every} \(T_n\), \(n\neq0\), nonzero and
\(T_0\) non-scalar; \(|q_a(v,n)|>d_{a,n}\) is a finite sufficient certificate, and it is eventually necessary.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). \(B_n=T_nK^{-1}\) extends boundedly (A2 at \(s=0\)) and \(|q_r(v,n)-\langle i_av,B_ni_av\rangle|\le d_{r,n}\).
A1 gives \(K_r^{-1}v_r=J_{r\leftarrow a}K_a^{-1}v\); apply the VR-1 tail at order zero (\path{virasoro_routes.md}:111--112) to it,
whose order-one norm is \(1\), and Cauchy--Schwarz.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). A5 \(\Rightarrow\) \(|\langle i_av,B_ni_av\rangle|\ge\kappa\), so \(B_n\neq0\) and \(T_n\neq0\) on \(D\) (\(K^{-1}i_av\in D\)).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Conversely \(T_n\neq0\Rightarrow B_n\neq0\) (\(K\) maps \(D\) to \(D\)); polarisation on the dense algebraic union gives a
coarse unit \(v\) with \(b=|\langle i_av,B_ni_av\rangle|>0\); since \(d_{r,n}\to0\), \(|q_r|\ge b/2\) eventually.
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). Telescoping A3 gives \(|q_r-q_a|\le\sum_{j=a}^{r-1}e_{j,n,0}\le d_{a,n}\): the certificate gives \(\kappa=|q_a|-d_{a,n}\).
If A5 holds, pick \(r\) with \(d_{r,n}<\kappa\). Computing the tail majorant is substantive input, not an observed trend.
\par\(\langle1\rangle5\). If \(T_k=0\) for some \(k\neq0\): \([T_k,T_{j-k}]=(2k-j)T_j\) kills \(T_j\) for \(j\neq0,2k\), and
\([T_{3k},T_{-k}]=4kT_{2k}\) kills \(T_{2k}\); no central term enters. \([T_0,T_n]=-nT_n\) makes \(T_0\) non-scalar. QED.

\subsection{The Central-Charge Clause}

Normalised modes: \(L_n=T_n+\delta_{n0}(c^*-c)/24\), so \([L_m,L_n]=(m-n)L_{m+n}+\tfrac{c^*}{12}(m^3-m)\delta_{m+n,0}\); this is the shift
law of 79.1 (\path{79_ks_shell_and_refinement_defect.tex}:78--80). The invariant is \(c^*\); ``\(c>0\)'' must name its ledger.
\textbf{(PE):} \(D\) contains a Hilbert-total linear subspace \(V_{\rm fin}\) with \(L_nv=0\) for all sufficiently large \(n>0\), \(v\in V_{\rm fin}\).

\textbf{Theorem 104.4 [derived; any; category-free \(+\)(PE); \(\mathsf R2\) restriction] (NOTE:\allowbreak 154--207; REVIEW:\allowbreak 180--189).}
With the normalised relations and adjoint pairing on \(D\), (PE) gives \(c^*\ge0\), and \(c^*=0\) forces all \(L_n=0\).
Hence A5\(+\)(PE) gives \(c^*>0\). Positivity of \(L_0\) is \emph{not} used.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). For \(v\in V_{\rm fin}\) and large \(k\): \(\|L_{-k}v\|^2=2k\mu+\tfrac{c^*}{12}(k^3-k)\|v\|^2\), \(\mu=\langle v,L_0v\rangle\in\mathbb R\);
so \(c^*\ge0\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Let \(c^*=0\), \(\nu=\|L_0v\|^2\). With \(u=L_{-2k}v/k\), \(w=L_{-k}^2v/k^2\): \(\|u\|^2=4\mu/k\),
\(\langle u,w\rangle=6\mu/k\) (from \([L_{2k},L_{-k}]=3kL_k\), \([L_k,L_{-k}]=2kL_0\)), and \(L_k^2L_{-k}^2v=(8k^2L_0^2+4k^3L_0)v\) (using
\(L_kL_0v=0\)) gives \(\|w\|^2=8\nu/k^2+4\mu/k\). Then \(\det G=4\mu(8\nu-5k\mu)/k^3\ge0\) for all large \(k\) forces \(\mu=0\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). So \(L_{\pm k}v=0\) for large \(k\); \([L_k,L_{m-k}]\) with \(2k\neq m\) gives \((2k-m)L_mv=0\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). \(\langle u,L_mv\rangle=\langle L_{-m}u,v\rangle=0\) for \(u\in V_{\rm fin}\); totality gives \(L_m=0\) on \(D\), contradicting A5. QED.
Exact word-algebra checks: \path{checks/noncollapse_symbolic.py}; the Gram mutation \(6h\to5h\) is rejected.

\(\|Q_{\rm out}^tXQ_{\rm in}^{-t-\lambda}\|\le\sqrt{uu'}\,(1+\theta)^{t+\lambda/2}\).

\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Factor weights as \((Q_{\rm out}/Q_{\rm in})^tQ_{\rm in}^{-\lambda}\) for columns (sum \(\le u(1+\theta)^t\)) and as
\((Q_{\rm out}/Q_{\rm in})^{t+\lambda}Q_{\rm out}^{-\lambda}\) for rows (sum \(\le u'(1+\theta)^{t+\lambda}\)).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Schur test: row sums \(\le a\), column sums \(\le b\) give norm \(\le\sqrt{ab}\) (Cauchy--Schwarz per row).

\subsection{Setting and the Two Towers}

Let \((O,\eta)\) be a \emph{pointed} object: \(\eta:\one\to O\),
\(\eta^\dagger\eta=1\), \(p_0=\eta\eta^\dagger\), and \(\iota:X\to O\) an
orthogonal splitting of \(p_1=1-p_0\), so \(O\cong\one\oplus X\) (Penneys:219--237).
\(O\) is \emph{connected} if \(\Hom(\one,X)=0\).  Put
\(\Alg_L=\operatorname{End}_\Cc(O^{\otimes L})\), \(H_{L,c}=\Hom(c,O^{\otimes L})\),
\(\alpha_L(a)=a\otimes\mathrm{id}_O\); for an order-preserving injection
\(\varphi:[k]\to[l]\) (CONVENTIONS (t)) let \(V_\varphi:O^{\otimes k}\to O^{\otimes l}\)
insert \(\eta\) outside the image, \(\theta_\varphi(a)=V_\varphi aV_\varphi^\dagger\),
\(P_\varphi=V_\varphi V_\varphi^\dagger\).  \(d_O\) is the positive spherical
dimension (Penneys:1015--1019; EGNO:1700--1706), \(\tau_L=\Tr_\Cc/d_O^{L}\).
\textbf{Convention (amends (r); CONVENTIONS (am), 2026-10-22).}  The site is
the pointed pair \((O,\eta)\); \(X\) may contain unit summands, and (r)'s
species object is the connected case.  Configuration spaces are ordered, with
no \(N!\) and fibres \(\Hom(c,X^{\otimes N})\) (NOTE.md:75--79, :86--90; REVIEW.md:51).

\textbf{Proposition 77.1 [derived] (D1-T1, NOTE.md:120--151).}  \(\alpha\) is
a unital injective \(*\)-map; \(V\) is a coherent isometric placement functor,
\(V_\psi V_\varphi=V_{\psi\varphi}\); \(\theta_\varphi\) is an injective
\(*\)-map onto the corner \(P_\varphi\Alg_lP_\varphi\) with
\(\theta_\varphi(1)=P_\varphi\).  The unital tower has the fusion-graph
incidence \(m_d(L{+}1)=\sum_cm_c(L)N^d_{c,O}\) (CA-65); the corner tower has
block maps \(a_c\mapsto J_ca_cJ_c^\dagger\) and not that incidence.  A pointed
unitary \(u\) (\(u\eta=\eta'\)) intertwines both towers via \(\mathrm{Ad}\,u^{\otimes L}\).
\emph{Proof sketch.}  Compressing \(a\otimes\mathrm{id}_O\) with
\(\mathrm{id}\otimes\eta\) returns \(a\); \(V_\varphi^\dagger V_\varphi=1\) by
cancelling each \(\eta^\dagger\eta\), and composites by coherence
(Penneys:394--398); \(B\mapsto V_\varphi^\dagger BV_\varphi\) inverts \(\theta_\varphi\) on the corner.

\textbf{Proposition 77.2 [derived; near-tautological] (D1-T2, NOTE.md:153--182).}
All \(\Alg_L\), \(\theta_\varphi\), concatenation and the partial isometries
\(V_\varphi V_\psi^\dagger\) reconstruct the Cauchy completion (Penneys:358--362) of the
subcategory generated by \(O\), hence \(\Cc\) if \(O\) tensor-generates it, up to pointed
unitary monoidal equivalence.  Concatenation already carries the monoidal structure
(REVIEW.md:113--116); the content is the negative lesson that algebras plus \(\theta\)
do not suffice.  Braiding and twist must be marked separately.

\textbf{Proposition 77.3 [negative] (D1-N1, NOTE.md:184--197).}  Along any
nested placement schedule with lengths \(\to\infty\),
\(\varinjlim(\Alg_L,\theta)=\bigoplus_cK(H_{\infty,c})\) over reachable charges:
the unmarked corner limit retains only block Hilbert dimensions.

\subsection{Occupation Grading and Parity}

Let \(Q_{L,n}\) sum the occupation-pattern projections with \(n\) factors
\(p_1\), and \(Z_{L,n}=\bigoplus_{|S|=n}X^{\otimes n}\).

\textbf{Proposition 77.4 [derived] (D1-T3, NOTE.md:208--247; counts
re-derived at REVIEW.md:123--125).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\Alg_L=\bigoplus_{m,n}\Hom(Z_{L,n},Z_{L,m})\);
\(\Alg_L^{(k)}=\bigoplus_{m-n=k}Q_{L,m}\Alg_LQ_{L,n}\) has
\(\Alg_L^{(k)}\Alg_L^{(l)}\subseteq\Alg_L^{(k+l)}\), \((\Alg_L^{(k)})^\dagger=\Alg_L^{(-k)}\).
Each block is an \((\operatorname{End}Z_m,\operatorname{End}Z_n)\)-bimodule, in charge \(c\)
of size \(\binom Lm\dim\Hom(c,X^{\otimes m})\times\binom Ln\dim\Hom(c,X^{\otimes n})\);
\(\hat N_lV_\varphi=V_\varphi\hat N_k\).
\item With \(s=\dim\Hom(\one,X^{\otimes2})\), \(t=\dim\Hom(X,X^{\otimes2})\),
\(z=\dim\Hom(\one,X)\): \(\dim\Alg_2^{(+2)}=s\),
\(\dim\Alg_2^{(+1)}=2t+2z\), so the number-changing part of \(\Alg_2\) has
dimension \(2s+4t+4z\), i.e.\ \(2s+4t\) for connected \(O\).  The
number-preserving part is not an ideal and its complement is only a bimodule.
\item \(\Alg_L^{(k)}=0\) for all odd \(k\) and all \(L\) (parity preservation) iff
\(\Hom(X^{\otimes m},X^{\otimes n})=0\) for all \(m,n\ge0\) with \(m-n\) odd.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] Distribute \(O^{\otimes L}\) over \(\one\oplus X\) and
insert \(1=\sum_nQ_{L,n}\) on both sides (Penneys:219).  For two sites
\(Z_0=\one\), \(Z_1=X\oplus X\), \(Z_2=X^{\otimes2}\), giving item 2.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] Necessity in 3: a nonzero \(f:X^{\otimes n}\to X^{\otimes m}\)
sits in the occupied corner of \(\Alg_{\max(m,n)}\) as an odd-degree element.
Sufficiency: every block of $\langle1\rangle1$ is such a Hom space. QED.
\end{description}
Testing only \(t=0\) does not establish the all-scale condition; the CA-69
parity-even diagram subalgebra can be proper for this structural reason
(NOTE.md:243--247).

\subsection{Dual-Pair Blocking Is Decidable}

\textbf{Theorem 77.5 [derived] (D1-T4, NOTE.md:251--287; REVIEW.md:117--122).}
Write \([O]=\sum_co_c[c]\) (\(Y\) is a blocking root as in CA-87).  There is \(Y\in\Cc\), not necessarily simple, with \(O\cong Y\otimes\bar Y\)
iff the integer system
\[
  o_c=\sum_{a,b}y_ay_bN^c_{a,\bar b}\ \ (c\in\Irr\Cc),\qquad
  \sum_ay_a^2=o_\one,\qquad y_a\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}
\]
is solvable.  The search is finite.  If it succeeds, the unitary
\(u:O\to Y\otimes\bar Y\) can be chosen with
\(u\eta=d_Y^{-1/2}\mathrm{coev}_Y\), so the vacancy projector becomes the
normalised cup projection \(u p_0u^\dagger=d_Y^{-1}\mathrm{coev}_Y\mathrm{coev}_Y^\dagger\).
\textbf{Assume} the fusion coefficients and dagger. \textbf{Prove} criterion, finiteness, transport.
\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] For \(Y=\bigoplus_ay_aa\), the multiplicity of \(c\)
in \(Y\otimes\bar Y\) is the first sum. \textsc{By} distributivity.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] \(N^\one_{a,\bar b}=\delta_{ab}\) gives the second
equation; hence \(y_a\le\lfloor\sqrt{o_\one}\rfloor\) and exhaustive search
terminates, with exact integer arithmetic. \textsc{By} Penneys:427--430, :484--487.
\item[$\langle1\rangle3$.] Equal multiplicities give an isomorphism; polar
decomposition makes it unitary. \textsc{By} semisimplicity, Penneys:233.
\item[$\langle1\rangle4$.] \(U(o_\one)\) acts transitively on unit vectors of
\(\Hom(\one,Y\bar Y)\), so \(u\) may be corrected on the unit-isotypic block to send
\(\eta\) to \(d_Y^{-1/2}\mathrm{coev}_Y\), a unit vector since
\(\mathrm{coev}_Y^\dagger\mathrm{coev}_Y=d_Y\) (CONVENTIONS (r)). QED.
\end{description}
\textbf{Remarks [derived].}  (i) For connected \(O\), \(o_\one=1\) forces \(Y\)
simple, the hypothesis of Lemma~87.1; Theorem~77.5 strictly generalises it by the
converse, decidability, non-simple \(Y\) with multiplicities, and transport of
\(\eta\).  Self-duality of \(O\) and \(\mathrm{FPdim}\,O=(\mathrm{FPdim}\,Y)^2\)
are necessary only.  (ii) After blocking, range \(k\) on \(O\)-sites is range
\(2k\) on the alternating \(Y,\bar Y\) chain, vacancy insertion is insertion of a
normalised cup pair, and occupation counts pairs outside that channel.  (iii)
Failure excludes only dual-pair blocking inside \(\Cc\), not other words
\(Y_1\otimes\cdots\otimes Y_k\), enlarging \(\Cc\), module-category dualities or
equal universality classes; success selects no Hamiltonian (NOTE.md:281--287).

\textbf{Question (REVIEW.md:99--108; unchecked).}  Over alternating \(O\bar O\) words the
projections \(d_O^{-1}\mathrm{coev}\,\mathrm{coev}^\dagger\) match the cup Jones projections
of Hollands:1240--1271, and after blocking \(p_0\) is one; is the unital tower a basic construction?

\textbf{Proposition 77.6 [negative; strengthened] (D1-N2, NOTE.md:289--313;
REVIEW.md:109--112).}  A bare \(O\) is not an algebra object (FRS:2272--2284).
\(O\otimes\bar O\) carries the matrix algebra structure of FRS:2331--2336, and by
FRS:2913--2920 all \(U\otimes U^\vee\), including \(U=\one\), are Morita
equivalent [source-local; the authors defer the proof].  Hence the Morita class of
\(O\otimes\bar O\) is \emph{the same for every nonzero} \(O\) and carries no
information about the carrier.  Independently [derived], \(O\) and \(O\oplus O\)
have equivalent module categories but \(\dim\operatorname{End}\) differing by four.

\subsection{Vacuum Insertion, States, and Qualifications of CA-71--CA-73}

\textbf{Proposition 77.7 [derived] (D1-T5, D1-T5b, NOTE.md:317--336, :377--395).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every \(T\), \(f\mapsto V_\varphi f\) is a bijection
\(\Hom(T,O^{\otimes k})\to\{f:T\to O^{\otimes l}:P_\varphi f=f\}\), natural in \(T\).
\item For a state \(\omega\) with \(p=\omega(P_\varphi)>0\), the normalised
corner pullback has GNS vector \(\pi(P_\varphi)\Omega/\sqrt p\), at squared
distance \(2(1-\sqrt p)\) from \(\Omega\), and \(1-p\le2(1-\sqrt p)\le2(1-p)\).
\(\sum_n\sqrt{1-p_n}<\infty\) suffices for convergence of transported vacuum vectors.
\item \(\tau_l(P_\varphi)=d_O^{k-l}\), invariant under unitary dressing; trace
states cannot approach weight one under extensive insertion when \(d_O>1\).
\item The CA-72 unital completion has multiplicativity defect
\(\mathrm{cov}_\chi(S,T)(1-P_\varphi)\), of operator norm \(|\mathrm{cov}_\chi(S,T)|\)
whenever \(P_\varphi\ne1\), independently of \(\omega(P_\varphi)\).
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Proofs.}  (2) norm one, overlap \(\sqrt p\); (3) the categorical trace of an
isometry's range projection is its domain's dimension; (4) multiply out (REVIEW.md:126).

\textbf{Qualifications [derived; REVIEW.md:127--130, :173].}
(a) CA-72 Corollary~72.8 reads GNS-seminorm smallness as the soft-system
condition, but eq.~(29) of 2306.16063:568--575 is an operator-norm condition;
by 77.7(4) the seminorm is not that norm.
(b) The right caret \(\mathrm{id}_O\otimes\iota_\one\) never inserts before the
first strand, so it does not generate placements with \(\varphi(1)>1\); the
CA-71:167--169 generation sentence is false as stated.
(c) \(\bigoplus_c\Hom(c,O^{\otimes L})\) is not \(\Phi(1)^{\otimes L}\) in
\(\Hilb\), so the Thompson-\(V\) extension of B--S:522--541 is unavailable
(CA-71:173--174 ``indeed \(V\)'' unsupported); the \(F\)-action of B--S:500--511 survives.
(d) \(\theta_\varphi\) pins \emph{every} inserted site and sends constants to
\(P_\varphi\): \(\theta(F(a;1))=F(\theta(a);P_\varphi)\), qualifying CA-73:159--172.
(e) The isometry deficit read at CA-68:160--162 arises for overlapping
smearing under hard-core exclusion; box cells have none (Theorem~77.8).

\subsection{The Configuration Carrier}

For a cell split into children of lengths \(\ell,\ell'\) let \(R:O\to O^{\otimes2}\),
\(R\eta=\eta\otimes\eta\),
\(R\iota=\sqrt{\ell/(\ell{+}\ell')}\,(\iota\otimes\eta)+\sqrt{\ell'/(\ell{+}\ell')}\,(\eta\otimes\iota)\),
i.e.\ \(R=U(\mathrm{id}_O\otimes\eta)\) with a real rotation \(U\) on \(X\eta\oplus\eta X\).
With \(\Delta_N=\{0<x_1<\dots<x_N<1\}\), set
\(\mathcal F_{\Cc,X}=\hoplus_{c,N}L^2\big(\Delta_N;\Hom(c,X^{\otimes N})\big)\).

\textbf{Theorem 77.8 [derived] (D1-T6, NOTE.md:399--455; REVIEW.md:131--135).}
Along nested partitions of \((0,1)\) with mesh \(\to0\), the \(R\) are exact
charge- and number-preserving isometric refinements, coherent under all
parenthesisations (amplitudes telescope to \(\sqrt{|J|/|I|}\)), and the box maps
\(K_\pi(t\text{ at }j_1<\dots<j_N)=\prod_r|I_{j_r}|^{-1/2}\mathbf 1_{I_{j_r}}(x_r)\,t\)
extend to a unitary from the Hilbert inductive limit onto \(\mathcal F_{\Cc,X}\).
For adjacent intervals,
\(\mathcal F_c(I\cup J)\cong\hoplus_{a,b}\mathcal F_a(I)\otimes\mathcal F_b(J)\otimes\Hom(c,a\otimes b)\):
fusion fibres are retained, not replaced by onsite factors; no braiding is used.
\textbf{Caveat:} \(\mathrm{Ad}\,R\) is again a corner map; this is a Hilbert
carrier theorem, not a unital observable refinement.

\textbf{Proposition 77.9 [negative] (D1-N4, NOTE.md:464--473).}  Bare dyadic
insertion has an atomic limit: \(\ell^2\) of a countable position set with fusion
fibres; moving a vector to a nearby point changes it by norm \(\sqrt2\), so the
geometric translations are not strongly continuous.

\subsection{Conjecture, with Caveat}

D1-C (NOTE.md:9--62) conjectures that explicit nearest-neighbour \(H_\varepsilon\),
built from \(D_{\mathrm{hop}}=\pi_{X0}-\pi_{0X}\) and declared reaction morphisms
\(b_\alpha:X\to X\otimes X\), \(v_\beta:\one\to X\otimes X\) (NOTE.md:35--41), converge
in projected-resolvent sense on \(\mathcal F_{\Cc,X}\) to a sector-coupling collision
form, under declared closedness and core hypotheses.  \textbf{Status: conjectural;
framing overstated} (REVIEW.md:163).  The reviewer found (REVIEW.md:78--91) that
\(D_{\mathrm{hop}}^\dagger D_{\mathrm{hop}}\) equals standard hard-core hopping
plus a contact attraction \(-2\varepsilon^{-2}n_jn_{j+1}\): it engineers Neumann
collisions (two-particle \(E_0=0\), \(E_1=9.8673\) at \(L=60\)), whereas standard
hopping gives Dirichlet collisions (\(E_0=9.8673\); \(\pi^2=9.8696\))
(\path{checks/review_contact_output.txt}:4--7), under which all traces vanish
and the number-changing coupling disappears.  The limit is thus non-universal,
sees \(\Cc\) only through finite fibres and two linear maps (REVIEW.md:92--98), and is
a nonrelativistic (\(z=2\)) benchmark with no conformal, Wilson or fidelity content.

\textbf{Open problem [Question] (INTEGRATION.md:103--106, :133--136).}  On
\(\mathcal F_{\Cc,X}\), construct coherent local \emph{unital} refinements of a
number-changing field algebra (a categorical second quantisation), compatible
with one state family satisfying WT-1 (CA-81).  Corner maps do not solve this.

\subsection{What This Does Not Claim}

No dynamics, critical point, unital OAR map, continuum CFT or fidelity
statement is made.  Occupation \(\hat N\) is microscopic, not a CFT particle
number; no argument identifies the empty configuration with a conformal vacuum
(NOTE.md:475--479).  The Jones-tower identity is a Question.  D1-T7 (partial
form convergence) and the D1-C analytic gap are left to a later shard.

\subsection{Grids}

The synthetic block includes XYZ triples on \(\{-1,0,1\}^3\setminus\{0\}\),
XY anisotropy, compass \(XX/ZZ\), DM-style antisymmetric \(XY-YX\), Heisenberg
with one field component, and 24 deterministic dense generic matrices.  The
generic matrices are generated by fixed sine/cosine formulas in the code rather
than by an ambient random number generator.

\subsection{Purpose}

These samples answer engineering questions: Does the conservation gate reject
generic dense inputs?  Do structured bilinear families reach the boost gate?
Do current-collapsed one-axis cases remain caught?  They do not answer whether
XYZ or DM chains are critical.

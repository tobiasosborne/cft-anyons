\subsection{An Explicit Microscopic Polynomial}

Take $k+1$ two-flavor complex fermion copies, $k\ge1$, with half-integer
momenta in the finite window and the filled negative sea. Let
$t^a=\sigma_a/2$ and use the no-wrap one-particle shifts
\[
 j_{M,p}^a(e_r\otimes v)=e_{r-p}\otimes t^a v
\]
when both labels are in the window, and zero otherwise. Normal-ordered
complex second quantization gives finite quadratic currents $J_{M,p}^{u,a}$.
Let $I$ sum the first $k$ copies, $J$ denote the last, and $D=I+J$.
For a level-$l$ family $X$ define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf S^{[l]}_{M,n,R}(X)&=\frac1{l+2}
  \sum_{a=1}^3\ \sum_{\substack{|p|\le R\\|n-p|\le R}}
   {:}X_{M,n-p}^a X_{M,p}^a{:},\\
 C_{M,n,R}&=\mathsf S^{[k]}(I)+\mathsf S^{[1]}(J)-\mathsf S^{[k+1]}(D).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The normal ordering orders current modes, keeping their contraction terms
when the displayed products are expanded. Thus $C$ is an explicit CAR
polynomial of degree at most four, not an unspecified continuum operator.

The infinite current cocycle has coefficient $p\delta_{ab}/2$ at level
one: exactly $p$ Fermi crossings contribute $\Tr(t^at^b)=\delta_{ab}/2$.
Independent copies add levels. The sourced GKO result gives
\[
 c=\frac{3k}{k+2}+1-\frac{3(k+1)}{k+3}
   =1-\frac6{(k+2)(k+3)}.
\]
This fixes the normalization before any finite computation.

\subsection{Exact Stabilization on Free-Energy Cores}

\textbf{Theorem CR-2 [local derivation].}
\textbf{Assume} the no-wrap finite currents above, compatible sea GNS
embeddings, and auxiliary excitation energy
\[
 E_M=\sum_{u,f,r>0}r\,n_{u,f,r}
       +\sum_{u,f,r<0}|r|(1-n_{u,f,r}).
\]
Let $P_e$ project onto auxiliary energy at most $e$.
\textbf{Prove} the embedded $C_{M,n,R}$ agrees with the continuum coset
mode $C_n$ on $P_e$ whenever
\[
 R>e+|n|,\qquad M/2-1/2>e+2R.
\]
\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] $[E_M,J_{M,p}]=-pJ_{M,p}$.
\textsc{By} each nonzero bilinear moving occupation from $r$ to $r-p$;
the scalar subtraction has $p=0$. Consequently $J_{M,p}P_e=0$ for $p>e$
by nonnegativity of $E_M$. The same holds in the infinite sea.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] Every omitted normally ordered current pair
annihilates $P_e$. \textsc{By} its indices summing to $n$: if either
absolute value exceeds $R$, one index is positive and greater than $e$.
Normal ordering places that mode on the right; use $\langle1\rangle1$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle3$.] Retained products agree with continuum
products in the safe window. \textsc{By} deviations from the sea lying
within $|r|\le e$, one current action involving only $|r|\le e+|p|$,
and two retained actions involving only $|r|\le e+2R$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle4$.] The finite retained sum equals the continuum
sum on $P_e$. QED \textsc{by} $\langle1\rangle2$--$\langle1\rangle3$.
\end{description}

The choice $R_M=\lfloor M/8\rfloor$ eventually meets both conditions for
every fixed $e,n$. A fixed word of modes uses the enlarged core
$e+\sum_j|n_j|$ and therefore also stabilizes exactly. The symmetric
two-index cutoff preserves finite adjoints; same-sign pairs must be grouped
before taking adjoints, and zero-index pairs use the exact same-component
commutation of $J_0^a$ with $J_p^a$.

\textbf{Vacuum corollary CR-3.} For $n\ge1$, the smaller conditions
$R\ge n$ and $M/2-1/2>n$ suffice for
$C_{M,-n,R}\Omega=C_{-n}\Omega$. Indeed, positive and zero currents
annihilate the sea, so only two negative indices of total magnitude $n$
contribute; all involved orbitals have $|r|\le n$.

\subsection{Exact Witnesses and the Remaining Interface}

The sparse-CAR producer evaluates the actual polynomials with exact complex
rational amplitudes and canonical fermion signs. No many-body matrix is
allocated. At $M=8,16$ and $R=3,4$, it gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|cc}
 k&c&\|C_{-2}\Omega\|^2&\|C_{-3}\Omega\|^2\\\hline
 1&1/2&1/4&1\\
 2&7/10&7/20&7/5
\end{array}
\]
The independent oracle is $c(n^3-n)/12$, not a fitted central charge.
The run distinguishes the general CR-2 window from the smaller CR-3
vacuum window. CAR signs, level addition, adjoint pairings and refined
descendants are checked separately; coefficient and embedding-sign
mutations are rejected.

What remains is a coherent \emph{local coset observable} construction.
The ambient fermion tower retains additional sectors. A stress difference
does not remove them, and finite commutants or vacuum-cyclic compressions
have not been shown to commute with refinement. Global finite-scale
positivity and microscopic ground states of the quartic Hamiltonian are
also unproved. The result solves a generator and cutoff-control problem;
it does not yet enlarge the completed modular-data compiler beyond CA-82.

\subsection{Setting and the Seam Pairing}

\(\Cc\) is unitary fusion, \(O\neq0\) a site object (CONVENTIONS (r); (am) for \(O=\one\oplus\one\)), with dual
\((\overline O,\mathrm{ev}_O:\overline O\otimes O\to\one,\mathrm{coev}_O:\one\to O\otimes\overline O)\) from the unitary
spherical structure, raw cups of squared norm \(d_O\) (CONVENTIONS (b); Penneys:992--1030).  Tiles, \(h\) and \(H=-\sum
h_j\) follow (ak), (al).  The \emph{linear history} tile \(1+th\) (CA-86 Lemma 86.1) and the \emph{exact} tile
\(e^{th}\) (CA-78 Prop.~78.2) are kept apart.  Put \(W_+=O\otimes\overline O\), \(W_-=\overline O\otimes O\), with cut
spaces \(\mathcal H(W)=\bigoplus_a\Hom(a,W)\) as in CA-80.  \emph{Spatial RP} means positivity over all linear
combinations of half-patch insertions, not of one scalar partition sum (NOTE.md:52--55).

\textbf{Definition 92.1 [declaration; \(\mathsf L0\)].}  A \emph{seam pairing} is a unitary \(s:W_+\to W_-\) with
\(s\,\mathrm{coev}_O=\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger\), declared with a rule pairing all non-seam tiles with their reflected
adjoints.  The rule is antilinear, involutive and compatible with dagger and sewing; the reverse seam uses \(s^\dagger\)
(NOTE.md:37--47).  It is fixed before \(h\).  \emph{Gauge (REVIEW.md:122--132):} \(s\circ W\) with \(W\) unitary in
\(\operatorname{End}_\Cc(W_+)\) and \(W\mathrm{coev}_O=\mathrm{coev}_O\) is equally admissible and changes the kernel to
\(W^\dagger K_s\).  On non-vacuum blocks and on \(\mathrm{coev}_O^\perp\) \(W\) is free, so polar decomposition makes
those blocks PSD for \emph{some} \(s\): single-tile RP is a property of \((\Cc,O,h,s)\).

\textbf{Lemma 92.2 [derived; existence of \(s\)].}  A unitary \(s\) with \(s\,\mathrm{coev}_O=\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger\)
exists iff \(\dim\Hom(a,W_+)=\dim\Hom(a,W_-)\) for all \(a\in\Irr\Cc\) (INTEGRATION.md:17--18); in particular when
\(O\cong\overline O\).
\(\langle1\rangle1\). Necessity: a unitary isomorphism preserves multiplicities (NOTE.md:216--218).
\(\langle1\rangle2\). Sufficiency: matching orthonormal bases of each \(\Hom(a,W_\pm)\) give a unitary \(V\); \(V\mathrm{coev}_O\) and
\(\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger\) lie in \(\Hom(\one,W_-)\) with equal norm \(\sqrt{d_O}\), so a unitary \(U\) on that block maps one to the
other; set \(s=(U\oplus1)V\).  \(\langle1\rangle3\). If \(\psi:O\to\overline O\) is an isomorphism, \(\psi\otimes\psi^{-1}:W_+\to W_-\)
is one, so the multiplicities agree.  QED.
\(s\) alone does not supply the whole reflection rule (NOTE.md:45--47).  At \(\mathsf L1\) the identity
\(\mathrm{ev}^\dagger=c_{O,\overline O}(\theta_O\otimes1)\mathrm{coev}\) would supply \(s\); it is a reviewer remark with
\emph{no local source} (Law~1 gap, REVIEW.md:241--245) and is not used.  Self-duality need not exist (Penneys:917).

\subsection{The Bent Kernel and Single-Tile Spatial RP}

With associators inserted for the named bracketings, and \(v_{a,\mu}:a\to W_+\) isometric inclusions,
\begin{equation}
  \operatorname{rot}(T)=(1_{\overline O}\otimes1_O\otimes\mathrm{coev}_O^\dagger)(1_{\overline O}\otimes T\otimes1_{\overline O})
  (\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger\otimes1_O\otimes1_{\overline O}),\quad K_s(T)=s^\dagger\operatorname{rot}(T),\quad
  K^a_{\mu\nu}=d_a^{-1}\operatorname{tr}_\Cc\big(v_{a,\mu}^\dagger K_s(T)v_{a,\nu}\big).\tag{92.1}
\end{equation}
\emph{Evaluation prescription} (NOTE.md:144--173): expand \(O,\overline O\) into simples keeping every copy and vertex
index; place a tile on a non-adjacent slot as \(F_{\mathrm{path}}^\dagger(1\otimes D_\alpha\otimes1)F_{\mathrm{path}}\)
(CA-78:44--53); insert the oriented cups in their actual slots; multiply by \(s^\dagger\), not by an unprovided
braiding; divide Penneys-normalised vertices by \((d_id_j/d_k)^{1/4}\) (Penneys:530--568).  Each entry is then a
finite sum of products of \(F\)-symbols, pivotal cups and entries of \(s\), with all multiplicities; in arbitrary
cut bases use the inverse Gram of CA-80:70--81.

\textbf{(RH) [hypothesis, not derived].}  After bending, the reflected half-patch, read through \(s\), evaluates to the
adjoint of the half-amplitude.

\textbf{Theorem 92.3 [derived, conditional on \(s\) and (RH); E3-S].}  \textbf{Assume} Definition 92.1, one crossing
tile \(T=\sum_\alpha w_\alpha D_\alpha\), half-insertions spanning \(\mathcal H(W_+)\), and (RH).  \textbf{Prove}
spatial RP iff every block \(K^a=[K^a_{\mu\nu}]\) of (92.1) is PSD.
\(\langle1\rangle1\). \(\operatorname{rot}\) is type-correct and complex-linear into \(\Hom(W_+,W_-)\) (intermediate word
\(\overline O\otimes O\otimes O\otimes\overline O\)); it is not an endomorphism.  BY Penneys:398--430.
\(\langle1\rangle2\). \(v^\dagger Xv\in\operatorname{End}(a)=\mathbb C\) and \(\operatorname{tr}_\Cc=d_a\cdot\)scalar, so
\(K^a\) is the \(a\)-block of \(K_s(T)\).  BY Penneys:1010--1019.
\(\langle1\rangle3\). BY (RH), all remaining cuts run along tile legs and CA-80 Lemma 80.1 gives the mixed form
\(q(x,y)=\langle x,K_s(T)y\rangle\).  This is where (RH) enters; the review: E3-S is ``close to a definition plus
Lemma 80.2; the real content is the explicit oriented formula'' (REVIEW.md:116--120).
\(\langle1\rangle4\). CA-80 Lemma 80.2 with spanning; \(d_a>0\) is harmless.  QED.
The Hermiticity equations \(K^a_{\mu\nu}=\overline{K^a_{\nu\mu}}\) are \emph{spatial}, not the temporal dagger pairing of
Cor.~80.3.  Theorem 92.3 is the typed form of D5's crossing-kernel test (D5 NOTE.md:231--239; REVIEW.md:57).

\textbf{Proposition 92.4 [conditional; multi-tile sufficiency].}  \textbf{Assume} several crossing tiles whose ports
and pairings tensor and reassociate compatibly, disjoint along the seam, all other tiles in reflected pairs, any extra
seam insertion positive and placed inside the reflected factorisation.  \textbf{Prove} \(K_{s,i}\ge0\) for all \(i\)
implies all-patch spatial RP.  \(\langle1\rangle1\). \(K_{s,i}=B_i^\dagger B_i\) blockwise, so \(\bigotimes_iK_{s,i}=
(\bigotimes B_i)^\dagger(\bigotimes B_i)\ge0\).  \(\langle1\rangle2\). Reassociation is unitary; charge restriction and
half-patch attachment give a compression \(V^\dagger(\bigotimes K_{s,i})V\ge0\).  QED (NOTE.md:201--214).
Necessity holds only if the admitted patches include single-tile full-span tests.  Compatibility is not automatic
for non-braided \(\Cc\) (planar order of the seam word) and may be unsatisfiable (REVIEW.md:133--141).  On a
translation-invariant brick lattice ``bulk tiles in reflected pairs'' is \(\Theta_x(h)=h\).

\subsection{The Linear History: an Exact Schur Criterion}

Let \(u=\mathrm{coev}_O\), \(e=u/\sqrt{d_O}\), \(P=\ket e\bra e\), \(Q=1-P\), \(A=K_s(h)\), \(a=\langle e,Ae\rangle\),
\(b=QAe\), \(C=QAQ|_{Q\mathcal H}\), \(C^+\) the inverse on \(\Ran C\).

\textbf{Theorem 92.5 [derived, conditional on \(s\), (RH), one crossing tile with full span; E3-L].}
\textbf{Prove} (i) \(K_s(1)=uu^\dagger=d_OP\); (ii) \(1+th\) is spatially RP for all \(0<t<\varepsilon\), some
\(\varepsilon>0\), iff
\begin{equation}
  A=A^\dagger,\qquad C\ge0,\qquad b\in\Ran C;\tag{92.2}
\end{equation}
(iii) at a given \(t>0\) iff (92.2) and \(d_O+t(a-b^\dagger C^+b)\ge0\), i.e.\ for
\(t\le t_*=d_O/(b^\dagger C^+b-a)\) when the denominator is positive, and for all \(t\) otherwise.
\(\langle1\rangle1\). At \(T=1\) the interchange law cancels the associators: \(\operatorname{rot}(1)=\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger
\mathrm{coev}_O^\dagger\); unitarity of \(s\) gives \(s^\dagger\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger=\mathrm{coev}_O\).  Hence
\begin{equation}
  K_s(1+th)=\begin{pmatrix}d_O+ta & tb^\dagger\\ tb & tC\end{pmatrix}.\tag{92.3}
\end{equation}
Checked with nontrivial 3-cocycles, including non-self-dual \(O\) (\path{review_output.txt}:2--4).
\(\langle1\rangle2\). Positivity at any \(t>0\) forces \(A=A^\dagger\) and \(C\ge0\); for \(z\in\ker C\) the term linear
in \(\lambda\) on \(\lambda e+z\) forces \(b^\dagger z=0\), so \(b\in(\ker C)^\perp=\Ran C\).
\(\langle1\rangle3\). Under (92.2), \(\langle(\alpha,y),K_s(1+th)(\alpha,y)\rangle=t\|C^{1/2}(y+C^+b\alpha)\|^2
+(d_O+ta-tb^\dagger C^+b)|\alpha|^2\); \(y=-C^+b\alpha\) gives necessity.
\(\langle1\rangle4\). \(d_O>0\) and finite dimension give (ii) from (iii).  QED (NOTE.md:233--272).
\(\varepsilon\) depends only on \((d_O,a,b,C)\), so for identical crossing tiles it is \emph{uniform in chain length \(N\)
and row number \(M\)} (REVIEW.md:159--162).  The criterion is not \(K_s(h)\ge0\) (Prop.~92.6(iii)).  Checks: 300 random
pencils and a range failure (\path{review_output.txt}:31--32; \path{seams_output.txt}:6).
\emph{Exact gates [derived remark].}  For \(e^{th}\) the criterion is only pointwise, \(K^a(w(t))\ge0\) with the CA-78
ODE; RP near \(t=0\) implies \(A=A^\dagger\) and \(QAQ\ge0\), but the range condition is not inferable: \(\big[\begin{smallmatrix}
1&t\\t&t^2\end{smallmatrix}\big]\ge0\) has derivative \(\big[\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\big]\), whose pencil fails
it (an illustration, not a categorical gate; NOTE.md:274--282).

\textbf{Remark 92.7 [derived in \(\mathrm{Vec}\) with the flip; conjectural in general].}  By (N1), \(K_{\mathrm{flip}}(h)\) is

\subsection{A Finite Osterwalder--Schrader Lemma (Category-Free)}

\textbf{Lemma 92.8 [derived; E3-OS].}  \textbf{Assume} a finite space \(V\), a shift \(\tau\), a nonzero PSD form
\(q_0\), and \(q_1(f,g)=q_0(f,\tau g)\) with \(0\le q_1\le q_0\).  \textbf{Prove} \(T[f]=[\tau f]\) is a well-defined
positive contraction on \(\mathcal H=V/\mathrm{rad}\,q_0\); if \(T\) is injective, \(E=-a^{-1}\log T\ge0\) and
\(T^n=e^{-naE}\); if \(\tau1=1\), \(q_0(1,1)=1\), then \(\Omega=[1]\) has zero energy; multi-time kernels are positive.
\(\langle1\rangle1\). For PSD \(q\), \(q(z,z)=0\Rightarrow q(f,z)=0\) (vary \(\lambda\) in \(z+\lambda f\)).
\(\langle1\rangle2\). \(z\) null \(\Rightarrow q_1(z,z)=0\Rightarrow q_0(f,\tau z)=0\ \forall f\): \(T\) is well defined.
\(\langle1\rangle3\). \(\langle[f],T[g]\rangle=q_1(f,g)\), so \(T=T^\dagger\), \(0\le T\le1\).
\(\langle1\rangle4\). Diagonalise; eigenvalues in \((0,1]\) under injectivity.
\(\langle1\rangle5\). \(\sum_{ij}\langle v_i,T^{n_i+n_j}v_j\rangle=\|\sum_iT^{n_i}v_i\|^2\).  QED (NOTE.md:286--317).
\emph{Strictness} (NOTE.md:333--341): injectivity is needed for a Hamiltonian on the whole quotient (\(K(1)\) above has
rank one), and the lower bound is needed (\(q_0=1\), \(\tau=-1\)).  \emph{What it does not give:} Theorem 92.3 supplies
\(q_0\); the bound \(q_1\le q_0\), shift closure and compatibility of the two forms are unproved for any strip.  No
covariance, locality, field regularity or continuum.  A reflection excludes positive momentum: \(JPJ^\dagger=-P\),
\(P\ge0\) force \(P=0\); positive energy and signed momentum (Schottenloher:4113--4117, :4150--4172), and covariance is
a separate axiom (:4616--4642).

\subsection{Two Directions: What Fails as Posed and What Survives}

\textbf{Proposition 92.9 [derived, negative; brief E3(b), E3(c) as posed].}  (i) The temporal and spatial transfers
act on cut spaces of different objects and dimensions; a perpendicular commutator is undefined until a common
carrier is identified (NOTE.md:366--376).  (ii) \(\operatorname{rot}\) is complex-linear and ``\(F\) composed with
dagger'' conjugate-linear, so they differ on any nonzero complex space (literal reading).  Robustly, no single-tile
similarity exists: the identity tile has full temporal rank and rank-one spatial \(K(1)\).  \emph{Not refuted:} a
relation between whole-lattice row and column transfers, e.g.\ \(\Tr T_t^M=\Tr T_x^L\) on a torus, true for any
tensor network without an \(F\)-move; E3(b) is false only in its literal clause (REVIEW.md:174--184).  (iii) On one
cut, YBE gates need not give commuting brick sweeps (CA-78 Prop.~78.7(i)) and commuting gates need not satisfy YBE
under any analytic reparametrisation (78.7(ii)); for the perpendicular pair these are analogies (REVIEW.md:197--199).

\textbf{Lemma 92.10 [derived under its hypotheses; E3-Y, E3-D, E3-T].}  (Y) \textbf{Assume} one carrier
\(\mathcal H\), auxiliary \(A,B\), \(L_{aj}(u)\) trivial on \(B\), \(L_{bj}(v)\) trivial on \(A\), \(R\) invertible on
\(A\otimes B\), \([L_{aj},L_{bk}]=0\) (\(j\neq k\)), \(RL_{aj}L_{bj}=L_{bj}L_{aj}R\), and a linear closure with
\(\mathrm{Cl}(M_aM_b)=t(u)t(v)\), \(\mathrm{Cl}(M_bM_a)=t(v)t(u)\), \(\mathrm{Cl}(R^{-1}XR)=\mathrm{Cl}(X)\).
\textbf{Prove} \([t(u),t(v)]=0\).  \(\langle1\rangle1\). Regroup \(M_aM_b=\mathsf A_N\cdots\mathsf A_1\),
\(\mathsf A_j=L_{aj}L_{bj}\).  \(\langle1\rangle2\). \(R\mathsf A_j=\mathsf B_jR\) for \(j=N,\dots,1\).  \(\langle1\rangle3\).
Apply \(\mathrm{Cl}\).  QED.  (D) With defects \(D_j=R\mathsf A_j-\mathsf B_jR\),
\([t(u),t(v)]=\mathrm{Cl}\big(R^{-1}\sum_k\mathsf B_N\cdots\mathsf B_{k+1}D_k\mathsf A_{k-1}\cdots\mathsf A_1\big)\); \(N\) terms,
no uniform bound, no converse.  (T) On a common carrier two transfers starting at \(1\) commute to first order, and
\begin{equation}
  [e^{\delta A}e^{\delta B},e^{\delta B}e^{\delta A}]=-\delta^3[A+B,[A,B]]+O(\delta^4),\qquad
  A=\textstyle\sum_{\mathrm{odd}}h_j,\ B=\sum_{\mathrm{even}}h_j\tag{92.4}
\end{equation}
(NOTE.md:378--473; \path{seams_output.txt}:7; \path{review_output.txt}:41).  Identifying the two directional transfers
with \(t(u),t(v)\) of one closed family is \emph{unproved} for general \(\Cc\); (T) answers the brief's first-order
request with zero (REVIEW.md:200--204).

\subsection{The First Gap: Reflection Coherence}

(RH) for a multi-tile brick seam is \emph{assumed via the definition of the pairing, not derived}; \(s\) is pinned
only on \(\mathrm{coev}_O\) and is gauge beyond it (REVIEW.md:289--299).  \textbf{Reflection-coherence lemma
[proposal; full; \(\mathsf L0\) + a declared unitary self-duality \(\psi:O\to\overline O\), or \(\mathsf L1\); finite].}
Build \(\Theta_x\) from the antilinear anti-monoidal functor \(X\mapsto(X^\vee)^\dagger\) (Penneys:682--708, :992--1003)
composed with \(\psi\) (or a braiding, or E2's mirror carrier).  Prove (i) \(\Theta_x\) is antiunitary on every cut
space, compatible with Lemma 80.1 sewing, \(\Theta_x^2\) trivial on observables; (ii) the induced \(s\) satisfies
\(s\,\mathrm{coev}_O=\mathrm{ev}_O^\dagger\), Frobenius--Schur phases tracked; (iii) \(K_s(h)\) Hermitian iff
\(\Theta_x(h)=h\) (INTEGRATION.md:124--136).  With Prop.~92.4 this would give spatial RP of the whole CA-86 linear
history for self-dual sites, a statement about \((\Cc,O,h)\), not \((\Cc,O,h,s)\).  An FS phase is absorbed in the
vacuum entry of \(s\); \(\Theta_x^2=-1\) on pseudo-real sites is unexamined (REVIEW.md:270).  Further open lemmas
(NOTE.md:566--591): exact-gate, strip-translation, crossing-and-closure, continuum.  \emph{Layer observation:} a
coherent \(s\) is available for self-dual \(O\) at \(\mathsf L0\) with declared \(\psi\), expected at \(\mathsf L1\)
(unsourced), and can fail for non-self-dual \(O\) in non-braided \(\Cc\): the \(\mathsf L0/\mathsf L1\) split of
\(\mathsf R0^u\) (CA-88:104).

\subsection{What This Does Not Claim}

No whole-history iff for spatial RP for general \((\Cc,O,h)\); no coherent \(s\) for the whole brick grammar; no
spatial sewing identity across a plaquette seam and no anomaly-line limit (CA-75 Link (a2) stays open; only the seam
operator exists); no crossing relation between directional transfers; no lattice OS axioms beyond Lemma 92.8; no
positive momentum, Euclidean covariance or continuum.  Nothing reaches \(\mathsf R1\)--\(\mathsf R5\).  Theorem 92.5
is for the linear history only; exact gates are characterised pointwise; multi-tile necessity is not claimed.

\subsection{Exact Input and Concrete Output}

In order $(1,\sigma,\psi)$ the supported seed has
\[
 S=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&\sqrt2&1\\\sqrt2&0&-\sqrt2\\1&-\sqrt2&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \Theta=\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{\pi i/8},-1),\qquad c=\frac12.
\]
The compiler compares $S$, twists, fusion multiplicities and the selected
real $c$ exactly, allowing unit-preserving relabelling. Rational polynomial
arithmetic modulo $z^8+1$ represents $z=e^{\pi i/8}$ and
$\sqrt2=z^2-z^6$. Character $T$ retains its separate vacuum phase
$e^{-2\pi i c/24}$; it is not coerced into this smaller cyclotomic field.
Explicit factor lists declare products; no general factorization algorithm
or reconstruction of arbitrary $F/R$ data is claimed.

At even size $M$, take antiperiodic momenta
$r=-M/2+1/2,\ldots,M/2-1/2$ and $\epsilon_M=2\pi/M$. The finite
microscopic mode matrix is the source-modified weighted shift
\[
 A_{M,n}e_r=\cos^2(\epsilon_M n/4)
 \frac{\sin(\epsilon_M(r-n/2))}{\epsilon_M}e_{r-n}
\]
when both labels are in the window, and zero otherwise. The normal-ordered
self-dual quadratic $Q_M(A_{M,n})$ includes the factor $1/2$ and the
vacuum scalar; the recipe emits its actual CAR terms.
The $n=0$ sine Hamiltonian is nearest-neighbour in the real spatial basis,
with an antiperiodic seam. Its positive-momentum occupation energies are
positive, so the declared sea is its finite ground state. This chiral
Hamiltonian is distinct from the auxiliary two-component Dirac benchmark.

The algebra map refines CAR generators by the isometric zero-padding map,
$\Psi(f)\mapsto\Psi(Jf)$. It is unital and preserves conjugation and
parity. A one-particle corner matrix $JAJ^*$ is not substituted for this
CAR algebra map.

\subsection{The Local Observable Theory}

The selected finite observable algebra is the even self-dual CAR algebra.
For $k$ factors take independent parity fixed points $(\mathbb Z_2)^k$.
Total parity alone would retain odd-times-odd fields and give a different
observable extension. Refinements are equivariant and the sea states are
compatible. Thus these initial chiral states give constant Wilson rows;
CA-81's nonconstant Dirac rows are a separate example of the same formalism.

The local reconstruction uses norm limits of even products of smoothly
sampled, interval-supported real antiperiodic test sections. Its represented
weak closure is the output interval algebra. This prescription defines the
sampled-generator local net; it does not assert equality with a maximal
construction using every arbitrary bounded scaling sequence.

\textbf{Local convergence argument [structured derivation].}
\textbf{Assume} a smooth real test section $f$ supported inside a fixed
interval chart, sampled as $v_M(x)=\sqrt{\epsilon_M}f(x)$.
\textbf{Prove} its sharp Fourier embedding converges to $f$ in $L^2(dx)$.
\begin{description}
\item[$\langle1\rangle1$.] Every fixed Fourier coefficient converges.
\textsc{By} the Riemann sum for the coefficient in the orthonormal basis
$e^{irx}/\sqrt{2\pi}$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle2$.] The norms converge to $\|f\|_2$.
\textsc{By} unitarity of the discrete Fourier transform and the Riemann sum
$\epsilon_M\sum_x|f(x)|^2\to\int|f|^2$.
\item[$\langle1\rangle3$.] Weak convergence and convergence of norms
give strong convergence. \textsc{By} density of finite Fourier sums,
$\langle1\rangle1$, boundedness from $\langle1\rangle2$, and expansion
of the squared Hilbert-space difference.
\item[$\langle1\rangle4$.] QED \textsc{by} $\langle1\rangle3$.
\end{description}
CAR norm continuity then gives convergence of every fixed even field
product. Density of supported smooth tests identifies the generating local
even CAR algebra. The NS vacuum representation and the sourced even-net
identity identify its vacuum interval net with $\operatorname{Vir}_{1/2}$.
The universal global circle observable algebra is not identified with a plain
even CAR algebra; the source explicitly distinguishes them.

\subsection{Symmetry and Modular Fidelity}

The source's modified-KS theorem supplies the strong finite-energy limit
and convergence of its unitary implementers in the Majorana representation.
The finite Schwinger computation separately checks the self-dual central
coefficient $(n^3-n)/24$; it is not a central term of a finite matrix
commutator without normal ordering.

The complete-net sector theorem at minimal-model parameter $m=3$ gives
three sectors, with weights $0,1/16,1/2$, Ising fusion and the displayed
twists and $S$. This supplies fidelity of the identified local net rather
than guessing its sectors from finite spectra.

For products, the compiler retains each factor's stress family and their
placed sum. The sum alone is not the full tensor-product net. The sourced
tensor-sector completeness result and factorwise braiding give product
modular data and additive central charge. The output explicitly records
the relation between input labels and canonical product labels.

This construction establishes a supported branch. General category inputs,
other Ising chiralities, arbitrary central-charge lifts, and general tensor
functors remain outside its implemented domain. The broader machine must
expand that domain with additional constructions and proofs.

\subsection{The Finite Hard-Core Carrier}

Fix the site object \(O=\one\oplus X\) from CA-65.  For \(L\) open sites, define
\[
  H_L(\Cc,O)
  :=
  \bigoplus_{c\in\Irr(\Cc)}
  \Hom_{\Cc}(X_c,O^{\otimes L}).
\]
The inner product on each Hom space is the categorical one: if \(s,t\) have
irreducible source \(X_c\), then \(s^\dagger t\) is a scalar multiple of
\(\operatorname{id}_{X_c}\).  The direct sum over charges is orthogonal.

Expanding the maybe-object gives the variable-particle grading.  For one
species \(X\),
\[
  O^{\otimes L}
  =
  (\one\oplus X)^{\otimes L}
  \cong
  \bigoplus_{S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,L\}} X^{\otimes S},
\]
where \(S\) records the occupied sites and the tensor factors are kept in
increasing site order.  Hence
\[
  H_L(\Cc,O)
  \cong
  \bigoplus_{N=0}^{L}\ 
  \bigoplus_{|S|=N}\ 
  \bigoplus_{c\in\Irr(\Cc)}
  \Hom_{\Cc}(X_c,X^{\otimes S}).
\]
This is the sourced variable-\(N\) target from \texttt{literature/SYNTHESIS.md}.
The hard-core condition is built into the site object: each site contributes
either \(\one\) or one non-vacuum summand, never two particles at the same site.

For multiple species, take \(X=\bigoplus_{a\neq\one}X_a\).  The same formula
refines by a species labelling of the occupied set \(S\).

\subsection{Action of the Word Algebra}

The finite algebra
\[
  \Alg_L(\Cc,O)=\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O^{\otimes L})
\]
acts on every charge sector by postcomposition:
\[
  T\cdot w:=T\circ w,\qquad
  T\in\Alg_L,\quad w\in\Hom_{\Cc}(X_c,O^{\otimes L}).
\]
Thus \(H_L\) is the direct sum of the defining modules for the matrix blocks
\[
  \Alg_L\cong\bigoplus_c M_{m_c(L)}(\mathbb C),
  \qquad
  m_c(L)=\dim\Hom(X_c,O^{\otimes L}).
\]
This is the finite carrier on which the kinematic quotient from CA-65 acts.

A charge-diagonal finite state can be written after choosing one minimal
projection \(e_c\) in each nonzero charge block.  Let
\[
  p:=\bigoplus_c e_c .
\]
Using the ordinary finite matrix trace in the chosen block presentation, set
\[
  \omega_{L,p}(T)
  :=
  \frac{\Tr(pTp)}{\Tr(p)}.
\]
The corresponding GNS quotient is intended to recover one cyclic column from
each charge block, hence a space of dimension \(\sum_c m_c(L)=\dim H_L\).
The choice of \(e_c\) is not canonical when \(m_c(L)>1\); coherent choices in
\(L\) are an open point in this construction.

\subsection{Birth and Death Operators}

Let \(a\) be a simple object with conjugate \(\bar a\), and choose the raw cup
\[
  v_a:\one\to a\otimes\bar a .
\]
The raw normalization is
\[
  v_a^\dagger v_a=d_a\,\operatorname{id}_{\one}.
\]
At adjacent sites \(j,j+1\), define the local birth map
\[
  b^a_{j,j+1}
  :=
  (\iota_a\otimes\iota_{\bar a})
  \circ v_a
  \circ
  (\pi_{\one}\otimes\pi_{\one}),
\]
where \(\iota_a:a\to O\) is the site summand inclusion and
\(\pi_{\one}:O\to\one\) is the vacuum summand projection.  Its adjoint
\[
  d^a_{j,j+1}:=(b^a_{j,j+1})^\dagger
\]
is the death map.  These maps change the occupation number by \(N\leftrightarrow
N\pm2\), and annihilate configurations not matching the two-vacuum or
\(a,\bar a\) local pattern.

The default convention is raw cups with explicit \(d_a\) bookkeeping:
\[
  (b^a_{j,j+1})^\dagger b^a_{j,j+1}
  =
  d_a\,P_{\one\one}.
\]
The normalized cup \(d_a^{-1/2}v_a\) would make the birth map a partial
isometry on the two-vacuum corner, but that is not the default convention.

\subsection{Hollands' Dense Chain as a Corner}

Hollands' chain Hilbert space \(V^L\) is built from sequences of intertwiners,
and the corresponding operators are relative commutants.  In the present
tower it is recovered by two compressions.  First take the fixed-\(N=L\) corner
that drops the vacuum summand at every site:
\[
  O=\one\oplus\tau
  \quad\leadsto\quad
  P_\tau O^{\otimes L}\cong \tau^{\otimes L}.
\]
Then take the total-charge-\(\one\) corner:
\[
  e_{\one}P_\tau H_L
  \cong
  \Hom_{\Cc}(\one,\tau^{\otimes L}).
\]
Hollands' Jones projections are local elements in this compressed corner.  The
extra Q-system/subfactor data used in the dense-chain source are not supplied
by a bare UFC input and remain deferred.

\subsection{Fibonacci Test Targets}

For Fibonacci, take
\[
  \Irr(\mathrm{Fib})=\{\one,\tau\},
  \qquad
  \tau\otimes\tau=\one\oplus\tau,
  \qquad
  O=\one\oplus\tau .
\]
With \(F_0=0\), \(F_1=1\), the multiplicities are
\[
  (m_{\one}(L),m_{\tau}(L))=(F_{2L-1},F_{2L})
  \qquad(L\ge1).
\]
Therefore
\[
  \dim H_L=m_{\one}(L)+m_{\tau}(L)=F_{2L+1},
\]
and
\[
  \dim\Alg_L=m_{\one}(L)^2+m_{\tau}(L)^2=F_{4L-1}.
\]
The first arithmetic targets are
\[
  \dim H_2=5,\quad \dim H_3=13,\quad \dim H_4=34,
\]
and
\[
  \dim\Alg_2=13,\quad \dim\Alg_3=89,\quad \dim\Alg_4=610.
\]
For the dense-chain corner \(O=\tau\), the same recurrence reduces to CA-09's
\((a_n,b_n)\) path-count sequence.

For the raw Fibonacci cup
\[
  v:\one\to\tau\otimes\tau,
\]
Penneys gives
\[
  v^\dagger v=\phi\,\operatorname{id}_{\one},
  \qquad
  \phi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
Thus the local cup-cap invariant for the raw birth/death pair is \(\phi\), not
\(1\).  The unitary Fibonacci recoupling matrix is
\[
  F^{\tau\tau\tau}_{\tau}
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
    \phi^{-1} & \phi^{-1/2}\\
    \phi^{-1/2} & -\phi^{-1}
  \end{pmatrix}
  =
  \left(F^{\tau\tau\tau}_{\tau}\right)^\dagger .
\]
For this nontrivial block the matrix is also involutory, but the adopted
convention is still the unitary gauge so that matrix presentations use the
ordinary adjoint.

\subsection{Boundary}

This shard does not prove existence or uniqueness of a dynamical vacuum.  The
finite state \(\omega_{L,p}\) is a useful charge-diagonal target, not a canonical
vacuum.  The categorical Markov trace is the expected natural
``infinite-temperature'' state only after the spherical/pivotal convention is
locally sourced.  The residual choice of \(e_c\) for \(m_c>1\) is open.

The general statement ``unitary UFC implies canonical spherical structure and
positive quantum dimensions'' is still a source gap for this block.  The
Fibonacci self-duality sign \(\kappa_\tau=+1\) is also not locally sourced.
Approximate Virasoro relations becoming exact in the scaling-limit GNS null
space remains a programme conjecture, not a result of this shard.

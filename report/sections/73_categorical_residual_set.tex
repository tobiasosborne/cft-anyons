\subsection{Setting}

Fix \((\Cc,O)\) with \(O=\one\oplus X\) per CONVENTIONS (r), the open-chain
tower \(\Alg_L=\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O^{\otimes L})\), and a self-adjoint
two-site density \(h\in\Alg_2\) placed at bonds by the shift,
\(h_j\in\Alg_L\) for \(1\le j\le L-1\), so that \([h_j,h_k]=0\) for
\(|j-k|\ge2\) (disjoint supports).  The Tier-1 dilute density of CA-70 is
the default instance: a real combination of occupancy projectors, the dense
exchange \(u_j=b_j^tb_j\), hopping \(a_j+a_j^t\), and pair terms
\(b_j+b_j^t\), all parity-even by CA-69.  The qubit block is the
\(\Cc=\mathrm{Hilb}\), \(O=\mathbb C^2\) degenerate case, which is what
makes the port below a specialization-in-reverse rather than an analogy.

\subsection{The Ported Local Residual Calculus}

\textbf{Definition [port].}  For a local density \(u\) supported on \(m\)
sites at position \(j\), the coboundary is \((Du)_j=u_j-u_{j+1}\).  The
local residual densities are, verbatim from CONVENTIONS (n) but now as
elements of \(\Alg_L\):
\[
  p_j=i[h_j,h_{j+1}],\qquad
  A_j=i[h_j+h_{j+1},\,p_j],\qquad
  B_j=i[p_j,h_{j+1}]+2i[p_j,h_{j+2}],
\]
with the conservation and boost residual equations
\[
  R^{\mathrm{cons}}_j(h;u)=A_j-(Du)_j,
  \qquad
  R^{\mathrm{boost}}_j(h;u,w,\lambda,e)
  =B_j-u_j-\lambda\,\bar h_j-(Dw)_j,
  \qquad \bar h=h-e\,\mathbf 1,
\]
where \(\lambda\) is the unconstrained multiplier of CONVENTIONS (s) (not a
squared velocity) and the scalar parts of \(u,w\) are gauge, exactly as in
CA-38.  \textbf{Derived [port]:} nothing in the CA-38/CA-39 derivations
uses the Pauli basis --- only bilinearity, locality, and the shift --- so
every identity holds in \(\Alg_L\); the Pauli \emph{coefficient} equations
of CA-38 are recovered by expanding in the matrix-unit or diagram basis of
the charge blocks \(\bigoplus_cM_{m_c(L)}(\mathbb C)\).

\subsection{Open-Window Mode Calculus}

\textbf{Definition [choice].}  Fix the phase base \(z_n=e^{2\pi in/L}\) on
the length-\(L\) open chain (CONVENTIONS (t): index phases at own scale).
For a window \(W=[j_0,j_1]\subset[1,L-1]\) define
\(H_n^{(W)}=\sum_{j\in W}z_n^jh_j\) and
\(J_n^{(W)}=\sum_{j\in W}z_n^jp_j\).

\textbf{Lemma [derived].}  The exact periodic identity (42.1) ports to the
open window with an edge remainder:
\[
  [H_m^{(W)},H_n^{(W)}]=-i(z_n-z_m)\,J_{m+n}^{(W)}
  +\,\partial_{mn}^{(W)},
\]
where \(\partial_{mn}^{(W)}\) is supported in the two edge cells of \(W\)
(the telescoping of CA-42's derivation fails only where the window ends).
The same holds for the ported (42.2)--(42.3) with their \(D\)- and
\(F\)-density expansions.  The Julia suite asserts the support statement
directly; it is the strong form of ``bulk identity modulo boundary''.
Periodic modes --- the setting of CA-70's \(L_n^{(N)}\) and of the
registered periodic dilute algebra --- are deliberately \emph{not} defined
here (decision D1; CA-70 obligation 3).

\subsection{Witt/Virasoro Residuals with Two Central Constants}

\textbf{Definition [port + CONVENTIONS (s)].}  For a state \(\omega\) let
\({:}X{:}_\omega=X-\omega(X)\mathbf 1\) termwise on densities.  The chiral
ansatz on a window is
\[
  \ell_n=A_n{:}H_n^{(W)}{:}_\omega+B_n{:}J_n^{(W)}{:}_\omega
  +\mu\,\delta_{n0}\mathbf 1,
  \qquad
  \bar\ell_n=A_n{:}H_n^{(W)}{:}_\omega-B_n{:}J_n^{(W)}{:}_\omega
  +\mu\,\delta_{n0}\mathbf 1,
\]
and the closure residuals carry the \emph{two-constant} central term of
CONVENTIONS (s):
\[
  r^{++}_{mn}=[\ell_m,\ell_n]-(m-n)\ell_{m+n}
  -\tfrac{\delta_{m+n,0}}{12}\bigl(m^3c^*_L-m\,c_L\bigr)\mathbf 1,
\]
with \(r^{--}_{mn}\), \(r^{+-}_{mn}=[\ell_m,\bar\ell_n]\) analogous.
Setting \(c^*_L=c_L\) is a measured outcome or theorem target, never an
input.  The estimator pair \((c_L(m),c^*_L(m))\) is defined by solving
\(\omega([\ell_m,\ell_{-m}]-2m\ell_0)=\tfrac1{12}(m^3c^*_L-mc_L)\) at two
mode values, relative to a declared \((\omega,W)\).

\subsection{The Categorical Residual Set}

\textbf{Definition.}  The scale-\(L\) residual family of the pair
\((h,\text{window data})\) is
\[
  \mathcal R_L(h)=
  \bigl\{R^{\mathrm{cons}}_j,\ R^{\mathrm{boost}}_j\bigr\}_{j}
  \cup
  \bigl\{r^{\sigma\sigma'}_{mn}\bigr\}_{|m|,|n|\le K},
\]
as elements of \(\Alg_L\).  This is the object whose absence was seam S3 of
the 2026-07-06 consistency map: the moment/GNS constraints
\(\omega(X^\dagger RY)=0\) of CA-43/CA-63, and the relaxed profiles and
\(\nu\)-objectives of CA-64, are now well-posed on the categorical tower by
substitution.  \textbf{Boundary:} CA-63's existence theorem is proved for
the qubit UHF algebra; its transport to the AF tower is W1.6's categorical
corollary, not a claim of this shard.

\subsection{Exact Transport and the Stretched Dichotomy}

\textbf{Lemma 73.1 [derived] (exact transport).}  Let
\(\theta_\varphi\) be the corner morphism of a placement (CA-71, CA-72).
Since \(\theta_\varphi\) is a \(*\)-morphism (Lemma 72.1), for every
noncommutative \(*\)-polynomial \(F\),
\[
  \theta_\varphi\bigl(F(h_{j_1},\dots,h_{j_r})\bigr)
  =F\bigl(\theta_\varphi(h_{j_1}),\dots,\theta_\varphi(h_{j_r})\bigr).
\]
Hence the \emph{entire} residual calculus transports exactly: the
\(\theta_\varphi\)-image of every element of \(\mathcal R_k(h)\) is the
same residual built from the \emph{stretched densities}
\(\theta_\varphi(h_j)\), which for the dyadic placement are supported on
fine sites \(\{2j-1,2j,2j+1\}\) with the inserted site pinned to vacuum
(the CA-68 stretched-covariance phenomenon at the density level).

\textbf{Observation [derived] (the dichotomy).}  The stretched density is
\emph{not} the fine density: \(\theta_\varphi(h_j)\ne h^{(l)}_{j'}\) for
any placement of the same \(h\) at fine scale.  Consequently the CA-72
descent-compatibility contract \emph{fails} for the naive pairing
\((\mathcal R_k(h),\mathcal R_l(h))\): annihilating the fine residuals does
not annihilate the \(\theta\)-transported coarse ones.  This is the precise
residual-level content of ``bare vacuum insertion is naive''.

\textbf{Definition + Proposition 73.2 [derived] (coherent family).}  The
\emph{stretched-augmented} residual family assigns to the fine scale
\[
  \mathcal R^{\mathrm{coh}}_l
  :=\mathcal R_l(h)\ \cup\
  \bigcup_{\varphi}\theta_\varphi\bigl(\mathcal R_k(h)\bigr)
\]
over the placements \(\varphi\) of the chosen refinement schedule.  If a
state \(\omega_l\) annihilates \(\mathcal R^{\mathrm{coh}}_l\) in the
CA-63 sense (\(\pi_{\omega_l}(R)=0\) for all
\(R\in\mathcal R^{\mathrm{coh}}_l\)), then the normalized pullback
\(\omega_k\) annihilates \(\mathcal R_k(h)\), by Lemma 72.4 applied to
\(\pi_{\omega_l}(\theta_\varphi(R'))=0\).  Descent-compatibility thus holds
\emph{by construction} for the coherent family.  \textbf{Price [recorded]:}
the fine moment problem gains the stretched constraints --- a strictly
stronger feasibility demand on \(\omega_l\).  \textbf{Alternative
[recorded, W2.1]:} a dressing \(U\) may instead be chosen so that
\(\mathrm{Ad}_U\circ\theta_\varphi\) carries stretched residuals
asymptotically into the null structure of \(\mathcal R_l(h)\); quantifying
that is precisely the dressed-refinement task, now stated as an equation
rather than a slogan.

\subsection{Boundary}

Tier-2 (parity-odd, fusion-vertex) densities are not defined here; they are
the S4/W3.1 item.  Periodic modes and the periodic evaluation map remain
gated behind D1 (CA-70 obligation 3; interchiral caveat recorded in the
plan).  No estimation protocol for \((e_\infty,v_L,c_L,c^*_L)\) is fixed
here (W2.2).  Residual annihilation, exact or relaxed, is never symmetry
evidence (CA-44, CA-64).  The transport lemma is exact but says nothing
about convergence; the coherent family makes descent hold by fiat at the
cost of stronger constraints, and whether any physical state family
satisfies them is precisely the open state-existence frontier (W1.6, W2.1).

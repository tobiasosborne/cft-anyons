\subsection{Site Object First}

The input to the word algebra is a pair \((\Cc,O)\), not the category \(\Cc\)
alone.  Here \(\Cc\) is a unitary fusion category and \(O\in\Cc\) is the
one-site object.  The default mobile hard-core choice is
\[
  O=\one\oplus X,
\]
where \(X\) is the allowed non-vacuum species object.  For Fibonacci,
\(X=\tau\); for an all-species hard-core site, \(X=\bigoplus_{a\neq\one}X_a\).

For a finite open interval \(I\) with \(|I|=L\), fix the left-associated tensor
power from CONVENTIONS.md (c),(r):
\[
  O_I := (((O\otimes O)\otimes O)\cdots)\otimes O .
\]
The local anyonic observable algebra is
\[
  \Alg_I(\Cc,O):=\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O_I),
  \qquad f^*:=f^\dagger .
\]
Because endomorphism algebras in a unitary category are finite-dimensional
unitary *-algebras, each \(\Alg_I\) is a finite-dimensional C*-algebra.

Choose representatives \(\{X_c\}_{c\in\Irr(\Cc)}\), with \(\one\) first by
CONVENTIONS.md (a) as fixed in this block, and define
\[
  m_c(L):=\dim_{\mathbb C}\Hom_{\Cc}(X_c,O_I).
\]
After choosing orthonormal bases of these Hom spaces,
\[
  \Alg_I(\Cc,O)\cong \bigoplus_{c\in\Irr(\Cc)} M_{m_c(L)}(\mathbb C).
\]
This matrix form is a presentation.  The abstract algebra
\(\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O_I)\) is basis-free; fusion-tree bases and
\(F\)-symbols enter only when we write matrices.

\subsection{Placed Matrix Blocks}

Let \(w,w'\in\Hom_{\Cc}(X_c,O_I)\) be basis isometries in the same charge block
and let \(f\in\operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(X_c)\).  The corresponding placed matrix
block is
\[
  E_I(w',f,w):=w'\circ f\circ w^\dagger\in\Alg_I(\Cc,O).
\]
For basis vectors \(u,v,w,z\) in compatible charge blocks,
\[
  E_I(u,g,v)\,E_I(w,f,z)
  =
  \delta_{v,w}\,E_I(u,g\circ f,z),
\]
and
\[
  E_I(w',f,w)^*=E_I(w,f^\dagger,w').
\]
For simple \(X_c\), \(f\) is a scalar multiple of \(\operatorname{id}_{X_c}\),
so these are ordinary matrix units inside the \(c\)-block.  This is the
categorical replacement for positioned Pauli words in CA-47: rows and columns
are not bare strings of letters, but charge-resolved fusion-tree basis vectors.

\subsection{Inclusions, Shift, and AF Limit}

For \(I\subset J\), the local inclusion is obtained by tensoring with identities
on the added site factors, using the fixed bracketing convention:
\[
  f\longmapsto
  \operatorname{id}_{O}^{\otimes \ell}\otimes f\otimes
  \operatorname{id}_{O}^{\otimes r}.
\]
On a one-sided chain the shift is
\[
  \alpha(f)=\operatorname{id}_{O}\otimes f .
\]
The quasi-local observable algebra is the inductive-limit C*-algebra
\[
  \Alg(\Cc,O):=\overline{\bigcup_I \Alg_I(\Cc,O)}.
\]
It is AF by construction: it is the norm closure of an increasing union of
finite-dimensional C*-algebras.

The Bratteli diagram is the fusion graph of \(O\).  If
\(O_I\cong\bigoplus_c m_c(L)X_c\), then
\[
  m_d(L+1)=\sum_c m_c(L)\,N_{c,O}^{d},
  \qquad
  N_{c,O}^{d}:=\dim\Hom_{\Cc}(X_d,X_c\otimes O).
\]
Sanity check: for \(\Cc=\Hilb_{\mathrm{fd}}\) and
\(O=\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C^d\), this gives
\[
  \Alg_L\cong M_{(d+1)^L}(\mathbb C),
\]
and the inductive limit is the ordinary UHF spin-chain algebra with a
\((d+1)\)-dimensional maybe-spin site.

\subsection{The BU-Style Free Pre-Layer}

The adopted free layer is the tensor *-algebra
\[
  \mathcal{BU}_0(\Cc,O):=T(V),
  \qquad
  V:=\bigoplus_{j\in\mathbb Z}\ \bigoplus_{a\neq\one}
      \Hom_{\Cc}(X_a,O)_j .
\]
The subscript \(j\) records the lattice site.  The involution is the formal
dual/adjoint operation fixed by CONVENTIONS.md (b),(r); in a fusion-tree matrix
presentation the unitary gauge is mandatory so that \(*\) is entrywise conjugate
transpose.

The intended presentation map is
\[
  \pi:\mathcal{BU}_0(\Cc,O)\twoheadrightarrow \Alg(\Cc,O),
\]
with
\[
  J_{\mathrm{fus}}:=\ker\pi .
\]
Thus
\[
  \mathcal{BU}_0(\Cc,O)/J_{\mathrm{fus}}
  \cong
  \Alg(\Cc,O).
\]
The ideal \(J_{\mathrm{fus}}\) is kinematic.  It contains the hard-core site
relations, fusion admissibility, \(F\)-coherence, and the matrix-block
relations above.  This mirrors the AQFT construction in which one starts from a
free unital *-algebra and quotients by a two-sided *-ideal of relations.

The load-bearing split is therefore
\[
  \mathcal{BU}_0
  \xrightarrow{\ \pi\ \text{(kinematic fusion)}\ }
  \Alg(\Cc,O)
  \xrightarrow{\ \text{state/GNS}\ }
  H_\omega .
\]
Fusion is not deferred to the state.  The GNS null space may impose dynamical or
scaling relations, but category fidelity lives already in \(J_{\mathrm{fus}}\).

\subsection{Defect Algebra Is a Different Layer}

Hollands' double triangle algebra is a finite-dimensional vector space built
from Hom spaces such as \(\Hom_M(cd,\bar a b)\), with its own vertical product.
That algebra is not the local observable algebra \(\Alg_I(\Cc,O)\).  In this
programme it is the deferred defect/MPO/symmetry layer represented on, or
acting by endomorphisms of, the observable tower.  A later shard must specify
that representation and its compatibility with the shift.

\subsection{Functoriality Target}

For a unitary monoidal dagger functor
\(F:\Cc\to\Cc'\) and a chosen unitary \(F(O)\cong O'\), the expected finite-level
map is
\[
  \operatorname{End}_{\Cc}(O^{\otimes L})
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{End}_{\Cc'}((O')^{\otimes L}),
\]
given by applying \(F\) and conjugating by the chosen site-unitary tensor power.
The coherence data make this pseudofunctorial rather than strictly functorial
on the nose.  The free construction \(\mathcal{BU}_0\) is natural at the
generator level, and \(\pi\) should be natural once \(J_{\mathrm{fus}}\) is
implemented.

\subsection{Boundary}

This shard does not construct the dynamical vacuum, prove a scaling limit, or
derive Virasoro generators.  Approximate Virasoro relations becoming exact in a
continuum limit remain a programme conjecture.  The categorical Markov trace
and its naturality under spherical/unitary functors are not used here because
the local source chain for canonical spherical structure is still open.  The
exact generator-level implementation of \(\mathcal{BU}_0\), the endpoint-closing
rule for charged words, and the double-triangle representation on \(\Alg\) are
implementation tasks, not completed results.

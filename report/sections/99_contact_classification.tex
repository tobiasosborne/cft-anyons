\subsection{Exact Family and Classification}

Fix unitary fusion \(\Cc\), pointed \(O=\one\oplus X\), \(X\ne0\), and \(t,\mu>0\). Write
\(E_{c,n}=\Hom(c,X^{\otimes n})\), \(\Delta_n=\{0<x_1<\cdots<x_n<1\}\),
\(\mathcal F=\hoplus_{c,n}L^2(\Delta_n;E_{c,n})\), with isometry inner products, no factorial and \(\Delta_0\) a point.
Use CA-77's isometric box maps \(K_\varepsilon\), \(\varepsilon=1/L\); smooth samples have amplitudes
\(\varepsilon^{n/2}u(x_{j_1},\ldots,x_{j_n})\), \(x_j=(j-\tfrac12)\varepsilon\).
Declare finite fixed lists \(b_\rho:X\to X^{\otimes2}\), \(v_\sigma:\one\to X^{\otimes2}\),
\(\lambda_\rho,\kappa_\sigma\ge0\), and moments
\(a=\sum\lambda_\rho+\sum\kappa_\sigma\), \(\beta=\sum\lambda_\rho b_\rho\), \(\nu=\sum\kappa_\sigma v_\sigma\),
\(Q_b=\sum\lambda_\rho b_\rho^\dagger b_\rho\), \(\gamma=\sum\kappa_\sigma v_\sigma^\dagger v_\sigma\).
With \(\pi_{00},\pi_{X0},\pi_{0X},\pi_{XX}\) the summand coisometries of \(O^{\otimes2}\) (\(0\) denotes \(\one\)), set
\(D_h=\pi_{X0}-\pi_{0X}\), \(D_b=\pi_{XX}-\sqrt\varepsilon b\pi_{X0}\),
\(D_v=\pi_{XX}-\varepsilon v\pi_{00}\), \(P_{XX}=\pi_{XX}^\dagger\pi_{XX}\). The \emph{exact family} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:99-family}
 H_\varepsilon=\sum_{j=1}^{L-1}\!\left[t\varepsilon^{-2}D_h^\dagger D_h+
 \sum_\rho\lambda_\rho\varepsilon^{-1}D_{b_\rho}^\dagger D_{b_\rho}+
 \sum_\sigma\kappa_\sigma\varepsilon^{-1}D_{v_\sigma}^\dagger D_{v_\sigma}
 +(\alpha_\varepsilon-a)\varepsilon^{-1}P_{XX}\right]_j+\mu\hat N .
\end{equation}
Here \(\alpha_\varepsilon=(c_\varepsilon+2t)/\varepsilon\) is the \emph{total} contact; the added excess is \(\alpha_\varepsilon-a\).
Kinetic/contact action is fibre-scalar, with the displayed open endpoint completion and no outer penalty.
Use \(H_\varepsilon^{[2]}=P_{\le2}H_\varepsilon P_{\le2}|_{\Ran P_{\le2}}\) on \(\mathcal F_{\le2}=P_{\le2}\mathcal F\).

\textbf{Theorem 99.1 [derived; any (open); \(\mathsf L0\)+declared family; \(\mathsf R0^c\)+off-ladder \(z=2\)]
(NOTE:746--800; REVIEW:126--129,147--163).}
For \eqref{eq:99-family}, \(K_\varepsilon(H_\varepsilon^{[2]}-i)^{-1}K_\varepsilon^*\) converges strongly to a
self-adjoint resolvent on the entire \(\mathcal F_{\le2}\) iff either \(\alpha_\varepsilon\to\alpha\in\mathbb R\),
giving \(H_\alpha\) below, or \(|\alpha_\varepsilon|\to\infty\), giving \(H_D^W\) below. Arbitrary sign oscillation is allowed.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Sufficiency: 99.4 and 99.6. Necessity: rigidity gives \(X^2\ne0\), since the unit summand of
\(X^*\otimes X\) would otherwise give \(X=0\). Thus finite-contact forms differ by
\((\alpha-\alpha')\|Tu_2\|^2\ne0\) on a suitable smooth vector, and the Dirichlet form has a proper smaller domain.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). A sufficiently shifted resolvent \(R=(H+z)^{-1}\) recovers its form, including its domain, by
\(q(u)+z\|u\|^2=\sup_f\{2\operatorname{Re}\langle u,f\rangle-\langle f,Rf\rangle\}\), with value \(+\infty\) off-domain
(complete the square on the form-dense range of \(R\); NOTE:774--788). The limiting operators are distinct.
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Two finite clusterpoints, or a finite and an absolute-divergent subsequence, contradict uniqueness
of the strong limit. No finite clusterpoint means \(|\alpha_\varepsilon|\to\infty\); otherwise the unique finite clusterpoint
is the limit. QED. The contact-invisible case \(X=0\) is excluded only from necessity.

\textbf{Proposition 99.2 [derived; any (open); \(\mathsf L0\)+declared family; finite, \(\to z=2\)]
(NOTE:66--128; REVIEW:42--45,177--180).}
Compression retains lower squares whose reaction outputs have \(n>2\). Put
\(\mathcal V=\bigoplus_{c,n\le2}H^1(\Delta_n;E_{c,n})\), with \(H^1(\Delta_0)=E_{c,0}\), and
\(S_c(u)(y)=B_{\beta,1,1}u_{c,1}(y)+V_{\nu,0,0}u_{c,0}\), with the insertions of (aw), abbreviated \(B_\beta,V_\nu\).
Then the smooth-sample limit for finite \(\alpha\) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:99-form}
\begin{split}
q_\alpha(u)&=t\sum_{c,n\le2}\|\nabla u_{c,n}\|^2+\sum_{c,n\le2}\langle u_{c,n},W_nu_{c,n}\rangle
 +\alpha\sum_c\|Tu_{c,2}\|^2-2\operatorname{Re}\sum_c\langle Tu_{c,2},S_c(u)\rangle,\\
W_0&=\gamma,\qquad W_1=(\mu+\gamma)I+Q_b,\qquad W_2=(2\mu+\gamma)I+(Q_b)_1+(Q_b)_2 .
\end{split}
\end{equation}
All trace pairings use \(dy=dS/\sqrt2\); \(D=\partial_1-\partial_2\). Endomorphisms act by coherent
postcomposition on Hom fibres, without a tensor-product factorization of the physical fibre.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Expand the squares (94.2, 94.10): upper coefficient \(a\) becomes \(\alpha_\varepsilon\), cross maps
are \(\beta,\nu\), lower diagonals \(Q_b,\varepsilon\gamma\). At fixed \(n\), empty bonds number \(L+O(n)\);
missing \(Q_b\) insertions occupy only adjacent/outer strips. Smooth recovery gives \eqref{eq:99-form}.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Compression of the resolvent is different: for \(H=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&g\\g&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
\(g\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\), and first-coordinate \(P\), \(P(H-z)^{-1}P=-z/(z^2-g^2)\), whereas

\textbf{Lemma 99.3 [derived; any; category-free, fixed finite fibre; \(\to\) off-ladder \(z=2\)]
(NOTE:302--452; REVIEW:66--81).}
For rescaled arrays \(f_{jk}\in E\), \(j<k\), let \(N_\varepsilon=\varepsilon^2\sum\|f_{jk}\|^2\),
\(E_\varepsilon=\sum_{p\sim q}\|f_p-f_q\|^2\) over unordered legal one-step edges, and
\(C_\varepsilon=\varepsilon\sum_{j<L}\|f_{j,j+1}\|^2\). For every \(\eta>0\),
\(C_\varepsilon\le\eta E_\varepsilon+C_0(1+\eta^{-1})N_\varepsilon\), with \(C_0\) mesh/fibre independent.
Bounded \(N_\varepsilon+E_\varepsilon\) gives a subsequence with strongly convergent bulk box steps and full
collision steps \(A_\varepsilon\to Tu\) in \(L^2\), for \(u\in H^1(\Delta_2;E)\). The fibre \(E\) is fixed.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). Reflect to a square array, filling \(g_{jj}=f_{j,j+1}\), \(g_{LL}=f_{L-1,L}\).
Two-edge routes give square mass \(\le4N_\varepsilon\), energy \(\le6E_\varepsilon\). Clamped affine interpolation
\(P_\varepsilon\) has mass \(\le8N_\varepsilon\), gradient energy \(\le6E_\varepsilon\), and square-step error \(\le72\varepsilon^2E_\varepsilon\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). Its diagonal trace differs from the filled diagonal step by squared norm

for coarse meshes use \(C_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon^{-1}N_\varepsilon\). This proves the displayed inequality.
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Cell-average approximations give bulk compactness. Apply the trace bound to differences,
first taking the bulk limit, then shrinking the collar: strong full traces follow. Absolute continuity removes
the last cell; omitted diagonal half-box mass is \(\le\varepsilon C_\varepsilon\). QED, including both collision corners.

\textbf{Theorem 99.6 [derived; any (open); \(\mathsf L0\)+declared family; \(\mathsf R0^c\)+off-ladder \(z=2\)]
(NOTE:571--738; REVIEW:107--120).}
For \(|\alpha_\varepsilon|\to\infty\), the limit is \(H_D^W\), associated with
\(q_D=t\sum\|\nabla u_n\|^2+\sum\langle u_n,W_nu_n\rangle\) on \(\mathcal V_D=\{u\in\mathcal V:Tu_2=0\}\).
Sectors decouple, with all \(W_n\) retained. No common lower bound or restriction on sign/rate is assumed.
\par\(\langle1\rangle1\). An operator core uses arbitrary \(u_0\), finite Neumann cosine sums \(u_1\), and smooth spatially
odd \(u_2\), outer-Neumann and zero near the diagonal corners. Cosine determinants are complete; they vanish
as \(O(\rho^2)\) at these corners. Radial cutoffs cost \(O(d)\) in the Laplacian norm, proving graph-core density.
\par\(\langle1\rangle2\). For a fixed core vector set \(s=(x+y)/2\), \(r=y-x\), \(g(s)=\partial_ru_2(s,0)\),
\(G(x,y)=g(s)\chi(r)\), with \(\chi\) smooth/even, one near zero and supported so \(G\) avoids the outer walls.
Put \(\overline S(x,y)=B_\beta[u_1(x)+u_1(y)]/2+V_\nu u_0\). Sample
\(F_\varepsilon=u_2+(2t/\alpha_\varepsilon-\varepsilon)G+\overline S/\alpha_\varepsilon\), but overwrite each adjacent rescaled
sample by \(a_j=[2tg(j\varepsilon)+S(u)(j\varepsilon)]/\alpha_\varepsilon\). The overwrite error is \(O(\varepsilon^3+\varepsilon^2/|\alpha_\varepsilon|)\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). On interior adjacent rows, \(2a_j-F_{\rm left}-F_{\rm right}=-2\varepsilon g+O(\varepsilon^3+\varepsilon^2/|\alpha_\varepsilon|)\).
Thus kinetic/contact/source action is \([S(u)(s)-S_\varepsilon(s)]/\varepsilon+O(\varepsilon+|\alpha_\varepsilon|^{-1})=O(1)\),
where \(S_\varepsilon(s)=B_\beta u_1(s-\varepsilon/2)+V_\nu u_0\). Exact overwriting cancels every growing contact term.
\par\(\langle1\rangle4\). Degree-one endpoint rows use \(u_2=g=0\) and \(u_1'(0)=u_1'(1)=0\). Other rows have
Neumann difference consistency. Feedback \(-B_\beta^*a_j\), \(-\varepsilon\sum V_\nu^*a_j\) is \(O(|\alpha_\varepsilon|^{-1})\).
Counting strip area gives \(\|K_\varepsilon v_\varepsilon-u\|+\|K_\varepsilon H_\varepsilon^{[2]}v_\varepsilon-H_D^Wu\|
\le C_u(\sqrt\varepsilon+|\alpha_\varepsilon|^{-1})\to0\).
\par\(\langle1\rangle5\). For \(f=(H_D^W-i)u\), the resolvent norm \(\le1\) bounds the convergence error by this graph error.
Such \(f\) are dense by the operator core; the same bound extends convergence to every input. QED.

\textbf{Theorem 99.8 [derived; any (open); \(\mathsf L0\)+nonreacting data; \(\mathsf R0^c\)+off-ladder \(z=2\)]
(NOTE:139--261,852--859; REVIEW:46--57,165--167).}

\par\(\langle1\rangle3\). Fixed-sector convergence and resolvent norm \(\le1\) control Fock tails by density. QED.

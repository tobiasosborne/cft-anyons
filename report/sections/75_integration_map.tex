\textbf{Notes on the rows.}  (1) Lemma~87.1 is a \emph{blocking criterion}:
if a simple \(Y\) has \(Y\otimes\bar Y\cong\one\oplus X\), the dilute tower
is a dense \(Y\)-tower with paired sites, so layers (2)--(6) inherit nothing
new from dilution.  (4) WT-1 needs unital injective \(*\)-maps; CA-72
supplies a non-unital \(*\)-morphism or a unital non-multiplicative map
(Link~(b) below).  VR-1 has no verified instance: the accepted free-fermion
estimate holds on fixed momentum cores, not as a uniform graph-norm bound.
(5) The only sourced convergence theorem is free-fermion (CA-14 boundary);
the fermion benchmark records that periodic momentum wrap invalidates its
central-term recipe.  (6) LC-12 \S5, under an explicit modular-character
hypothesis, forces infinitely many Virasoro-primary families in the vacuum
carrier for the instance treated there; the general-\(c\) form of that
constraint on \(V_\one\) is not written (Question, D5).

\subsection{Unwritten Links}

\textbf{Link (a) [Question]: the history sum as lattice avatar of the
cell equations.}  Fix \((\Cc,O,h)\).  For a brick-wall rectangle \(P\)
with \(\ell\) sites and \(M\) rows, the Trotter product
\(Z^{\mathrm{lat}}_P:=U(\delta\tau)^M\in\operatorname{End}_\Cc(O^{\otimes\ell})\)
is, to first order per plaquette, the CA-86 history sum.  LC-1 asks for
cell maps \(Z_P:D_{\mathrm{in}}\to D_{\mathrm{out}}\otimes L_P\) obeying
C0--C7.  Open:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a1)] \emph{Typing.}  Which module category and channel spaces
\(M^i_{ab}\) of LC-1 \S1 correspond to the open-chain carriers
\(\Hom_\Cc(X_c,O^{\otimes\ell})\) used as lattice boundary data?
\item[(a2)] \emph{Sewing (C2, C3).}  Vertical stacking of rectangles
multiplies Trotter products; spatial gluing is not a tensor product,
because plaquettes straddle the seam.  Is the seam correction a
bounded-support insertion whose limit is LC-1's anomaly-line
identification?
\item[(a3)] \emph{Ward (C4, C7).}  Corollary~86.2 says isotopy fails at
finite cutoff through the geometric weights.  Do derivatives of
\(Z^{\mathrm{lat}}_P\) in the anisotropy parameter produce insertions equal
to the layer-(5) KS densities up to residuals that vanish under layers
(3)--(4)?
\item[(a4)] \emph{Limit.}  Does C0 hold at every cutoff (since
\(\operatorname{Ev}_\Cc\) obeys the \(F\)-moves), and do refined lattice
cells converge to a solution of C1--C6?
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Link (b) [Question]: the CA-72 defect scalar as input to WT-1's A3.}
WT-1 A3 asks for summable \(d_{k,n}\ge
\|\omega_{n+1}\circ\alpha^k_{n+1}-\omega_n\circ\alpha^k_n\|\).  CA-72 gives,
for each placement \(\varphi_n\), the scalar
\(\varepsilon_n:=1-\omega_{n+1}(P_{\varphi_n})\), which fixes the vacuum defect
of the descended isometry (Theorem~72.3) and the modulus of softness of the
unital completion (Corollary~72.8).  Open:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(b1)] \emph{Typing.}  A1 of WT-1 demands coherent unital injective
\(*\)-maps; \(\theta_\varphi\) is not unital and \(\Phi_\chi\) is not
multiplicative.  Does a soft variant of WT-1 hold for coherent-up-to-defect
UCP towers whose multiplicativity defect is the Theorem~72.7 expression?
\item[(b2)] \emph{Split.}  Is \(d_{k,n}\) bounded by an explicit function
of \(\{\varepsilon_j\}_{j\ge n}\) plus the mismatch between \(\omega_n\) and
the normalized pullback of \(\omega_{n+1}\) (Lemma~72.5 Gram domination)?
\item[(b3)] \emph{Attainability.}  Corollary~72.8 needs only
\(\varepsilon_n\to0\); WT-1 needs summability.  Does any dressed family
\(W=UV\) (CA-68) achieve \(\sum_n\varepsilon_n<\infty\) for ground states of
a fixed layer-(2) Hamiltonian?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{One Acceptance Contract}

\textbf{Proposal.}  A \emph{lattice tower} for \((\Cc,\mathsf E)\) is
accepted only with the following data and proofs.  Each item names the
approach supplying a construction [C] and the one supplying the proof
obligation [P]; ``none'' means no current source constructs it.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Declared input.}  \((\Cc,\mathsf E)\), with the fidelity target
(modular data up to unit-preserving relabelling, or braided equivalence, or
a defect target).  [C] CA-65 pair \((\Cc,O)\); [P] CONVENTIONS (u),
\path{realisation_and_fidelity.md}:23--53.
\item \emph{Local algebras.}  Finite-dimensional C*-algebras \(\Alg_n\) with
declared support structure and translation.  [C] CA-65; [P] CA-15 item 1,
LC-4 \S1.
\item \emph{Local dynamics.}  Self-adjoint finite-range density \(h_n\),
cutoff-dependent couplings allowed.  [C] CA-86 tiles, LC-4 \S1 transfer
factors; [P] LC-1 C7, CONVENTIONS (ac).
\item \emph{Refinement.}  Coherent unital injective \(*\)-maps of bounded
support growth with smearing, or a declared soft fallback.  [C] CA-68,
CA-71, CA-72 completion; [P] CA-67 strict list and NG1--NG5, LC-4 \S3.
\item \emph{States.}  Microscopic states \(\omega_n\) with summable row
drift and a computable tail.  [C] CA-66 states, LC-4 \S3 ground-state
tier; [P] WT-1 A2--A3, WT-2.
\item \emph{GNS refinement.}  \(J_k[a]=[\alpha_k(a)]\), pointed or with
quantified vacuum defect.  [C] CA-72 Theorem~72.3; [P] WT-1 step
\(\langle1\rangle5\), LC-4 \S3.
\item \emph{Lattice symmetry.}  Standard KS modes of \(h_n\) with declared
\(e_n,b_n\), velocity and the two central constants.  [C] CA-14, LC-4 \S2;
[P] CA-15 items 2 and 6, CONVENTIONS (s), (af).
\item \emph{Residual descent.}  A residual family compatible with
refinement and controlled on observable words, not only on the vacuum.
[C] CA-73 coherent family; [P] CA-72 contract, VR-2.
\item \emph{Generator convergence.}  Graph-norm certificate on the
\emph{same} GNS system as item 6.  [C] VR-1 (no instance); [P] LC-4 \S4,
CA-68 compatibility target, CA-81.
\item \emph{Covariance.}  Observable covariance of the limiting generators
on local algebras.  [C] none; [P] CA-15 scaling and covariance checks.
\item \emph{Analytic completion.}  Positive energy, essential
self-adjointness, integration to a projective unitary representation,
local-net axioms.  [C] none; [P] LC-4 \S5, VR-1 boundary.
\item \emph{Local conformal consistency.}  The stress of the limit is an
internal field generating coordinate changes, matching item 7.  [C] LC-1,
LC-9 (equations only); [P] LC-1 C4--C7, LC-W.
\item \emph{Fidelity.}  Identification of the limit's sector data with the
declared target, including exclusion of unintended sectors.  [C] LC-9,
LC-10 (equations only); [P] \path{realisation_and_fidelity.md}:46--62,
LC-4 \S5, LC-12 \S5 constraint.
\end{enumerate}
